# Cypher System Artifacts

Artifacts are lasting devices, magic items or strange relics. They are stronger than equipment, can't usually be bought, and keep working until they deplete. Based on the artifact rules and the genre chapters of the Cypher System Reference Document.

## The library
The Artifacts library holds the artifacts from the reference document's genre chapters (fantasy, fairy tale, science fiction, superhero, horror, post-apocalyptic, cyberpunk, Weird West and modern fantasy), filtered by Genre. Each has:
- **Level:** often a roll such as 1d6 + 2. Roll it when the group finds the artifact and note the result on the character's copy.
- **Form:** what it looks like.
- **Effect:** what it does when used or activated. Many effects scale with the artifact's level.
- **Depletion:** how likely each use is to be its last.

Gaining an artifact is worth XP equal to its level, shared by the group (at least 1 each).

## Finding and using
- Identifying a found artifact is an Intellect task, usually at the artifact's level, and takes fifteen minutes to a few hours. An expert can identify it instead.
- Using it unidentified is an Intellect task at its level + 2; failure means it does nothing, or the wrong thing.
- The first use after identifying it is another Intellect task, usually at its level. After that it is used like any gear, normally without spending Pool points.

Roll these as Intellect tasks on the sheet with the artifact's level as the task level.

## Depletion
Each time an artifact is used or activated, click its **Depletion** in **Depletion rolls**. The roll throws the artifact's die (d6, d10, d20 or d100):
- At or under the depletion number ("1 in 1d20", "1-2 in 1d6"), the roll says **Depleted**: the artifact still works this time, but that use is its last. Remove it, or keep it if the text says it still works as an ordinary item.
- A dash never depletes. **Automatic** works once.
- Some depletions multiply by a number, such as the task difficulty or the number of earlier uses; the roll asks for it.
- Notes in brackets say when to roll (each day, each hit, each time something is added), and what depletion means for that item. Follow them rather than rolling for every action.

A depleted artifact can sometimes be repaired or recharged, by the repair rules or a special ability, often for only one more use. Character abilities such as Absorb Energy can force a depletion roll.
