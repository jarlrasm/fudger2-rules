# Playing the Cypher System

## Rolling
The GM gives every task a **level** from 0 to 10. The target number is **level × 3**. Each task roll asks for its **level**, then rolls d20 against the target. Leave it blank when only the GM knows it, and the roll shows just the d20.

- Each step of **easing** lowers the level by one. Skills ease tasks (Trained 1, Specialized 2; an Inability hinders by 1), as do assets (at most 2) and **Effort**.
- **Effort** costs 3 points from the relevant pool for the first level and 2 for each level after, minus the pool's **Edge**. A character's Effort starts at 1 and rises by 1 with each Extra Effort advancement (Max Effort on the sheet). Set Effort before rolling; the roll adds it as eases.
- **Special rolls:** 17 and 18 add 1 or 2 damage in combat and nothing else. 19 gives a minor effect (or +3 damage), and 20 a major effect (or +4 damage) with no pool cost. An impaired character gets none of these. A 1 is a free GM intrusion.
- **Rerolls:** after any roll, a player can spend 1 XP to roll again and keep the better result. The roll offers Spend 1 XP to reroll and takes the point off XP.
- **Attacks:** each row in Attacks is melee (the next square) or ranged, with its Range in feet (10 immediate, 50 short, 100 long).
- The GM never rolls: players roll to attack and to defend.

## GM intrusions
The GM can complicate any situation by offering the player 2 XP (one to keep, one to give away). The player can refuse by paying 1 XP.

## Damage and recovery
Damage comes off **Might** first, then Speed, then Intellect.

- When a pool hits 0 the character moves down the **damage track**: Hale, Impaired (Effort costs more, no minor or major effects), Debilitated (can only move), then Dead.
- **Recovery rolls** (1d6 + tier) restore points to the pools. They come in order: one action, ten minutes, one hour, ten hours.

## Cyphers and XP
Cyphers are one-use powers; a character can only carry as many as their limit. XP comes from discoveries and intrusions, and pays for advancement (4 XP for each step).

## Style
The game is about discovery, not killing. Keep the weird wonderful, reward exploration with XP, and use intrusions to keep things moving.
