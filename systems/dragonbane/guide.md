# Playing Dragonbane

## Rolling
Roll a **d20 at or under** the skill value (or the attribute, for attribute rolls). A natural **1 is a Dragon** (critical success) and a **20 is a Demon** (critical failure).

- A skill's base chance comes from its attribute (3 to 7); trained skills double it. Tick **Advancement** when a roll is a Dragon or Demon, and roll for those skills at the end of the session.
- **Boons** roll two d20s and keep the lower; **banes** keep the higher (Shift and Ctrl). Boons and banes cancel one for one.
- Each **condition** gives a bane on rolls with its attribute (Exhausted STR, Sickly CON, Dazed AGL, Angry INT, Scared WIL, Disheartened CHA); the sheet adds it automatically.
- **Pushing:** after a failed roll, a character can take a condition of their choice and roll again. The roll window offers *Push (take a condition)*.

## Combat
At the start of each round everyone draws an initiative card from 1 to 10 and acts lowest first (add the Initiative Cards deck to the session and use **Deal initiative** in the encounter, or roll the Initiative field). Each turn: one action and movement. Movement is set by kin (human, elf 10; halfling, dwarf, mallard 8; wolfkin 12) and changed by AGL (-4 at 6 or less, -2 at 7-9, +2 at 13-15, +4 at 16 or more); the sheet works it out.

1. Attack with a weapon skill; the defender may **parry** (with a weapon, damaging its durability) or **dodge** (Evade) as their action.
2. Damage is the weapon die plus the STR or AGL damage bonus, minus armor. A Dragon on an attack lets the attacker pick an extra effect.
3. At 0 HP a character is **dying**: each turn they make a death roll on CON. Three successes stabilise them; three failures and they die. A Healing roll gets them back up.
4. **Willpower Points** pay for heroic abilities and magic, and come back with rest. A round rest restores some WP, a stretch rest some HP and conditions.

## Style
Fast, funny and dangerous adventure in a land of ducks, dragons and demons. Encourage bold play and keep the pace up.
