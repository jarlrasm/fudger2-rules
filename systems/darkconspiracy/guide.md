# Playing Dark Conspiracy

## Checks
Roll 2D6, add the skill level and the DM of the characteristic that fits the task (the Check column on a skill uses the characteristic picked in its row). 8 or more succeeds. How far above or below 8 the roll lands is the Effect: 6 or more is a great success, -6 or worse a disaster. Make easy tasks +2 or more and hard ones -2 or worse.

A skill the character doesn't have rolls at -3 (Unskilled under Checks), less with Jack of All Trades. Untick Trained on a skill to mark it as one they lack.

Set Roll under Checks to Boon (roll 3D6, keep the best two) or Bane (keep the worst two) when the situation helps or hinders. Holding Shift or Ctrl while rolling does the same once. Several Boons or Banes don't stack, and one of each cancels.

## Combat
1. Call initiative. It rolls Initiative under Checks, 2D6 plus the better of the DEX and INT DMs. Highest acts first.
2. On their turn a character moves about 6 metres and makes one attack. Attacks lists every weapon they carry, plus Unarmed. Guns, thrown weapons and blades use DEX; clubs and fists use STR. 8 or more hits.
3. Damage is the weapon's dice plus the attack's Effect. Subtract the target's armour Protection.
4. Damage comes off END first (Current under Characteristics). When END is gone, the rest comes off STR or DEX. Condition shows the result: Wounded at 0 END, Unconscious when STR or DEX is also gone, Dead when all three are.

Creatures have Hits instead of characteristics. They attack with Attack DM, or with the rows in their own Attacks list for weapons they carry, and go down at 0 Current Hits.

## Healing
Lost END comes back with a night's rest. STR and DEX need days of rest and a Medic check to help. First aid right after a fight restores a few points, once per injury.

## The Darkness
Facing the Dark Minions raises Dark Influence under Company, and its effect is shown next to it. Trust measures how far the cell's employer believes them. Empathic talents spend EMP. Keep the horror slow, strange and personal, and let most of what lies behind the conspiracy stay unseen.
