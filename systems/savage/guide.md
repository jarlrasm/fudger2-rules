# Playing Savage Worlds

## Rolling
Traits (attributes and skills) are dice from d4 to d12. Roll the trait die and, for **Wild Cards** (player characters and major villains), a **Wild Die** (d6) too, and keep the better. The target is usually **4**; every full 4 over it is a **raise**. Set Target Number and Modifier on the sheet before rolling (for attacks, the target number is the foe's Parry).

- **Aces:** a die that rolls its highest number is rolled again and added (both dice explode).
- Unskilled rolls use a d4 at −2. Wounds (up to −3) and Fatigue come off every roll automatically.
- If both the trait die and the Wild Die show 1 it is a **critical failure**.
- **Bennies:** spend one to reroll a trait roll (the roll window offers *Spend a Benny* on a failure), to soak damage, or to recover from Shaken. Each player starts a session with three.

## Initiative
Deal one **action card** to each Wild Card and to each group of Extras at the start of every round, and act from Ace down to Two (ties: spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs). A **Joker** acts whenever they like and adds +2 to trait and damage rolls; shuffle after that round. The Action Deck comes with the system; rolling the Initiative field draws a random card instead.

## Combat
1. Fighting against Parry, Shooting and Throwing against 4 (modified by range, cover and light).
2. Damage (weapon dice, many add Strength's die; a raise adds +1d6) against **Toughness**: success makes the target **Shaken**, each raise adds a **Wound**. An Extra with any wound is out of the fight.
3. A Shaken character can only move until they recover (a Spirit roll at the start of their turn, or a Benny).
4. Wild Cards with more than three wounds are **Incapacitated** and roll Vigor to see whether they are bleeding out.

## Style
Fast, furious, fun. Keep scenes moving, describe big action, and hand out Bennies for good roleplaying.
