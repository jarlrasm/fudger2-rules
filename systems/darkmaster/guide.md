# Against the Darkmaster: play guide

Epic fantasy in the old percentile style. The heroes are bold folk of many kins standing against a single great evil, the Darkmaster, whose armies, spies and monsters spread over the land. Play it as a heroic saga with dangerous, bloody fights: wounds are real, and courage (Drive) is what lets heroes beat the odds.

## Rolls

- Every roll is an **open-ended d100** plus a bonus. A natural 96 to 100 rolls again and adds (and again on another 96+). A natural 01 to 05 rolls again and **subtracts**; the roll window offers that second roll as a button.
- Skill rolls are read on one scale: **4 or less** critical failure, **5 to 74** failure, **75 to 99** partial success (it works, with a cost or only partly), **100 to 174** success, **175 or more** outstanding success.
- The roll asks first for a **modifier** (help from others is about +10 each, good tools, extra time, the hero's own Drive at +10) and then a **difficulty**: Routine +30, Easy +20, Light +10, Standard +0, Challenging -10, Hard -20, Very Hard -30, Extremely Hard -40, Heroic -50, Sheer Folly -60, Insane -70.
- A skill bonus is 5 per rank plus the stat bonus, the vocation bonus and special bonuses (kin, culture, items). The sheet adds them; the Activity Penalty is taken off every roll for you.
- **Saves** (Toughness against poison, disease and bodily harm; Willpower against fear, illusion and mind control) are open-ended rolls against **50 plus five times the attack level** of the effect. The result says by how much the save passed or failed; many effects last longer the worse the failure.

## Drive and Passions

- Each hero has three Passions: Nature, Allegiance and Motivation. When a hero follows one into danger or at a real cost, give a **Drive point** (at most 5).
- Spend 1 Drive for +10 on a roll, to **reroll a failed roll at +10** (the roll window offers this and lowers Drive), or to lessen a critical strike the hero takes. Spending all 5 at once can set a roll to 100 or add +20 to a critical the hero deals.

## Combat

- Each round runs in phases: readied spells (Spell A), readied missiles (Ranged A), melee from the longest weapons to the shortest, other missiles (Ranged B), then other spells (Spell B). Each combatant picks a **Phase** on the sheet; Initiative sorts by phase, with Swiftness breaking ties. Actions are full, half or free: one full and one free, or two halves.
- An **attack** is open-ended d100 plus the combat bonus (CB), less the target's **Defence**. The result is read on the weapon's **attack table** (Edged, Blunt, Missile, Grappling, Beast, or a spell's Bolt or Area table) under the target's armour type (None, Light, Medium, Heavy), and is capped at the weapon's Max. The table gives hits (Health lost) and often a critical of some severity: Superficial, Light, Moderate, Grievous or Lethal.
- A total of 4 or less is a **fumble**: something goes wrong with the weapon or the attack.
- **Parry**: a fighter may move some CB into Defence for the round; put it in the Parry field and the attacks drop by the same amount.
- **Critical strikes** roll d100 (not open-ended) plus the severity's bonus on the critical table for the weapon (cut, impact, pierce, grapple, beast, fire, frost, lightning, dark magic). Criticals bring extra hits, Bleed per round, Stun rounds, Prone, Held, activity penalties, crippled limbs or death.
- If you have no tables at hand, improvise (this is our rule of thumb, not the book's): a result under about 70 misses, 70 to 99 deals a few hits, and from 100 up it deals more hits and a critical whose severity grows every 25 points or so; heavier armour turns more blows into plain hits.

## Health and recovery

- Health is the kin's base plus the Body skill, up to the kin's cap. At 0 or less the hero is down and dying unless helped; a critical can kill outright.
- **Bleed** costs that much Health each round until bound (Healer roll or the Bind wounds button). **Stun** rounds allow no actions. The **Activity Penalty** sums the lasting penalties from wounds.
- A night of rest restores Magic Points and lifts weariness; Health comes back slowly by natural healing, faster with healing herbs and spells.

## Magic

- Spells come from **spell lores**. Each rank in a lore opens its next weave (spell), up to ten. Casting costs Magic Points and takes an open-ended roll on the lore's bonus; armour, haste and pushing a spell beyond its weave make it harder.
- Rolling **doubles** (11, 22, ... 99, 00) on a casting calls for a **Magical Resonance** roll: magic gone strange, or the Darkmaster's attention drawn to the caster.
- Dark lores and tainted things give **Taint**. A hero whose Taint grows falls under the Shadow: their Passions twist and they become the GM's.

## Creatures and the Darkmaster

- Creature stat blocks give Level, movement, armour type, Defence, Toughness and Willpower saves, hits, attacks (bonus, size and table), how they take criticals, and Roguery, Adventuring and Lore bonuses for any other roll.
- Make the **Darkmaster** first, on its own sheet: epithet, nature, domain, artifact of power, goal and weakness. Track its **Attention**: strong magic and bold deeds draw its eye and its servants.

## Advancement

At the end of a session ask the table whether they explored new places, fought the Darkmaster's servants, followed their Passions, and so on; each yes is a point on the **Heroic Path**, and every ten is a level: more development points for ranks, more Magic Points, and higher saves.
