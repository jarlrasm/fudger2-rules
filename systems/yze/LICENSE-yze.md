# Year Zero Engine SRD

`YZE-Standard-Reference-Document.pdf` is the Year Zero Engine Standard Reference Document (version 1.0) by Fria Ligan AB, shipped unchanged, and `Year-Zero-Engine-License-Agreement.pdf` is the Year Zero Engine Free Tabletop License (version 1.0, March 28, 2023) it is used under. Both come from https://freeleaguepublishing.com/community-content/free-tabletop-licenses/. `yze.json` is a library that holds only these two documents, for the Alien RPG, Forbidden Lands and Vaesen sheets.
