# Playing Mutants & Masterminds

## Checks
Roll **d20 + bonus** against a **Difficulty Class (DC)**: 10 is easy, 15 average, 20 tough, 25 challenging, 30 formidable. The sheet asks for the DC.

- Every 5 over the DC is another **degree of success**; every 5 under another **degree of failure**. A natural 20 adds a degree.
- **Hero Points** let a hero reroll, recover, or edit the scene. Gain them from complications.

## Combat
Roll **d20 + Initiative** at the start of a conflict; act from highest to lowest.

1. **Attack:** d20 + attack bonus against **10 + the target's Dodge** (ranged) or **Parry** (close). The sheet asks for it. A natural 20 is a critical hit: +5 to the effect, or an extra effect.
2. **Damage:** the target makes a **Toughness** check against **15 + damage rank**. The sheet asks for the rank.
   - Success: no effect.
   - 1 degree of failure: **bruised** (−1 to further Toughness checks; it stacks).
   - 2 degrees: bruised and **dazed** (only a standard action next turn).
   - 3 degrees: bruised and **staggered**.
   - 4 degrees: **incapacitated**.
3. **Afflictions** and other effects use Fortitude or Will in the same way, with their own conditions.
4. Minions are taken out by any failed resistance check.

## Power points
Heroes get **15 power points per power level**. Abilities cost 2 per rank, defences 1 per rank, skills 1 per 2 ranks, advantages 1 per rank, and powers by effect. The sheet adds up the points spent. Power level caps attack plus effect, Dodge or Parry plus Toughness, and Fortitude plus Will at twice the PL.

## Powers
Each power is an **effect** with a rank, extras and flaws. The library holds the effects and advantages for reference; write each hero's powers in the Powers list with their cost.

## Style
Four colours and big stakes. Use complications to make heroes' lives hard, and pay them for it in Hero Points.
