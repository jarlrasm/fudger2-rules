# Playing Kids on Bikes (2nd edition)

## The game
A small town in an ordinary decade, a group of kids (and maybe a teen or a grown-up), and something strange: a monster in the woods, a secret lab, a classmate with powers. The town is built together at the table. Play is about friendship, fear and riding bikes into trouble the adults won't believe.

## Stats and dice
Six stats: **Brains**, **Brawn**, **Fight**, **Flight**, **Charm**, **Grit**. Each gets one die: d20, d12, d10, d8, d6 and d4, best to worst.
- **Snap decisions**: roll the stat's die against the GM's **difficulty**. The highest face **explodes**: roll again and add.
- **Planned actions**: with time and no pressure, take **half the die's highest face** instead of rolling (the sheet shows it), or roll anyway.
- **Adversity Tokens**: each failed roll earns one. Spend one for **+1** on a roll (also another player's, by describing how you help), or to power some strengths. The roll asks for the bonus and the difficulty.
- **Age** matters: kids are fearless and overlooked, teens are capable and reckless, adults have resources and responsibilities. Strengths such as Prepared, Quick Healing or Lucky bend the rules.

## Fights and harm
Fights are contested rolls, usually Fight against Fight or Flight. There are no hit points: the GM says how a character is hurt or scared, what it makes harder, and when they are out of the scene. Strengths and the story decide how they recover.

## Powered characters
A character with powers is often played by the whole table, sharing psychic energy tokens; note the power and its cost on the sheet.
