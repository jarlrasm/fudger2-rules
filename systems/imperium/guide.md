# Imperium Maledictum: play guide

The players are agents of a Patron, an Imperial servant with plans of their own, working in the Macharian Sector. Play is investigation, intrigue and sudden, lethal violence. Each character has an Origin, a Faction and a Role (Interlocutor, Mystic, Penumbra, Savant, Warrior or Zealot). The Patron has its own sheet.

## Tests

- A test rolls d100 (Tens and Units dice) at or under the skill or characteristic, after the difficulty: Very Easy +60, Easy +40, Routine +20, Challenging +0, Difficult -10, Hard -20, Very Hard -30.
- Success Levels (SL) are the target's tens digit minus the roll's tens digit. 0 SL is a Marginal result, 1 or 2 a plain one, 3 or 4 Impressive and 5 or more Astounding.
- 01 to 05 always succeeds and 96 to 00 always fails.
- Each roll asks an SL bonus first (Superiority, Fate's +1 SL, talents), then the difficulty.
- Advantage (Adv on the sheet, or hold Shift) swaps the dice's digits when that gives a lower roll. Disadvantage (Dis, or Ctrl) swaps them when that gives a higher one. They cancel out.
- When a test fails, the roll window offers a Fate reroll and takes the Fate point off.
- Opposed tests compare SL, and the higher SL wins. On a tie, the higher skill wins.

## Combat

- Initiative is the Agility Bonus plus the Perception Bonus, with no dice. Highest goes first, and ties go to the higher Reflexes.
- On their turn a character gets a Move and an Action.
- An attack is a Melee or Ranged test with the weapon's specialisation. The defender opposes it with Melee or Reflexes (Dodge).
- The ones digit of the attack gives the hit location: 1 head, 2 left arm, 3 right arm, 4 left leg, 5 right leg, 6 to 0 body.
- Damage is the weapon's damage plus the SL the attacker won by, less the armour at that location. The result shows damage plus the attack's own SL, so take off the defender's SL when the attack was opposed.
- Doubles on a successful attack or power test are a Critical. On an attack, roll 1d10 on the critical table for the location (head, arm, body or leg). Doubles on a failure, and 00, are a Fumble.
- Superiority is the group's momentum. Being prepared and dropping foes raise it. Critical Wounds, fallen allies and surprises lower it. A character adds it as an SL bonus to one test a turn. When it reaches the foes' Resolve, they flee, surrender or make a last charge.

## Wounds and healing

- Wounds count up from 0 to the maximum: Strength Bonus, twice Toughness Bonus and Willpower Bonus.
- A critical hit, or damage past the maximum, gives a Critical Wound. More Critical Wounds than the Toughness Bonus kills.
- NPCs: Troops fall at their first Critical Wound and Elites at their second. Leaders last as long as a character.
- The Night of rest button heals Wounds equal to the Toughness Bonus. Critical Wounds need medicae and time.
- Fate returns to its maximum each session (New session button). Burning Fate cheats death.

## Corruption and the warp

- Exposure to Chaos is resisted with Fortitude or Discipline. Each failure adds Corruption: 1 for minor exposure, 2 for moderate and 4 for major. Above Toughness Bonus plus Willpower Bonus the character succumbs to a mutation or malignancy.
- A psyker's power test (Psychic Mastery, with the discipline as a specialisation) adds the Warp Rating to the Warp Charge on success. On failure it adds the SL, up to the rating. A fumble doubles it, and a critical lowers it by the Willpower Bonus.
- Above the Willpower Bonus, every power is overt and perils threaten.

## Influence and patrons

- Influence runs from -5 (Hunted) to +5 (Honoured) with each faction. It is used to call in favours, find gear and open doors. A character can borrow the Patron's influence for its business.
- The Patron pays each character per assignment by its payment grade.

## Sheet fields that matter

- Combat: Wounds, Critical Wounds, Fate, Superiority, Initiative and the Adv/Dis selector.
- Armour by location, worked out from the Protection worn.
- Attacks (one per weapon, with damage) and Power uses (with Warp Rating).
- Conditions: Minor is 1, Major is 2.
