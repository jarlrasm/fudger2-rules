# Playing Forbidden Lands

## Rolling dice
Roll **base dice** (yellow) for the attribute's current value, **skill dice** (red) for the skill level and **gear dice** (black) for the item's bonus. Every **6** is a success; one is enough, and extra successes buy stunts or extra damage.

- **Pushing:** a player may push once: reroll every die that isn't a 6 or a skull (a 1 on base or gear dice). The roll window offers the push. After pushing, each base die skull is **1 damage to that attribute** and 1 Willpower, and each gear die skull lowers the gear bonus by 1.
- Willpower fuels talents and spells.

## Attributes and damage
Strength, Agility, Wits and Empathy take damage directly: physical attacks hit Strength, fear hits Wits, doubt hits Empathy. At 0 in any attribute the character is **broken**; broken by Strength they may suffer a critical injury. Rest heals attributes.

## Combat
Initiative is drawn: add the Initiative Cards deck to the session and use **Deal initiative** in the encounter (lowest acts first). Each round a character has a slow action and a fast action.

1. Melee (Strength + Melee) or Marksmanship (Agility + Marksmanship), with the weapon's bonus as gear dice.
2. Each extra success adds 1 to the weapon's damage.
3. The defender may parry or dodge, and armour rolls its rating as gear dice: each 6 stops 1 damage.

Monsters do not roll for attacks the same way: roll 1d6 on the monster's attack table (the Monster Attack field), then roll the attack's dice.

## Journeys
Travel hex by hex in quarter days. Each day one player leads the way, one keeps watch, and others forage, hunt, fish or make camp. Food and water are resource dice (d6 to d12): roll when used, and on a 1 or 2 the die steps down. Hungry, thirsty, sleepy or cold characters can't heal.

## Style
The land is wild and the players choose where to go. Reward exploring, building a stronghold, and surviving.
