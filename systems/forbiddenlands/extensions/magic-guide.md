# Year Zero Engine magic

Spellcasting from chapter 5 of the Year Zero Engine SRD (pages 30 to 40), for Forbidden Lands sheets.

## Disciplines
Magic comes in seven disciplines: Awareness, Healing and Shapeshifting for druids, and Blood Magic, Death Magic, Elementalism and Symbolism for sorcerers. Each is a talent with a rank from 1 to 3, set under **Disciplines** on the sheet. Raising a rank or learning a discipline needs a master of higher rank; without a teacher the XP cost is tripled. **General** spells can be cast with any discipline, so they use the caster's best rank.

Add spells from the library to **Spells**. Each has a rank, a casting type (slow action, power word as a fast action, or a ritual that takes about a shift), a range in the SRD's categories (Engaged, Short is the same zone, Medium the next zone, Long about four zones, Extreme about a kilometre) and a duration.

## Which spells can be cast
- A spell up to your rank in its discipline: normal casting.
- One rank above: **chance casting**, which always brings a magic mishap.
- Two or more ranks above: not possible.
- With **Grimoire** ticked (casting from a book or scroll), every spell counts one rank lower. Readying it takes a fast action in combat.

## Casting
Click Cast in **Spell casting**. The roll asks:
- **Willpower to spend**, at least 1. This is the base power level.
- **Dice left out for safe casting**: when the spell's rank is below your talent rank, you may roll one die fewer per rank of difference. With no dice left, the spell just works.

Then you roll one base die per Willpower spent (fewer when casting safely). A spell never fails:
- Each 6 overcharges the spell, adding 1 to the power level.
- Any 1 means a **magic mishap**.
- The roll cannot be pushed.

The result button spends the Willpower on the sheet, and after a mishap it also rolls on the mishap table (d12; the SRD's text says D66, but its table has twelve rows). **Magic mishap** under Magic rolls the table on its own. Willpower comes from pushed rolls in the base game; Transfer, Blood Channeling, Steal Life and Power Rune can give more, so the cast warns rather than refuses when you spend more than the sheet shows.

Each spell's effect works from the final power level, usually damage, healing or stress equal to it. Many say what power a given result needs, and victims often resist with Insight minus the power level.

## NPCs
Monsters and NPCs have no Willpower. Give them a **Magic Rank** and spells; their cast asks for a base power level, up to that rank, and rolls for overcharge and mishaps the same way.

## Special spells
- **Dispel Magic** cuts in as a reaction and lowers another spell's power level by its own; both casters roll.
- **Obscure Magic** and the long range of **Hand of Doom** cost an extra Willpower that adds no power: cast with the Willpower for the power you want, and take the extra point off the sheet by hand.
- **Bind Magic** stores a spell in an object for extra Willpower that adds dice but not power.
- **Symbolism**: a drawn or carved symbol adds 1 to the power level, but drawing takes minutes and carving a shift.
