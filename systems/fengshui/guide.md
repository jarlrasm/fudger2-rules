# Feng Shui 2: play guide

Feng Shui 2 is Hong Kong action cinema as a roleplaying game. The heroes fight in the secret Chi War over feng shui sites, places of chi power that bend history toward whoever is attuned to them. The war is fought across junctures, slices of time linked by the Netherworld: Ancient China, the Past of the 1850s, the Contemporary world and a Future after the apocalypse. Play it big: describe stunts, let the heroes look cool, and keep fights moving.

## Checks

Every check is an Action Value (AV) plus the **Swerve**. Roll two d6 of different colours: the positive die minus the negative die. A six rolls again and adds to that die, so either side can run away. The total is the **Action Result**.

- Take the Difficulty (or the target's Defense) from the Action Result: that is the **Outcome**. An Outcome of 0 or more succeeds; the bigger, the better.
- Difficulties: 5 a little tricky, 7 tricky, 10 tough, 15 real tough, 20 forget it, 25 impossible.
- A skill the hero doesn't have is checked at 7.
- **Boxcars**: both dice show 6. Both roll again (the sheet does this as the sixes explode), and something remarkable happens whether the check succeeds or not. A boxcars failure, or any negative Action Result, is a **way-awful failure**: describe a disaster.
- Every roll on the sheet asks for the Difficulty or Defense. Leave it empty when only the GM knows it; the roll then shows the Action Result.

## Fortune, Chi, Magic and Genome

Each hero has Fortune points, or Chi, Magic or Genome points if their schticks run on them. Spend one after a roll for a **Fortune die**: +1d6 that doesn't explode, at most one per check. The roll window's "Fortune die" button rolls it and takes the point off the sheet. A Fortune die can also be added to Defense against one attack. The Fortune Check rolls the points left, for pure luck. All points come back at the start of each session.

## The shot counter

Combat runs in **sequences** counted in **shots**. At the start of each sequence everyone rolls Initiative: Speed plus a d6 (no exploding), less any initiative penalty, gives the shot they first act on. The GM counts down from the highest shot. On a tie, heroes go before gamemaster characters (the tracker sorts them so).

- An attack, a stunt or most other actions cost **3 shots**: the hero's next action comes 3 shots later. Use Standard Action on the sheet.
- A **dodge** is a 1-shot interrupt: +3 Defense against one attack (Dodge on the sheet). Clear Dodging after the attack.
- Cheesing it (running away) costs 5 shots. Overrunning the end of a sequence becomes a penalty on the next Initiative.
- When nobody has shots left, start a new sequence and roll Initiative again.

## Attacks, damage and Impairment

An attack is the attack AV plus the Swerve against the target's Defense. If it hits, Outcome plus the weapon's Damage Value is the **Smackdown**; the target takes its Toughness off and marks the rest as **Wound Points**. The sheet's Attacks list does the sum when the roll is given the Defense.

- Heroes and featured foes take **Impairment** -1 at 25 Wound Points and -2 at 30: off attacks, Defense and skill checks. Some archetypes have higher thresholds; the sheet lets you change them.
- From 35 Wound Points a hero takes a **Mark of Death** and makes an **Up Check** (Toughness against 5) every time they are hit. Fail and they drop. After the fight each hero with Marks makes a **Death Check**, Toughness against 4 plus their Marks, with no Fortune dice.
- **Mooks** have no Wound Points: any hit drops one. A mook group's sheet counts how many are left.
- **Featured foes** are out at 35 Wound Points. **Bosses and uber-bosses** are Impaired at 40 and 45 and from 50 have an even chance to keel over with each hit.
- Multiple targets: -1 to the attack per target, against the highest Defense.
- Ranges: short 20 m, medium 40 m (+2 Difficulty), long 80 m (+4), extreme 160 m (+8).

## Healing

- **Partial recovery** between fights: Wound Points down to 10 and half the spent Fortune back, at -1 Toughness until fully healed.
- **Full recovery** at the start of each session: all Wound Points healed, all Fortune back.
- A Medicine check (5 shots) or a healing schtick takes its Action Result off the Wound Points; only one healing between fights counts.

## Chases

Vehicles have Acceleration, Handling, Squeal, Frame and Crunch. The driver rolls Initiative with Acceleration and starts with a Driving check. Closing or widening the gap deals Chase Points equal to Outcome plus Squeal less the other's Handling; ramming or sideswiping deals Outcome plus Crunch less the other's Frame. Vehicles are Impaired at 25 and 30 Chase Points and crash or are boxed in at 35. The vehicle sheet's rolls ask both numbers.

## Feng shui sites

A feng shui site entity records which faction holds it, who is attuned and what it gives. Attuning takes a day at the site by a member of the faction that holds it; the heroes' first attunement earns an advancement. Factions can lose a site to the heroes, who may burn it so nobody can use it.

## Running foes

A baseline fight is one featured foe and three mooks per hero. Mooks: Attack 8, Defense 13, Speed 5. Featured foes: Attack 12 to 14, Defense 12 to 13, Toughness 5 to 6, Speed 6 to 8. A boss is a featured foe with +3 attack, +2 Defense, +2 Toughness and +1 Speed; an uber-boss adds +2 attack and +2 Defense to that.

## Sheet fields that matter

- Action Values: Main and Backup Attack, Defense, Toughness, Speed.
- Combat: Wound Points, Impairment, Defense Now, Shot, Initiative, Marks of Death, Up and Death Checks.
- Fortune: type, Max and Current.
- Schticks and Weapons are library items; Attacks builds a roll for each weapon.
