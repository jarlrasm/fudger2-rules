# Playing Dark Heresy (1st edition)

## The game
The Warhammer 40,000 far future, in the Calixis Sector. The players are **acolytes** of an Inquisitor: lowly agents sent to root out heresy, mutants, cults, rogue psykers and alien taint. Each has a **home world** (Feral World, Hive World, Imperial World, Void Born, and in later books Forge World, Noble Born and others) and a **career** (Adept, Arbitrator, Assassin, Cleric, Guardsman, Imperial Psyker, Scum, Tech-Priest). Play is investigation first: interviews, searches, tailing suspects and reading forbidden texts, with short, deadly fights. Acolytes are fragile; wounds, insanity and corruption pile up.

## Tests
Roll **d100** and get the **target** or less. The target is a characteristic (or a skill) plus the difficulty:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Easy | +30 |
| Routine | +20 |
| Ordinary | +10 |
| Challenging | +0 |
| Difficult | -10 |
| Hard | -20 |
| Very Hard | -30 |

Every full 10 the roll is under the target is a **degree of success**; every full 10 over is a **degree of failure**. A roll of 100 always fails. **Basic** skills untrained test at half the characteristic; **advanced** skills can't be used untrained (the sheet shows 0). Trained uses the full characteristic, and +10 and +20 mastery add to it. Each level of **fatigue** gives -10; more fatigue than the Toughness bonus and the acolyte passes out.

A characteristic's **bonus** is its tens digit (times an Unnatural multiplier, which acolytes rarely have). Strength bonus adds to melee damage, Toughness bonus soaks damage, Agility bonus gives initiative and movement, Willpower bonus adds to psychic power rolls.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: 1d10 + Agility bonus, highest first. A turn is a full action or two half actions; a half move is the Agility bonus in metres, a full move double, a charge triple and a run six times.
- **Attacks**: Weapon Skill in melee, Ballistic Skill at range. The attack asks the kind of attack and the difficulty. Put aim (+10 half, +20 full), range (point blank +30, short +10, long -10, extreme -30), cover, darkness and size in the difficulty.
  - Melee: standard +0, charge +10, all-out attack +20, called shot -20; Swift Attack (+0, two attacks) and Lightning Attack (-10, three attacks) come from talents, and each attack is its own test.
  - Ranged: standard +0, semi-auto burst +10 (an extra hit per two degrees of success, up to the semi-auto rate), full auto burst +20 (an extra hit per degree, up to the full auto rate), called shot -20, suppressing fire -20 (full auto; everyone in the arc tests Willpower or is Pinned). A weapon without that rate of fire can't do it.
  - Ranged weapons **jam** on 96 or more, 94 for bursts. Reliable weapons jam only on 100, Unreliable from 91, Best craftsmanship never. Good and Best melee weapons add +5 and +10 to hit, Poor -10.
- **Hit location**: the attack roll's digits reversed: 01-10 head, 11-20 right arm, 21-30 left arm, 31-70 body, 71-85 right leg, 86-00 left leg.
- **Damage**: the weapon's damage (plus Strength bonus in melee and for thrown weapons). The damage roll asks the target's armour on that location (less the weapon's Penetration) and Toughness bonus, and takes them off. Armour counts double against Primitive weapons. Tearing weapons roll an extra die and drop the lowest.
- **Righteous Fury**: a natural 10 on a damage die. Test the attack again; if it hits, add 1d10 (the roll window's button), and again on another 10.
- **Defence**: a target may Dodge (Agility) or Parry (Weapon Skill) once a turn to cancel a hit.
- Damage past 0 wounds is **critical damage**: read the critical effects by hit location and damage type (Energy, Impact, Rending, Explosive). Write lasting effects in *Critical effects*.

## Fate
**Fate points** come back each session (*New session*). Spend one to reroll a test (a failed test offers *Spend Fate: reroll*), add a degree of success, count the initiative roll as 10, heal 1d5 damage, recover from stunning or remove fatigue. Burning one permanently saves the acolyte from death.

## Fear, insanity and corruption
- **Fear**: creatures and horrors have a fear rating of 1 to 4. The *Fear test* is Willpower at +0, -10, -20 or -30; a failure rolls on the Shock table (+10 per degree of failure), and a failure by 30 or more out of combat also gives 1d5 Insanity points.
- **Insanity points** come from horror, shock and the warp. Every 10 brings a Trauma test; the degree of madness (Stable, Unsettled from 10, Disturbed from 40, Unhinged from 60, Deranged from 80) brings disorders. At 100 the acolyte is lost.
- **Corruption points** come from the warp, daemons, forbidden lore and Perils of the Warp. Every 10 brings a Malignancy test (Willpower) and the risk of mutation. At 100 the acolyte is damned and leaves play.

## Psychic powers
An Imperial Psyker has a **Psy Rating**. To use a power, roll up to Psy Rating d10s and add the Willpower bonus; the total must reach the power's **threshold**. Fewer dice are safer: **any 9** on the dice brings **Psychic Phenomena** (the roll window offers the d100 roll), and 75 or more on that roll is **Perils of the Warp** (offered next). *Power roll* asks the dice and the threshold; *Power uses* has a roll for each power on the sheet with its own threshold. Psyniscience senses the warp; Invocation helps the psyker focus.

## Healing
*Night of rest* clears fatigue and heals 1 wound for a lightly damaged acolyte (damage no more than twice the Toughness bonus). Heavily damaged acolytes heal slowly and need Medicae; critical damage must be treated first.

## Experience
A new acolyte has 400 XP to spend. Each career has eight **ranks**, reached by total experience (Rank 2 from 500 XP, 3 from 1,000, 4 from 2,000, 5 from 3,000, 6 from 5,000, 7 from 7,000, 8 from 10,000); each rank opens new advances. Characteristic advances go Simple, Intermediate, Trained and Expert, +5 each.

## Acolyte Cells
Make the group an **Acolyte Cell**: its Inquisitor and Ordo, the contact who briefs it, its base, the mission, objectives, leads and suspects, and a mission log.
