# Playing Deathwatch

## The game
The Warhammer 40,000 far future. The players are **Space Marines** seconded from their Chapters (Ultramarines, Space Wolves, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Black Templars, Storm Wardens and others) to the **Deathwatch**, the Imperium's alien-hunting order. Each Battle-Brother has a **Speciality**: Apothecary, Assault Marine, Devastator Marine, Librarian, Tactical Marine or Techmarine. Together they form a **kill-team** sent on missions by the Watch Captain: purge a xenos nest, kill a warlord, recover an artefact, hold a position. Play is mostly war, against foes that can match a Space Marine and swarms that bury lesser men.

## Tests
Roll **d100** and get the **target** or less. The target is a characteristic (or a skill) plus the difficulty:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Trivial | +60 |
| Easy | +30 |
| Routine | +20 |
| Ordinary | +10 |
| Challenging | +0 |
| Difficult | -10 |
| Hard | -20 |
| Very Hard | -30 |
| Hellish | -60 |

Every full 10 the roll is under the target is a **degree of success**; every full 10 over is a **degree of failure**. A roll of 100 always fails. **Basic** skills untrained test at half the characteristic, **advanced** skills can't be used untrained (the sheet shows 0), Trained uses the full characteristic, and mastery adds +10 or +20. Each level of **fatigue** gives -10 to tests; more fatigue than the Toughness bonus and the Battle-Brother passes out.

A characteristic's **bonus** is its tens digit, multiplied by its **Unnatural** multiplier. Every Space Marine has Unnatural Strength and Toughness (x2), and power armour adds +20 to Strength first, so a Strength of 45 in armour has a bonus of 12. Strength bonus adds to melee damage, Toughness bonus soaks damage, Agility bonus gives initiative and movement.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: 1d10 + Agility bonus, highest first. A turn is a full action or two half actions; movement is the Agility bonus in metres for a half move, double for a full move, triple to charge and six times to run.
- **Attacks**: Weapon Skill in melee, Ballistic Skill at range. The attack roll asks the kind of attack and the difficulty (range, cover, size: Hulking targets such as Space Marines are +10 to hit). Melee: standard +10, all-out +20, charge +10, swift attack +0 (an extra hit per two degrees of success), lightning attack -10 (an extra hit per degree), called shot -20. Ranged: standard +10, semi-auto +0 (an extra hit per two degrees, up to the rate of fire), full auto -10 (an extra hit per degree), called shot -20. Ranged weapons **jam** on 96 or more (94 for bursts).
- **Hit location**: the attack roll's digits reversed: 01-10 head, 11-20 right arm, 21-30 left arm, 31-70 body, 71-85 right leg, 86-00 left leg.
- **Damage**: the weapon's damage (plus Strength bonus in melee). The damage roll asks the target's armour on that location (less the weapon's Penetration) and Toughness bonus, and takes them off. Tearing weapons roll an extra die and drop the lowest.
- **Righteous Fury**: a natural 10 on a damage die adds 1d10 (the roll window's button), again on another 10. Against xenos a Battle-Brother's Fury needs no confirming; against others, test the attack again first.
- Damage past 0 wounds is **critical damage**: read the critical effects by location and damage type. Targets may **Dodge** or **Parry** once a turn.

## Hordes
Masses of lesser foes are **Hordes**. A horde has **Magnitude** instead of Wounds. Each hit that does any damage after armour and Toughness takes 1 Magnitude; the attack result counts the hits, adding a weapon's Blast value; Righteous Fury takes 1 more; flame and other area weapons hit more. A horde's attacks add 1d10 damage per 10 Magnitude, up to 2d10 (the sheet adds them). Once it has lost a quarter of its Magnitude, a horde that is not Fearless tests Willpower (Morale) or breaks. At 0 it is destroyed or scattered.

## Squad Mode and Cohesion
Make the squad a **Kill Team**. It holds the kill-team's **Cohesion**, which starts each mission at its maximum (the leader's Fellowship bonus plus bonuses for the leader's Rank and Command; enter it). *New mission* refills it.
- In **Solo Mode** each Battle-Brother uses his own Chapter and Speciality abilities.
- In **Squad Mode** the kill-team fights as one: a Battle-Brother spends Cohesion to start an **Attack Pattern** or **Defensive Stance**, and others in Squad Mode join in. Entering Squad Mode needs Cohesion left.
- When the kill-team is shaken (its leader falls, a Battle-Brother is gravely hurt, the squad acts against its oath), roll a **Cohesion challenge**: 1d10 over the current Cohesion loses 1. Playing a Battle-Brother's **demeanours** well can earn Cohesion back.

## Renown, Rank and Requisition
**Rank** comes from total experience (Rank 1 below 17,000 XP). **Renown** rises with deeds: Initiated, Respected (20), Distinguished (40), Famed (60), Hero (80). Before each mission the Battle-Brothers get **Requisition**; better wargear costs Requisition and needs enough Renown. The sheet adds up the Requisition of the weapons, armour and gear listed.

## Fate, insanity and corruption
- **Fate points** come back each session (*New session*). Spend one to reroll a test (a failed test offers *Spend Fate: reroll*), add a degree of success, heal 1d5 wounds, remove fatigue or act first. Burning one saves the Battle-Brother from death.
- **Insanity** brings battle trauma, **Corruption** malignancies and mutations.

## Healing
*Night of rest* clears fatigue and heals 1 wound for a lightly damaged Battle-Brother (damage no more than twice the Toughness bonus). Heavier wounds need an Apothecary's Medicae; critical damage must be treated first.

## Librarians
A Librarian uses powers with a **Focus Power test**: Willpower plus 5 per psy rating used, against the difficulty. Doubles on an unfettered or pushed power bring Psychic Phenomena.
