# Playing Coriolis: The Great Dark

## The game
On the world of Mira, the city of Coriolis sits above the ruins of a vanished civilization. The players are **explorers**: they delve into the Great Dark beneath for relics and knowledge, and fight hunger, monsters and the **Blight**, a corruption that eats the mind. Hope keeps them going; Heart keeps them themselves.

## Rolling
Roll one **base die** (d6) per point of the attribute, plus dice from talents and the situation, plus **gear dice** (yellow) from a tool or weapon. The roll asks how many extra dice to add (negative for penalties). Every **six is a success**; one is usually enough, and extra sixes improve the result.

**Pushing**: once, reroll every die that is not a 1 or a 6. Then lose **1 Hope per 1** on the base dice, counting both rolls. 1s on gear dice lower the gear's bonus.

## Attributes and trackers
- Strength, Agility, Logic, Perception, Insight, Empathy, 2 to 5 (the key attribute of the profession up to 6).
- **Health** = Strength + Agility, lost to harm. At 0 the explorer is broken and takes a critical injury.
- **Hope** = Logic + Empathy, lost to despair and pushing. At 0 they give up.
- **Heart** = Perception + Insight, lost to the Blight. At 0 they are lost to it.
- Talents (1 to 3) add base dice. A keepsake can restore 1 Hope once a session.

## Combat
- Every round, everyone draws an **initiative card** from 1 to 10 and acts in ascending order.
- **Close combat** rolls Strength, **ranged** Agility, plus the weapon's gear bonus. Each success deals the weapon's damage; with as many successes as its **Crit** value the target also takes a critical injury. The target may block or dodge, turning it into an opposed roll.
- **Armor**: roll a die per armor point; each six stops a point of damage.
- Range bands: Close, Short, Medium, Long, Extreme.

## Birds
Explorers may be bonded to a **Bird**, a companion with its own Health, Energy and powers such as Attack, Defend and Clear Blight. Birds are their own entity type.

## Delves
**Supply** (D6 at the start of a delve) stands for food, water and air. Rest in a haven restores the trackers; the *Rest in the haven* button does that.
