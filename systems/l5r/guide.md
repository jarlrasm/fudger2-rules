# Playing Legend of the Five Rings (5th edition)

## Checks
A check rolls **ring dice** (black d6, as many as the ring) and **skill dice** (white d12, as many as the skill rank), and **keeps up to the ring's value**. The player picks the ring by the **approach**: Air (subtle, precise), Earth (careful, steady), Fire (bold, inventive), Water (adaptable, quick) or Void (selfless, intuitive). Set the Approach on the sheet before rolling.

Faces: **S** success, **E** explosive success (counts as a success and rolls another die of that kind, kept for free), **O** opportunity, **T** strife. Fudger keeps the best dice for you: successes first, then opportunities, then dice without strife. The sheet's **Keep** selector puts opportunities or strife-free dice first instead. This is an approximation: every explosive face rolls its extra die, even on a die that would not have been kept, so the GM can drop an extra die whose source was not kept.

- Meet the **TN** in successes to succeed (1 easy, 2 average, 3 hard, 4 very hard, 5+ extreme). Extra successes are **bonus successes**.
- **Opportunities** buy extra effects, even on a failure.
- Each kept **strife** adds 1 strife. When strife exceeds **composure**, the character is **compromised** (they can't keep dice with strife). They may **unmask**: show their true feelings and reset their strife to 0 (the Unmasked button).

## Conflict
Initiative is a check (usually Tactics or Meditation, keeping successes) added to **Focus**, or to **Vigilance** when caught unprepared. Each round a character takes a stance (the ring) and one action.

- **Strikes** use the weapon's Martial Arts skill against TN 2 (or more). Damage is the weapon's damage plus bonus successes minus the target's physical resistance, taken as **fatigue**. Spending opportunities or a critical strike uses the weapon's **deadliness** for a critical strike severity roll.
- When fatigue exceeds **endurance** the character is **incapacitated**.
- Conditions (Afflicted, Bleeding, Dazed, Disoriented, Exhausted, Immobilized, Prone, Unconscious) are tracked on the sheet.

## Honor, Glory and Status
Honor (personal integrity by bushidō), Glory (reputation) and Status (social standing) run 0–100. They go up and down with deeds and are tested in social checks.

## Void points
Spend a Void point to seize the moment: keep one extra die, or ignore critical strike effects. To keep an extra die, hold Shift while rolling (or set **Spend Void**), then tick a box in Void points: the boxes count spent points, up to the Void ring. A night of rest (the button) recovers one Void point and removes fatigue equal to twice Water; meditation can recover more.

## NPCs
NPCs use their rings and five skill groups (Artisan, Martial, Scholar, Social, Trade) instead of single skills. Minions are taken out by a single successful strike.
