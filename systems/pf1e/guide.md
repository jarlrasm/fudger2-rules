# Playing Pathfinder 1st edition

## Checks
Roll **d20 + modifier** against a **DC** set by the GM: meet or beat it to succeed. Skills add ranks, the ability modifier, +3 for a trained class skill, and misc bonuses; armor check penalties apply to Str and Dex skills. Some skills (Knowledge, Disable Device, Spellcraft, Use Magic Device and others) can't be used untrained. Taking 10 or 20 is allowed outside of danger.

**Saving throws** (Fortitude, Reflex, Will) are rolled against the DC of the effect. A natural 20 always succeeds and a natural 1 always fails.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: d20 + Dex + misc. Highest acts first.
- Each turn: a standard action, a move action and a swift action, or one full-round action.
- **Attacks**: d20 + BAB + Str (melee) or Dex (ranged) + size, against the target's AC. A natural 20 always hits and a 1 always misses. A full attack gives extra attacks at −5 each once BAB reaches +6: set the Attack slider.
- A natural roll in the weapon's threat range that hits (a natural 20 always does) is a **critical threat**; roll the attack again, and if that also hits, multiply the damage by the critical multiplier.
- **Touch AC** ignores armor, shield and natural armor; **flat-footed AC** loses the Dex and dodge bonuses.
- **Combat maneuvers** (trip, grapple, bull rush, disarm…) roll CMB against the target's CMD.
- Shaken, frightened and sickened give −2 to attacks, saves and checks.

## Damage and healing
At 0 HP a character is disabled; below 0 dying, losing 1 HP a round unless a DC 10 Con check stabilizes them; at negative Con score they are dead. Rest restores 1 HP per level per night. Nonlethal damage knocks out when it exceeds current HP.

## Magic
Spellcasters prepare or know spells per level; tick the slots as they are cast. Cantrips and orisons (level 0) are cast at will and use no slot. The save DC is 10 + spell level + casting ability modifier. Concentration checks are d20 + caster level + casting ability.

## Library
The built-in library has the core rules' weapons, armor, magic items, spells and feats and the first bestiary's monsters, from the Pathfinder Reference Document under the OGL.
