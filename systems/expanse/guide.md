# Playing The Expanse

## The game
Humanity has spread through the solar system but not beyond it. Earth and Mars glare at each other, Belters work the asteroids for both, and something alien is waking up. Play is grounded science fiction: politics, crews on small ships, scarce air and water, and violence with real consequences.

## Ability tests
Roll **3d6**, one of them the **Drama die** (red), and add the **ability** (Accuracy, Communication, Constitution, Dexterity, Fighting, Intelligence, Perception, Strength, Willpower; usually -2 to 4). A **focus** in the right field adds **+2**.
- Compare with the **target number** (TN): 9 routine, 11 average, 13 challenging, 15 hard, 17 formidable. The roll asks for it; leave it blank if only the GM knows.
- **Stunts**: on a success, if any two dice show the same number, the character gets **stunt points** equal to the Drama die, to spend on extra effects.
- Opposed tests: both roll, higher total wins; the Drama die breaks ties.
- **Fortune** can be spent to change a die's result (2 Fortune per point, the Drama die costs more).
- **Injured** gives -1 to tests and **Wounded** -2; the sheet takes them off.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: a Dexterity test, highest first; the order stays for the encounter. Injured and Wounded take 1 or 2 off it, as off every test.
- **Attacks**: Fighting (melee) or Accuracy (ranged) plus 2 for the weapon group's focus (Accuracy: Pistols, Rifles, Throwing for grenades; Fighting: Brawling, Light Weapons, Heavy Weapons for light and heavy melee), against the target's **Defense** (10 + Dexterity).
- **Damage**: the weapon's dice, plus Strength (melee) or Perception (ranged). The target takes off **Toughness** (Constitution) and **armor**.
- The rest comes off **Fortune**. Without enough Fortune the target can accept **Injured** (and later **Wounded**) to shed 1d6 damage each; damage that still gets through takes them out.
- Minor NPCs have no Fortune: one good hit takes them out.

## Recovery
Fortune comes back in **interludes** between scenes (the sheet's Interlude button restores it and clears fatigue). Injured and Wounded need treatment and rest.

## Ships
Ships have a **Hull** soak die, **Crew Competence** for tests without a named crew member, **Sensors**, weapons (point defense, torpedoes, railguns) and **losses** from hits: damaged hull, sensors, maneuverability, weapons, crew.

## Money
**Income** is rolled like an ability against an item's cost. Buying something whose cost is above the Income can lower it.
