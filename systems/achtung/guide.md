# Achtung! Cthulhu 2d20

## Tests
Choose the attribute (Agility, Brawn, Coordination, Insight, Reason, Will) and the skill (twelve of them) that fit the action; any pair is allowed. Their sum is the target number. Roll 2d20: each die at or under it is a success, a 1 is two successes, and when one of the character's focuses applies every die at or under the skill rating is two. The GM sets the Difficulty (0 to 5, usually 1 or 2); successes beyond it become Momentum. A 20 is a complication, and 19 too when the skill is 0. Up to three more d20s can be bought, with Momentum (1, 2 and 3 for the first, second and third die) or by giving the GM Threat. The sheet asks for the extra dice and the Difficulty.

## Momentum and Threat
Momentum is shared by the group (up to six) and spent to get more information, create advantages, add damage, or keep the initiative. The GM holds Threat, gained when players buy dice with it, from complications and from the scene, and spends it on enemy actions, reinforcements, complications and Mythos horrors.

## Combat
The heroes act first; after each character's turn the action passes to the other side, unless someone spends Momentum or Threat to keep it. Melee attacks are Agility + Fighting, ranged attacks Coordination + Fighting, usually Difficulty 1 (more at long range or against cover). Damage is rolled on Challenge dice (1, 2, blank, blank, 1+Effect, 1+Effect) and comes off Stress after Protection; Effects trigger weapon qualities. A single hit of 5 or more, or one that empties Stress, also inflicts an Injury. A character with an untreated Injury who takes another is out of the fight. Fear and Mythos horrors deal mental damage the same way, reduced by Courage.

## Recovery
A pause between scenes restores Stress. Injuries need first aid or medical care (a Medicine test) and leave lasting effects or scars.

## NPCs
Minions go down to a single Injury and often act in groups; elites take two; notables and nemeses fight like player characters and can spend Threat.
