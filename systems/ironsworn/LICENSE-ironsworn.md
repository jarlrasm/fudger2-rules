# Ironsworn content

`ironsworn.json` contains moves, assets and foes, and `oracles.frules` the oracle tables, from the *Ironsworn Rulebook* and the *Ironsworn Assets Master Set* by Shawn Tomkin, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

The data comes from the Datasworn project (https://github.com/rsek/datasworn) and was converted with `tools/ironsworn_import.py`, changing format and structure only: links between moves became plain text, and the oracle tables became lists of 100 entries, one per roll.
