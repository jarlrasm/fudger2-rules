# Playing Ironsworn

## Action rolls
Roll the **action die** (d6) + a stat (Edge, Heart, Iron, Shadow or Wits) + adds, against two **challenge dice** (d10). The action score can't go above 10.

- **Strong hit:** beat both challenge dice.
- **Weak hit:** beat one.
- **Miss:** beat neither. Ties go to the challenge dice. A miss usually means **Pay the Price**.
- **Match:** both challenge dice show the same number; something unexpected happens, good on a hit and bad on a miss.

Set Adds on the sheet for bonuses from moves and assets. The Suffer and Recover block rolls Endure Harm (Health or Iron, whichever is higher), Endure Stress (Spirit or Heart), Make Camp (Supply) and Face Death (Heart).

## Momentum
Momentum runs from −6 to the maximum (10 minus each marked debility). After a roll you may **burn** momentum: if it beats the challenge dice, use it as your action score, then reset it. The roll says when burning would help. With negative momentum, an action die equal to it is cancelled.

## Vows and progress
**Swear an Iron Vow** to take on a quest, and give it a rank. Mark progress by rank: Troublesome 12 ticks, Dangerous 8, Formidable 4, Extreme 2, Epic 1 (4 ticks fill a box; 10 boxes). The Mark column shows the ticks per mark. **Fulfill your vow** with a progress roll: the number of filled boxes against the challenge dice, with no action die and no momentum. Journeys, bonds and fights use progress tracks too.

## Combat
Whoever has **initiative** acts. Strike (Iron for close, Edge at range) on a hit inflicts harm, which marks progress on the foe; a strong hit keeps initiative. Clash when the foe has initiative. **End the Fight** with a progress roll. Foes inflict harm by rank (1 to 5): suffer it with **Endure Harm**, lowering Health.

## Solo and co-op play
Without a GM, the oracles answer questions. **Ask the Oracle** picks the odds and rolls d100 for yes or no. **Action** and **Theme** give ideas, and the other oracles give places, people, names and twists. With a GM, the GM uses them for inspiration.

## Style
The Ironlands are harsh and the vows are heavy. Let every vow change the world, and every miss make things worse.
