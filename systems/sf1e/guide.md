# Playing Starfinder 1st edition

## The game
Science fantasy in the far future: magic and technology side by side, starships crossing hyperspace, and a crew of explorers, soldiers and mystics. It is a d20 game built on the same bones as Pathfinder 1st edition.

## Checks
Roll **d20 + modifier** and meet or beat a **DC**. Skill bonus = ranks + ability modifier + 3 for a class skill with ranks + other bonuses. Armor check penalty applies to Acrobatics, Athletics, Sleight of Hand and Stealth. Trained-only skills (Computers, Culture, Engineering, Life Science, Medicine, Mysticism, Physical Science, Profession, Sleight of Hand) need a rank for most uses. Saving throws (Fortitude, Reflex, Will) succeed on a natural 20 and fail on a natural 1.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: d20 + Dex + misc; highest first. Each turn has a standard, a move and a swift action, or a full action.
- **Attacks**: melee is d20 + BAB + Str, ranged d20 + BAB + Dex, thrown d20 + BAB + Str. Energy attacks target **EAC**, kinetic attacks **KAC**. Combat maneuvers target KAC + 8.
- A **natural 20** that hits is a **critical hit**: double damage plus the weapon's critical effect. A natural 1 misses.
- **Full attack**: two attacks, each at -4.
- Ranged weapons have a range increment; -2 per increment beyond the first.
- Weapons use charges or rounds: track them under Ammunition.

## Stamina, Hit Points and Resolve
Damage comes off **Stamina Points** first, then **Hit Points**. At 0 HP a character is unconscious and **dying**: they lose 1 Resolve Point each round, and die when they have none left. Spending Resolve stabilizes; damage past 0 HP equal to max HP kills outright.

- **Rest 10 minutes** and spend 1 Resolve to regain all Stamina.
- A **full night's rest** restores Stamina, Resolve and spells, and Hit Points equal to the character's level.

## Starships
Starships are their own entity type. Each crew member takes a role each round: captain, engineer, gunner, pilot or science officer. Ships have AC (against direct-fire weapons), TL (against tracking weapons), Hull Points, shields in four arcs and a critical threshold.

## Creatures
Creatures have Hit Points but no Stamina, EAC and KAC, three saves and listed attack bonuses. Use the stat block's attack bonus against the target's EAC or KAC.
