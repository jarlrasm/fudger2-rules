# Index Card RPG Master Edition

## Targets
Each room or scene has one **Target**, usually 10 to 14, set by the GM and shown to the players. To do anything risky, roll d20 plus a stat (STR, DEX, CON, INT, WIS, CHA, each made of a base and loot bonus). Meet or beat the Target to succeed. Easy tasks are 3 easier (+3), hard ones 3 harder (−3). A natural 20 is a critical success and the following effort roll is Ultimate (d12); a natural 1 is a fumble. The sheet asks for Easy/Hard and the Target. Attacks against a creature use its Defense instead, and monsters roll against the heroes' Defense (10 + CON + armor loot).

## Effort
Success doesn't finish a task; **effort** does. Roll the effort die that fits: d4 basic effort (bare hands, simple work), d6 weapons and tools, d8 guns, d10 magic and energy, d12 ultimate. Add effort bonuses from loot and milestones. Obstacles, locks, rituals and enemies have **hearts**: each heart is 10 points of effort or damage.

## Hit points and dying
Heroes start with one heart (10 HP) and gain more through milestones. At 0 HP a hero is **dying**: roll a d4 for the rounds left; an ally can stabilize them with a WIS or INT roll. Resting between scenes restores all HP.

## Timers
The GM puts timers on danger: a die counting down each round (a d4 trap, a d6 collapse). When it hits zero, the thing happens. Timers keep every scene moving.

## Turns
No initiative roll: play goes around the table, and the GM acts between players or at the end of each round.

## Loot and milestones
Heroes carry ten equipped and ten carried items; loot gives stat, effort and Defense bonuses. At the end of an adventure each hero gets a **milestone**: a stat or effort point, a heart, an ability or better loot. The **hero coin** rerolls any one roll.

## Monsters
Monsters have hearts, a Defense, a bonus for their rolls, and actions with effort dice; nasty ones have special actions on a timer.
