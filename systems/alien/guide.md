# Playing the Alien RPG

## Rolling
Roll a pool of **d6s**: attribute + skill (+ gear bonus), plus one yellow **stress die** for each point of Stress. Every **6** is a success; one is enough, and extra successes buy stunts.

- **Push:** after a failed (or not good enough) roll, a character can push once: reroll every die that isn't a 6 (stress dice showing 1 stay), and add a stress die. The roll window offers *Push (+1 Stress)*, which also raises Stress on the sheet. Synthetics never push stress.
- **Panic:** if any stress die shows **1**, the character must make a **Panic Roll**: 1d6 + Stress. 7 or more means they lose control; the higher the roll, the worse the effect. A panicking character can't push.
- Stress rises from pushing, firing full auto, seeing horrors, and other characters' panic. It goes down by one each Turn of rest in a safe place.

## Combat
Each round has a **slow action** and a **fast action**. Initiative is drawn: add the Initiative Cards deck to the session and use **Deal initiative** in the encounter (lowest acts first), or roll the Initiative field (d10).

1. Close Combat (Strength) or Ranged Combat (Agility) against no opposed roll; range modifies the pool.
2. Each success deals the weapon's damage (extra successes add damage or stunts); armor rolls its rating to block.
3. At 0 Health a character is **Broken** and rolls on the Critical Injury table; some are fatal unless treated with Medical Aid.

## Survival
Track air, food, water and power as supply dice: roll them when used and lose one for each 1. Without food, water, sleep or warmth, characters suffer the conditions on the sheet.

## Style
Fear the creature, distrust the Company. Players should feel outmatched, and their **personal agendas** set them against each other.
