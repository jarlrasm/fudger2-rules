# Playing Stars Without Number

## Skill checks
Roll **2d6 + skill level + attribute modifier** against a difficulty: 6 easy, 8 typical, 10 hard, 12 very hard, 14 nearly impossible. Untrained skills are −1. Each skill row on the sheet has the attribute it uses; change it when another fits the task better. Only roll when failure is interesting and the outcome is uncertain.

## Combat
- **Initiative:** each side (or each character) rolls **1d8 + DEX modifier**; highest acts first.
- **Attack:** roll **d20 + attack bonus + combat skill (Stab, Shoot or Punch) + attribute modifier + the target's AC**. 20 or more hits. Without even level-0 in the combat skill the attack is at -2 (not -1 as for other untrained skills). A natural 20 always hits, a 1 always misses. The sheet asks for the target's AC.
- **Damage:** the weapon's die plus the attribute modifier. Many melee weapons deal **Shock** on a miss to targets with an AC at or below the Shock rating.
- At 0 HP a character is mortally wounded and dies in six rounds unless stabilised with Heal.

## Saving throws
Roll **d20** at or over the save: Physical, Evasion, Mental or Luck. Each is 16 minus level minus the better of two attribute modifiers.

## System Strain
Medical drugs, cyberware and some psychic powers add **System Strain**. It can't go above Constitution, and drops by one each night's rest.

## Psychics
Psychics commit **Effort** to use their disciplines' techniques; Effort comes back after a scene or a day.

## Ships
Make a Spaceships entity for the crew's ship. Ship weapons use the gunner's Shoot and the ship's numbers; damage beyond Armor lowers HP.

## Style
Build a sector of worlds with tags, factions with goals, and problems. Let the players choose where to go, and let the factions move between sessions.
