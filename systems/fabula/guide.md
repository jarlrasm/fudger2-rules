# Playing Fabula Ultima

## Attributes and checks
Characters have four attributes rated as dice: **Dexterity**, **Insight**, **Might** and **Willpower** (d6, d8, d10 or d12). A check names two attributes (they may be the same, like Ins + Ins) and rolls one die for each:

- **Result** = the two dice added together, plus bonuses. Beat the **Difficulty Level** (DL 7 easy, 10 normal, 13 hard, 16 very hard).
- The **High Roll** (HR) is the higher of the two dice. Damage is HR + the weapon's damage.
- **Critical success**: both dice show the same number, 6 or more. It always succeeds and grants an opportunity.
- **Fumble**: both dice show 1. It always fails; the GM gets an opportunity and the player gains a Fabula Point.

Fudger keeps a Checks list on the sheet; add any pair you need. A failed check offers to invoke a Trait: it spends a Fabula Point and rerolls both dice. A fumble can't be rerolled.

## Statuses
Status effects step attribute dice down (never below d6): **Slow** (Dex), **Dazed** (Ins), **Weak** (Mig), **Shaken** (Wlp), **Enraged** (Dex and Ins), **Poisoned** (Mig and Wlp). The sheet shows the current die.

## Conflict
- Sides alternate turns. To see who starts, the party's best **initiative** check (Dex + Ins) is compared with the villains' initiative score.
- **Accuracy checks** use the weapon's attributes against the target's **Defense** (based on Dexterity plus armor). **Magic checks** target **Magic Defense** (Insight-based).
- Damage types (physical, air, bolt, dark, earth, fire, ice, light, poison) meet **affinities**: vulnerable (double), resistant (half), immune or absorb.
- At half HP or less a character is in **Crisis**. At 0 HP they must surrender (and suffer a setback) or make a heroic sacrifice.

## Resources
**HP** and **MP** are five times the Might and Willpower dice plus level. **IP** (Inventory Points) pay for potions and tools. **Fabula Points** are spent to invoke traits and bonds for rerolls, or to alter the story.

## Bonds
Bonds tie characters to people with feelings (admiration or inferiority, loyalty or mistrust, affection or hatred). Each feeling adds +1 to checks that act on it.
