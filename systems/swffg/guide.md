# Star Wars Roleplaying (Edge of the Empire, Age of Rebellion, Force and Destiny)

## Checks
A check is a pool of narrative dice. The higher of the skill's rank and its characteristic gives the number of dice; the lower of the two is upgraded from green ability dice to yellow proficiency dice. The GM sets the difficulty in purple dice: 1 Easy, 2 Average, 3 Hard, 4 Daunting, 5 Formidable. Upgrades turn purple dice into red challenge dice (adversary talents, a Destiny Point spent by the GM). Bad circumstances add black setback dice, good ones blue boost dice.

Read the pool by cancelling: each failure cancels a success, each threat cancels an advantage. One net success or more passes. Advantage buys side benefits (recover strain, give a boost die to an ally, trigger a weapon's critical); threat costs strain or hands the enemy an edge. A Triumph counts as a success and a big boon; a Despair counts as a failure and a big setback. The sheet rolls the pool, asks for the difficulty dice and for setback dice (a negative number gives boost dice), and reports net successes, advantages, Triumphs and Despairs.

## Combat
Initiative is Cool and Presence when ready, Vigilance and Willpower when surprised; slots go in order of successes, then advantages. On a turn a character has one action and one maneuver (and can trade the action for a second maneuver, or suffer 2 strain for one more).

Attacks: melee and brawl are Average; ranged difficulty follows range band (Short Easy, Medium Average, Long Hard, Extreme Daunting). The target's Defense adds setback dice. A hit deals the weapon's damage plus one per net success, minus the target's soak, as wounds. Melee weapons written +N add Brawn. Advantages equal to the weapon's Critical rating (or a Triumph) inflict a critical injury: roll d100 plus 10 per critical injury the target already has.

## Wounds, strain and rest
Wounds over the wound threshold incapacitate a character and give a critical injury. Strain over the strain threshold knocks them out. After an encounter a Simple check of Cool or Discipline recovers one strain per success. A night's rest heals one wound and all strain; a stimpack heals 5 wounds (fewer each further use that day). Critical injuries need a Medicine check to heal.

## Minions, rivals and nemeses
Minions act as a group: they share wounds and use the group size minus one as ranks in their group skills, and they have no strain. Rivals take a few hits and have no strain either (strain damage becomes wounds). Nemeses have everything the heroes have.

## Obligation, Duty and Morality
Edge of the Empire characters carry Obligation: the GM rolls d100 at the start of each session, and a roll within someone's Obligation lowers the group's strain thresholds and drives the story. Age of Rebellion characters earn Duty for the Alliance. Force and Destiny characters have Morality: dark side pips used for powers and dark deeds give Conflict, and the GM rolls d10 against it at the end of the session.

## The Force and Destiny
Force-sensitive characters have a Force Rating: roll that many white Force dice for powers. Light side pips (open circles) power the effect; a light-side character may use dark pips by spending a Destiny Point, suffering strain and gaining Conflict. At the start of each session, roll one Force die per player for the Destiny pool: light pips are the players' Destiny Points, dark pips the GM's. Spending one upgrades a check's ability die (or the opposition's difficulty die) and turns the point to the other side.

## Ships and vehicles
Vehicles and starships have silhouette, speed, handling (boost or setback on piloting), defense by arc, armor (soak), hull trauma and system strain thresholds. Vehicle weapons use Gunnery, with damage scaled tenfold against personal targets.
