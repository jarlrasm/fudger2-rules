# Playing D&D 3.5

## Checks
Roll **d20 + modifier** against a **DC** set by the GM: meet or beat it to succeed. A skill adds its ranks, the ability modifier and misc bonuses (racial, synergy, items); armor check penalties apply to Str and Dex skills, double for Swim. Class skills can have up to level + 3 ranks; cross-class skills cost two points a rank and go up to half that. Some skills (Decipher Script, Disable Device, Knowledge, Open Lock, Spellcraft, Tumble, Use Magic Device and others) can't be used untrained. Taking 10 or 20 is allowed outside of danger. Ability checks are d20 + the ability modifier.

**Saving throws** (Fortitude, Reflex, Will) are rolled against the DC of the effect. A natural 20 always succeeds and a natural 1 always fails.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: d20 + Dex + misc (Improved Initiative gives +4). Highest acts first.
- Each turn: a standard action and a move action, or one full-round action, plus free actions; five-foot steps when you don't otherwise move.
- **Attacks**: d20 + base attack bonus + Str (melee) or Dex (ranged) + size, against the target's AC. A natural 20 always hits and a 1 always misses. A full attack gives extra attacks at −5 each once BAB reaches +6: set the Attack slider. Ranged attacks take −2 per range increment past the first.
- A natural roll in the weapon's threat range that hits is a **critical threat**; roll again to confirm, and on a hit multiply the damage.
- **Touch AC** ignores armor, shield and natural armor; **flat-footed AC** loses the Dex and dodge bonuses.
- **Grapple**: touch attack to grab, then opposed grapple checks (BAB + Str + grapple size modifier: Small −4, Large +4).
- Attacks of opportunity: moving out of a threatened square, casting, or ranged attacks next to an enemy provoke one.

## Damage and healing
At 0 HP a character is disabled (one move or standard action a turn); from −1 to −9 dying, losing 1 HP a round with a 10% chance each round to stabilize; at −10 dead. Natural healing restores 1 HP per level for a night's rest (2 per level for a full day of bed rest). Nonlethal damage heals 1 per level each hour. A single hit of 50 damage or more calls for a DC 15 Fortitude save against massive damage.

## Magic
Spellcasters prepare or know spells per level; tick the slots as they are cast. The save DC is 10 + spell level + casting ability modifier. Bonus spells come from a high casting ability. Concentration (a Con skill) keeps a spell going when hurt or distracted: DC 10 + damage taken + spell level.

## Monsters
Stat blocks list size and type, Hit Dice, Initiative, speed, AC (touch and flat-footed), base attack and grapple, attacks and full attack, space and reach, special attacks and qualities, saves, abilities, skills, feats and Challenge Rating. A party of four of a level equal to the CR can expect a fair fight.

## Library
The built-in library has the System Reference Document's weapons, armor, goods, spells, feats and monsters, Open Game Content under the OGL 1.0a.
