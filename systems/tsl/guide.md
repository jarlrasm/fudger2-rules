# Playing Thirsty Sword Lesbians

A Powered by the Apocalypse game of swashbuckling, melodrama and queer romance. Feelings matter as much as fights, and the GM never rolls.

## Moves
When a character does something that triggers a move, roll **2d6 + stat**:
- **10+ up beat**: they do it, and well.
- **7-9 mixed beat**: they do it, at a cost or with a complication.
- **6- down beat**: mark XP and the GM makes a move. A down beat is not always a failure: something interesting goes wrong.

The stats are **Daring** (force, of muscle or personality), **Grace** (poise and agility), **Heart** (emotional insight and expression), **Wit** (cleverness) and **Spirit** (metaphysical power and integrity), from -1 to +3. **Forward** adds to the next roll and **Ongoing** to every roll while it lasts; the sheet adds both.

Basic moves on the sheet: **Fight** (+Daring or +Grace) to incapacitate someone, choosing options such as inflicting a Condition or taking a String, while the opponent always chooses one too; **Defy Disaster** (any stat, by approach); **Entice** (+Heart) to gain a String; **Figure Out a Person** (+Wit) to ask questions; **Emotional Support** (+Heart or +Spirit) to let someone clear a Condition, mark XP or take +1 forward; **Call on a Toxic Power** (+Spirit). Figure Out a Person and Emotional Support ask whether a String is spent for +3. Playbook moves attached to the sheet that name a stat get their own roll under Move Rolls.

## Conditions and Defeat
There are no hit points. Conditions are hard feelings, and each gives -2 to one basic move (the sheet takes it off): **Angry** (Figure Out a Person), **Frightened** (Fight), **Guilty** (Emotional Support), **Hopeless** (Defy Disaster), **Insecure** (Entice). They clear through moves such as Emotional Support, or at the end of a scene after a destructive action. A PC who must mark a sixth Condition is **Defeated** for the scene. **Stagger** is a reactive move for a staggering blow: the sheet shows the options for the Conditions marked.

NPCs take Conditions too. Set how many Conditions defeat each one (1 for a minion, up to 5 for a nemesis). A formidable NPC who takes a Condition answers with a GM move that fits the feeling.

## Strings
A String is emotional influence over someone. Spend one to offer them XP for doing something, learn what it would take to get them to do what you want, add 1 to your roll against them, or add or take 1 from a roll they make. A fourth String on one person is a String Advance: learn a deep truth, keep one String and gain 2 XP. The GM has one generic String per player each session, and NPCs keep the Strings they gain.

## Experience
Mark XP on every down beat. Five XP buys an Advance (three in a one-shot). At the end of a session tick what happened (love confessed, a blow against oppression, leaping into danger with panache, a safety tool used) and press **End session**.

## Playbooks
Each playbook has a feature: the Beast's **Feral** (Transform at 4), the Chosen's **Destiny** and Aspects, the Devoted's **Devotion** and **Last Stand** (+Conditions), the Infamous's wicked past and vows, the Nature Witch's **Trials**, the Scoundrel's fickleness and Heat of the Moment, the Seeker's **Tradition**, Commandments and Convictions, the Spooky Witch's **Unseen**, and the Trickster's **Feelings** (the mask comes off at 4). Each also has a Smitten question and an extra question for Figure Out a Person in a fight.

## Toxic Powers
Toxic Powers are the oppressive forces of the setting: empires, inquisitions, afflictions, complacent elites. Give each its custom GM moves and one or more countdowns that advance on down beats or when ignored, and show the players the steps.

## Style
Be a fan of the characters. Make adversaries appealing, give them reasons, and make conflict emotional and personal. Offer hard choices and temptations, use Strings and Conditions often, and leave room for kissing. There is no initiative: move the spotlight as the drama asks.
