# Thirsty Sword Lesbians content

This work uses material from the *Thirsty Sword Lesbians* roleplaying game (found at https://swordlesbians.com), designed by April Kit Walsh and published by Evil Hat Productions, LLC, pursuant to the open license available at poweredbylesbians.com.

## What is here

`tsl.json` holds the basic moves, the nine playbooks (their stat columns, aesthetics and features) and every playbook move, feature move and Truth of Heart and Blade from the Sapphic Reference Document, the open text of the game, as published at https://poweredbylesbians.com/srd/ (the basic moves page and the nine playbook pages, read 2026-11-26). It was converted with `tools/tsl_import.py`. Only the format was changed: quotation marks and dashes were made plain, tick-box tracks were written on one line, and the commentary and examples of play that follow each basic move were left out. No art, logos or trade dress are included, and the Powered by Lesbians logo is not used.

The sheet layout, descriptions, hints and `guide.md` are written in our own words.
