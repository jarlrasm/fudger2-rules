# Playing Old-School Essentials

## Rolling
- **Attacks:** roll d20 plus the STR (melee) or DEX (missile) modifier; the result says which Armour Class it hits (THAC0 minus the total, descending AC). A natural 20 always hits and a 1 always misses. With ascending AC, compare the total plus the Attack Bonus to the target's AC.
- **Saving throws:** roll d20 at or above the save value. Wisdom's modifier applies to saves against magic.
- **X-in-6 checks:** open doors, find secret doors and traps, and listen roll a d6 at or under the number shown.
- **Ability checks** (an optional rule): d20 at or under the score.
- **Thief skills:** d100 at or under the percentage (hear noise is X-in-6).
- **Morale** for monsters and retainers: 2d6 over the morale score and they flee or surrender.

## Exploration
Time passes in 10-minute **turns** in dungeons. Rest one turn in six. Torches last 6 turns, lanterns 24. Roll for wandering monsters every two turns. Doors are often stuck. Mapping and careful play matter more than rolls.

## Combat
1. Each side rolls **initiative** with a d6 (or each character, using the optional rule: roll the Initiative field). The higher side acts first, each round.
2. Order in a round: morale checks, movement, missile attacks, spells, melee.
3. Damage comes off HP. At 0 HP a character is dead.
4. Spells are memorised each day in the slots shown and are gone once cast.

## Advancement
Experience comes mainly from treasure brought back to safety (1 XP per gold piece) and from defeating monsters. A high prime requisite adjusts the XP gained.

## Style
Rulings over rules. Describe the world, ask what the players do, and let their choices and the dice decide. Fights are deadly, so clever players avoid fair ones.
