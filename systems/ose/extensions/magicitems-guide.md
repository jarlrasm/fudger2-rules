# Basic Fantasy Magic Items

Magic treasure from the open Basic Fantasy rules (part 7 of the core book), which use the same B/X rules as Old-School Essentials.

## On the character
- Add items from the **Magic Items** library to the character. Each item has a Kind, a Bonus, and, where they apply, its charges when found, uses per day, an effect roll and the classes that can use it.
- **Magic weapons** are generic (Weapon +1, Weapon +1, +3 vs. Special Enemy, Cursed Weapon -1...). Rename the item after the weapon and fill in its damage die, and Long Range for a missile or thrown weapon. **Magic Attacks** then rolls it like a normal attack, with the bonus on attack and damage (plus STR for melee, DEX to hit for missiles). A weapon with a special enemy gets a second row with the higher bonus for that enemy.
- **Magic armour, shields and protection items** (Ring of Protection +1 to +3) show in **Magic Protection**. Keep the mundane Armour Class in the Combat block; AC with Magic takes the bonuses off it. Only the strongest protection item counts, and its bonus is added to the saving throw rolls shown there. Untick In Use on items carried but not worn.
- **Magic Item Rolls** rolls the dice in an item's Roll, such as a wand of fireballs (6d6) or a staff of healing (1d6+1 per charge).
- **Item Charges** is a list to count down wand and staff charges, wishes and other limited uses during play. Wands are found with 2d10 charges, staves with 3d10.

## Using items
- Potions last 1d6+6 turns unless the item says otherwise, and affect only the drinker.
- Wands are for Magic-Users, and saves against them use the Wands column. Staves are for Magic-Users unless noted (the staff of healing and staff of striking are for Clerics), and saves against them use the Spells column. Rods can be used by anyone.
- Spell scrolls: Magic-Users cast read magic on the scroll once first. A spell the caster doesn't know has a 10% chance to fail (not for Clerics), and each spell level above what the caster can cast adds 10%. Protection scrolls can be read by anyone who knows the language.
- A character wears at most one ring per hand, one cloak, one pair of boots, one helm and one pendant. Two items with the same lasting effect don't stack.
- Cursed items can't be got rid of without remove curse. Cursed armour shows its curse only when the wearer is first hit.

## Placing treasure
Monster sheets have **Magic Treasure** rolls for the random tables. Roll **Type of Item** first (the "Any" column; for a weapons-or-armour hoard, 01-70 is a weapon), then that kind's table:
- **Weapon** rolls the weapon type, its bonus (the melee or missile column, as fits the weapon), the special enemy and a special ability where the bonus calls for them.
- **Armour** rolls the type and bonus, with cursed results.
- **Scroll** gives the level of every spell on a spell scroll; **Wand Staff or Rod** gives the charges left.
- **Miscellaneous** rolls the effect subtable, the effect and the item's form, such as Ring of Invisibility.
- **Rare** names a rare item, and which device of summoning elementals it is.

Then add the item from the library, renamed and filled in as needed. The book lists no gold or XP value for magic items. Making them is covered in the Game Master section.
