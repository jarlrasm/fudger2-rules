# Playing Delta Green

## Rolling
Roll d100 **at or under** a skill or a statistic ×5. The Modifier slider adds or takes away 20 or 40 for easy or hard situations (Shift for +20, Ctrl for −20).

- **Critical success:** 01, or doubles (11, 22 …) at or under the target. **Fumble:** 00 (100), or doubles over the target.
- Opposed rolls: both roll; a success beats a failure, a critical beats a success, and otherwise the higher roll wins.
- Tick **Failed** when an agent fails a skill roll. After an operation, each ticked skill gains 1d4.
- Only call for a roll when failure would matter and time or danger stops the agent from just doing it.

## Sanity and Bonds
A shock calls for a Sanity roll; the loss is written like `1/1d6` (success/failure). Losing 5 or more at once means temporary insanity. Reaching the **Breaking Point** gives a disorder, and the breaking point is reset to current SAN minus POW.

- **Bonds** are the people the agent cares about. An agent can spend Bond points to lower a Sanity loss (projecting onto a Bond), which damages that relationship.
- Repeated violence or helplessness fills the **Adapted** boxes. After the third the agent stops losing Sanity to it but loses CHA and Bonds instead.

## Combat
Act in DEX order; roll the Initiative field to join the tracker.

1. Attack with a skill from the Attacks list. The target may Dodge (opposed), or fight back against melee.
2. Damage is subtracted from HP after armor. A **Lethality** weapon instead rolls d100 under its Lethality rating to kill outright; a miss deals the two dice added together as damage.
3. At 2 HP or less an agent is unconscious; at 0 they die. First Aid restores 1d4 once per injury.

## Style
The agents are government employees fighting something they can never understand, and the cost is their lives at home. Make the mundane paperwork feel real and the unnatural rare and terrible.
