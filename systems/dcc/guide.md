# Playing Dungeon Crawl Classics

## The game
Old-school sword and sorcery in the style of the pulp fantasy that inspired the first roleplaying games. Characters are fragile, magic is dangerous and unpredictable, and the dice are weird.

## The funnel
Play usually starts with each player running three or four **level-0** peasants. Roll 3d6 in order for Strength, Agility, Stamina, Personality, Intelligence and Luck, then an occupation (which gives a weapon and trade goods) and a birth augur (which says what the Luck modifier applies to). Most will die. Survivors pick a class at level 1: cleric, thief, warrior, wizard, dwarf, elf or halfling.

## Rolls
- Roll the **action die** (usually d20) plus modifiers against a DC. Some effects move the die up or down the **dice chain**: d3, d4, d5, d6, d7, d8, d10, d12, d14, d16, d20, d24, d30.
- **Ability modifiers**: 3 is -3, 4-5 is -2, 6-8 is -1, 9-12 is 0, 13-15 is +1, 16-17 is +2, 18 is +3.
- **Luck**: any character may burn Luck points to add to a roll, one for one. Burned Luck rarely returns (thieves and halflings get some back). The **Luck check** is a d20 roll under the current Luck score.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: d20 + Agility modifier (warriors add their level). Highest first.
- **Attacks**: action die + attack bonus + Strength (melee) or Agility (missile) against AC. A **natural 20** is a critical hit: roll the crit die on the class's crit table and add Luck. A **natural 1** is a fumble: roll the armor's fumble die on the fumble table.
- **Mighty Deeds**: warriors and dwarves roll a **deed die** instead of a fixed attack bonus. The player declares a stunt before rolling; if the deed die shows 3 or more and the attack hits, the deed happens.
- **Hit points**: a level-0 character at 0 HP dies. Higher levels bleed out over as many rounds as their level and can be saved by an ally; after the fight, someone may roll Luck to find the body still breathing.

## Magic
- **Wizards and elves** roll a spell check: d20 + caster level + Intelligence. The result is read on the spell's own table. A failure loses the spell for the day; a natural 1 misfires and may bring corruption.
- **Clerics** roll d20 + caster level + Personality. A natural roll inside the **disapproval range** angers their god (roll on the disapproval table), and each failure widens the range by 1. It resets to 1 each day.

## Healing
A night's rest heals 1 hit point (2 with a full day of bed rest). Clerics heal by laying on hands.
