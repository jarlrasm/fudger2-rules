# Playing Vaesen

## The game
Scandinavia in the 19th century. The player characters have **the Sight**: they can see the **vaesen**, the creatures of folklore, and they belong to the Society that investigates them from a ruined castle in Upsala. A mystery has three parts: the investigation, the vaesen's lair and its weakness, and the confrontation.

## Rolling
Roll a d6 for each point of **attribute + skill**, plus or minus modifiers. Each **six** is a success; one is enough. Extra sixes buy extra effects.

**Pushing**: once per roll a character may reroll every die that is not a six. Afterwards they must mark a **condition**, physical or mental, which Fudger reminds you of.

Attributes and their skills:
- **Physique**: Agility, Close combat, Force
- **Precision**: Medicine, Ranged combat, Stealth
- **Logic**: Investigation, Learning, Vigilance
- **Empathy**: Inspiration, Manipulation, Observation

## Conditions
There are no hit points. Damage and fear mark conditions:
- **Physical**: Exhausted, Battered, Wounded. Each gives −1 to Physique and Precision rolls.
- **Mental**: Angry, Frightened, Hopeless. Each gives −1 to Logic and Empathy rolls.

A character with all three of a kind marked who takes more is **broken** physically or mentally and suffers a critical injury or mental trauma. Conditions heal with rest at the castle.

## Combat and fear
- **Initiative**: deal a card from 1 to 10 to every character and vaesen; act in order.
- A hit deals damage (marks conditions) equal to the weapon's damage plus extra successes.
- Vaesen are not fought like people: most can only be defeated by finding their **weakness** in the investigation. They act by drawing from their own list of attacks and use **Might** for rolls. Encountering one forces a fear roll (Logic + Vigilance or Empathy) or a mental condition.

## Between mysteries
At the castle characters heal, upgrade the Society's headquarters and gain experience for what they learned.
