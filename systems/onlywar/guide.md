# Playing Only War

## The game
The Warhammer 40,000 far future, seen from the trenches. The players are soldiers of the **Imperial Guard**, one squad of a regiment the group builds together at the start: its **home world**, **commanding officer**, **regiment type**, **training and equipment doctrines** and a **drawback**. Each player picks a **speciality**. Guardsman specialities (Heavy Gunner, Medic, Operator, Sergeant, Weapon Specialist) each come with a **Comrade**; support specialities (Commissar, Ministorum Priest, Ogryn, Ratling, Sanctioned Psyker, Storm Trooper, Tech-Priest Enginseer) stand alone. Play is war: missions handed down from command, fought with too little kit against too many foes.

## Sheets
- **Characters**: the Guardsman.
- **Comrades**: one per Guardsman speciality. Its state (Unharmed, Wounded, Dead), cohesion, Fleeing and Pinned.
- **Squads**: the regiment and the squad's **Logistics Rating**, with the Logistics test.
- **Vehicles**: armour by facing, Structural Integrity, motive systems and weapons.
- **Creatures**: foes and other NPCs, with a compact profile and attack rows.

## Tests
Roll **d100** and get the **target** or less. The target is a characteristic (or a skill) plus the difficulty:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Trivial | +60 |
| Easy | +30 |
| Routine | +20 |
| Ordinary | +10 |
| Challenging | +0 |
| Difficult | -10 |
| Hard | -20 |
| Very Hard | -30 |
| Hellish | -60 |

A success is **one degree of success**, plus one for each step the tens digit of the roll is below the target's; a failure counts its **degrees of failure** the same way. 01 always succeeds and 100 always fails.

Skills have ranks: **Known** (+0), **Trained** (+10), **Experienced** (+20) and **Veteran** (+30). An untrained skill tests at -20, and the specialist skills (Common Lore, Forbidden Lore, Scholastic Lore, Linguistics, Navigate, Operate, Trade) can't be used untrained (the sheet shows 0). Each level of **fatigue** gives -10 to tests; more fatigue than the Toughness bonus and the Guardsman passes out.

A characteristic's **bonus** is its tens digit (times an Unnatural multiplier, as for an Ogryn). Strength bonus adds to melee damage, Toughness bonus soaks damage, Agility bonus gives initiative and movement.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: 1d10 + Agility bonus, highest first. A turn is a full action or two half actions. Movement is the Agility bonus in metres for a half move, double for a full move, triple to charge, six times to run.
- **Attacks**: Weapon Skill in melee, Ballistic Skill at range. The attack roll asks the kind of attack and the difficulty (range, cover, size). Melee: standard +10, all-out +30, charge +20, swift +0 (an extra hit per two degrees beyond the first), lightning -10 (an extra hit per degree beyond the first), called shot -20, guarded -10. Ranged: standard +10, semi-auto +0 (an extra hit per two degrees beyond the first), full auto -10 (an extra hit per degree beyond the first), extra hits no more than the rate of fire; called shot -20; suppressing fire -20, hits no more than the rate of fire, and everyone in the zone tests Pinning. Ranged weapons **jam** on 96 or more (94 for bursts and suppressing fire). A weapon without that burst in its RoF can't fire it.
- **Hit location**: the attack roll's digits reversed: 01-10 head, 11-20 right arm, 21-30 left arm, 31-70 body, 71-85 right leg, 86-00 left leg.
- **Damage**: the weapon's damage (plus Strength bonus in melee). The damage roll asks the target's armour on that location (less the weapon's Penetration) and Toughness bonus, and takes them off. The attack's degrees of success may replace the lowest damage die (the result names it). Tearing weapons roll an extra die and drop the lowest.
- **Righteous Fury**: a natural 10 on a damage die. Roll 1d5 (the roll window's button) and apply that critical effect for the location and damage type; at least 1 damage gets through. Against a vehicle the hit must damage it.
- Damage past 0 wounds is **critical damage**: read the critical effects by location and damage type. Targets may **Dodge** or **Parry** once a turn.

## Comrades and orders
A Comrade is a soldier who follows its Guardsman. It has no wounds or tests of its own:
- One hit wounds it and a second kills it (*Hit*). A wounded Comrade can't run. *Patch up* heals it; a dead one is replaced back in camp (*Replace*).
- A hit on the Guardsman that rolls **doubles** strikes the Comrade instead if it is in **cohesion** (within 5 metres, nothing solid between). Blast and Spray catch it in their area.
- It passes or fails **Fear** and **Pinning** with its Guardsman: Fleeing or Pinned until a rallying order (*Rally*). 10 Insanity or Corruption in one round kills it.
- **Orders** cost the Comrade a half or full action: **Ranged Volley** (+5 to the Guardsman's Ballistic Skill this turn), **Close Quarters** (the Guardsman counts as ganging up), **Take Cover**, and moves. Specialities add their own (the Medic's, the Heavy Gunner's, and so on: the Orders library type). An order helps only in cohesion unless it says otherwise.

## Logistics
Gear past the regiment's standard kit comes by a **Logistics test** on the Squads sheet: d100 at or under the Logistics Rating plus the front (time at the front, how long the front has been active, the war conditions), the item's **availability** for the troops deployed and its **craftsmanship** (Poor +20, Good -30, Best -50; standard kit for the warzone +20). The test asks the availability and craftsmanship. A failure means nothing turns up this time. The GM raises or lowers the rating after missions.

## Vehicles
Attacks on a vehicle use the armour of the facing hit and no Toughness: ask the facing's armour, leave Toughness at 0. Damage past armour comes off **Structural Integrity**; past 0 it is critical damage on the vehicle tables. The driver tests Operate plus the vehicle's Manoeuvrability. *Repair* restores it after a full repair.

## Fate, insanity and corruption
- **Fate points** come back each session (*New session*). Spend one to reroll a test (a failed test offers *Spend Fate: reroll*), add a degree of success, heal 1d5 wounds, remove fatigue or act first. Burning one saves the Guardsman from death.
- **Insanity** brings disorders at 40, 60 and 80, **Corruption** a Malignancy test for each 10. At 100 of either the Guardsman is lost.

## Healing
*Night of rest* clears fatigue and heals 1 wound for a lightly damaged Guardsman (damage no more than twice the Toughness bonus). Heavier wounds need a Medic's Medicae; critical damage must be treated first.

## Psykers
A Sanctioned Psyker uses powers with a **Focus Power test**: Willpower plus 5 per psy rating used, against the difficulty. Doubles on an unfettered or pushed power bring Psychic Phenomena.
