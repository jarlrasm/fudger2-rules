# LANCER content

`lancer.json` contains the mech frames, weapons, systems and weapon mods of the *LANCER Core Book*. It was converted with `tools/lancer_import.py` from Massif Press's lancer-data (https://github.com/massif-press/lancer-data), the data that COMP/CON uses. lancer-data is published under the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later (https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html), and so is this conversion of it.
