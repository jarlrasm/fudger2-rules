# Playing Lancer

## Rolls
Roll **d20 + bonus**. **Accuracy** and **Difficulty** cancel each other; roll the rest as d6s and add the **highest** (accuracy) or subtract it (difficulty). Set Accuracy on the sheet before rolling: positive is accuracy, negative difficulty.

- **Checks** (skill triggers for pilots, Hull, Agility, Systems or Engineering for mechs) succeed on **10 or more**.
- **Attacks** add Grit and hit on a total at or above the target's **Evasion** (weapons) or **E-Defense** (tech attacks). The sheet asks for it. A total of **20 or more** that hits is a **critical**: roll damage twice and keep the highest.

## Pilots and mechs
The character sheet is the pilot: License Level, Grit (half the license level, rounded up), HASE, skill triggers and talents. Make a **Mechs** entity for each pilot's mech, fill in the frame's numbers once, and copy in Grit and HASE. Put the mech on the battlemap; distances are in squares.

## Combat
Players and NPCs alternate: any player who hasn't acted may go, then the GM activates an NPC. Each turn has a move and two quick actions, or one full action.

1. Damage minus Armor takes HP. At 0 HP the mech loses a point of **Structure**, HP resets, and you roll **Structure Roll**: the lowest d6 decides the result.
2. Heat over the Heat Cap clears heat, loses a point of **Stress**, and you roll **Overheat Roll** for the overheating result.
3. At 0 Structure the mech is destroyed; at 0 Stress the reactor melts down.

## Downtime
Between missions pilots take downtime actions: power at a cost, get a damn drink, buy some time, and gather information. License Level rises after each mission.

## Style
Big machines, hard choices, and a hopeful future worth fighting for. Give every mission an objective beyond killing everything.
