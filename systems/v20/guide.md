# Playing Vampire: the Masquerade (20th anniversary edition)

## The game
The Kindred are the dead who drink the blood of the living. They hide behind the Masquerade, play the Jyhad of the elders through the clans and sects (Camarilla, Sabbat, Anarchs, independents), and fight the Beast inside them every night. Themes: personal horror, politics, hunger and the slow loss of Humanity.

## Rolls
Roll **attribute + ability** d10s against a **difficulty** (usually 6; 3 is easy, 9 very hard, never above 10). Each die at or above the difficulty is a success; each **1** cancels one. No successes with a 1 showing is a **botch**. One success is marginal, three complete, five or more phenomenal. The prompt asks for dice added or taken off and the difficulty.
- **Willpower**: spend a point before the roll for one automatic success that 1s can't cancel (hold Shift while rolling, then lower the pool).
- **Specialty**: with a fitting specialty each 10 counts twice (hold Ctrl or switch Specialty on).
- Untrained: a Talent rolls the attribute alone, a Skill at +1 difficulty (the prompt starts at 7), a Knowledge not at all.
- **Wound penalties** come off every pool: Hurt and Injured -1, Wounded and Mauled -2, Crippled -5.
- Resisted rolls: both sides roll; the higher net successes win by the difference.

## Blood and Generation
Generation sets the **Blood Pool** (10 at 13th, 15 at 8th, up to 100), how much blood can be spent a turn, and the highest trait rating. Rising each night costs a blood point (the Rise at dusk button). A point of blood heals a level of bashing or lethal damage (Heal with blood), raises Strength, Dexterity or Stamina by one for the scene, or fuels a Discipline. Aggravated damage costs five blood and a day's rest a level. Below 7 minus Self-Control the vampire is **hungry** and frenzies more easily. Feeding takes a hunting roll (often Perception or Wits + Streetwise, Subterfuge or Animal Ken).

## The Beast
- **Frenzy**: roll Self-Control (or Instinct) against the trigger (3 smell of blood when hungry to 8 public humiliation; Brujah +2). Five successes overcome it; fewer hold it off a turn each; none means frenzy for the scene; a botch, a long frenzy.
- **Rötschreck**: Courage against fire and sunlight (3 a lit cigarette to 9 trapped in a burning building), read the same way; failure is panicked flight.
- **Degeneration**: after a sin against the Hierarchy, roll Conscience (or Conviction). Failure costs a dot of Humanity or Path, and maybe a derangement.
- Willpower spent holds the Beast back for a turn.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: Dexterity + Wits + 1d10 (less wound penalties), highest first. Declare in reverse order, act in order.
- **Attacks**: Dexterity + Brawl, Melee or Firearms (difficulty 6 or the weapon's). A bite needs a grapple first and does Strength + 1 aggravated.
- **Damage**: roll the weapon's dice (Strength + bonus in melee) plus one die for each attack success past the first, which the damage roll asks for. Each 6 or more is a health level; 1s don't cancel and damage can't botch. Potence adds its dots as automatic successes to Strength damage.
- **Soak**: vampires soak bashing and lethal with Stamina + Fortitude (+ armor), and halve the bashing that gets through; only Fortitude soaks aggravated (fire, sunlight, claws, fangs). Bullets do bashing to vampires, except head shots. Mortals soak only bashing, with Stamina.
- **Health**: seven levels from Bruised to Incapacitated. Past Incapacitated a vampire falls into torpor; aggravated damage past it is Final Death.

## Recovery
Willpower returns at the end of a story (End of story button) and when the character acts on the Nature. Blood heals wounds as above. Experience buys traits after each story.
