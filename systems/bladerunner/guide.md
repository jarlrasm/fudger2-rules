# Playing Blade Runner: the Roleplaying Game

## The game
Los Angeles, 2037. The players are Blade Runners of the LAPD's Rep-Detect unit, human or replicant, hunting rogue replicants and investigating cases in a city of rain, neon and corporate power. Every case asks what it means to be human.

## Rolling
Attributes and skills are rated A to D, which is a die: A d12, B d10, C d8, D d6. A roll is the **attribute die plus the skill die**:
- Each die showing **6 or more** is a success; **10 or more** on a d10 or d12 is two successes. One success is enough; more give extra effect.
- Each roll first asks **Roll with**: **advantage** (a key memory, a specialty, a good plan) adds a third die the size of the larger die and keeps the best two; **disadvantage** adds one the size of the smaller die and keeps the worst two.
- **Pushing**: reroll every die that shows no success, 1s included. Every 1 showing after the push is a point of damage (physical rolls) or stress (others). Humans push once; replicants can push a second time.

## Health and Resolve
- **Health** = (Strength + Agility die sizes) / 4, rounded up. Damage reduces it; at 0 the character is broken and may take a critical injury.
- **Resolve** = (Intelligence + Empathy die sizes) / 4, rounded up. Stress reduces it; at 0 they break mentally.
- Rest between shifts restores both (the *Sleep between shifts* button), but not critical injuries.

## Combat
- Deal **initiative cards** from 1 to 10 at the start; act in order.
- Hand to hand: Strength + Hand to Hand Combat. Shooting: Agility + Firearms. Range bands: Engaged, Short, Medium, Long, Extreme.
- A hit deals the weapon's **damage**, less the target's **armor rating**. With as many successes as the weapon's **Crit** value, add a critical injury.

## Points
**Promotion points** reward closing cases the department's way; **Humanity points** reward acting on one's humanity; **Chinyen** is money. Each can be spent between cases.
