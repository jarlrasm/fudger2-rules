# Playing Hunter: the Reckoning (5th edition)

## The game
Ordinary people have seen a monster, a vampire, a werewolf, a ghost, and can't go back to pretending. They form a **cell** and hunt, with no powers to speak of, using research, preparation, weapons and each other. The monsters are stronger; the hunters win by planning, and lose by getting desperate.

## Dice pools
Roll **attribute + skill** d10s. Each **6 or more** is a success; every **pair of 10s** adds two more (a critical). The GM sets a **Difficulty** (successes needed; leave it blank if only the GM knows). The same prompt asks for dice added or taken off for tools, circumstances or wounds. Beat the Difficulty and the extra successes are the **margin**.
- **Willpower**: after a roll, reroll up to three failed normal dice, marking one superficial Willpower damage.
- A **specialty** adds a die when it applies.

## Desperation and Danger
The cell shares two numbers, kept on each sheet:
- **Desperation** (1-5): when a roll serves the hunter's **Drive**, hold **Shift** while rolling (or switch **Add Desperation** on) and that many orange dice are added to the pool. They count successes as usual, but if any shows a **1**, the hunter **overreaches**: either **Danger** goes up by 1, or the hunter falls into **Despair**.
- **Danger** (0-5): how close the quarry is to hitting back. The GM spends it on complications.
- **Despair**: no Desperation dice and no use of the Drive until the hunter does what their **Redemption** asks.

Failed hunts and losses raise Desperation; it falls when the cell wins.

## Combat
Conflicts run in turns the GM orders. Attacks are contested: Strength + Brawl or Melee, Composure (or Dexterity) + Firearms, against the target's defense pool. The **margin plus the weapon's damage** is the damage. Mortal weapons do superficial damage to people (firearms and blades aggravated to mortals per the GM); monsters' claws and fangs are often aggravated, and many monsters halve or ignore some damage.

## Health and Willpower
**Health** = Stamina + 3 boxes, **Willpower** = Composure + Resolve, each with superficial and aggravated damage. A full health track leaves the hunter **impaired** (-2 dice to physical pools; a full willpower track gives -2 to social and mental ones, and the sheet takes both off); aggravated damage filling the health track means death. Superficial willpower heals each session (higher of Composure or Resolve); superficial health heals with rest, Stamina points per day (the *Day of rest* button), aggravated with weeks of care.

## Edges and Perks
**Edges** are the hunter's tools: **Assets** (gear, arsenal, library), **Aptitudes** (skills honed for the hunt) and **Endowments** (uncanny gifts: sensing, repelling or thwarting the unnatural). Each Edge has **Perks** that sharpen it.
