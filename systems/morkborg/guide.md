# Playing Mörk Borg

## Tests
Roll **d20 + ability** (Strength, Agility, Presence or Toughness) against a **difficulty rating (DR)**: 6 very simple, 8 simple, 10 routine, **12 normal**, 14 difficult, 16 really hard, 18 should not be possible. The sheet asks for the DR and fills in 12.

- A natural **20** is a crit and a natural **1** a fumble, whatever the DR.

## Combat
Players roll everything. Monsters do not roll to hit.

- **Attack:** melee is Strength, ranged is Presence, normally DR12. A hit deals the weapon's damage; a crit deals double and damages the target's armour.
- **Defend:** when a monster attacks, the player tests **Agility** (the Defence field), DR12. On a failure they take the monster's damage minus their armour. A fumble damages their armour.
- **Armour** reduces damage by a die: −d2 light, −d4 medium, −d6 heavy, and heavier armour raises Agility and Defence DRs.
- **Initiative:** roll d6 each round; 1–3 the enemies act first, 4–6 the players.
- **Morale:** when a monster's leader dies or it loses half its HP, roll 2d6 against Morale. Higher means it flees or surrenders.

## Damage and death
At 0 HP a scvm is **broken**: roll on the broken table. Below 0 they die.

## Omens and powers
**Omens** (d2 a day for most) let a player reroll any die, lower damage to 0, or make a monster reroll. **Powers** come from scrolls: roll Presence + d4 for uses per day, and each use is a Presence test DR12. A failure ends the uses and hurts.

## Style
The world is ending. Roll the Calendar of Nechrubel each day; when the last misery strikes, it is over. Be brutal and quick, and make every room a bad idea.
