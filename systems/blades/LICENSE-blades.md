# Blades in the Dark content

`blades.json` contains special abilities, crew abilities and entanglements from the *Blades in the Dark* System Reference Document by John Harper (One Seven Design), licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The text comes from https://github.com/amazingrando/blades-in-the-dark-srd-content and was converted with `tools/blades_import.py`, changing format and structure only.
