# Playing Root: The RPG

## The game
A great forest of animal folk is torn between factions: the industrious Marquisate, the proud Eyrie Dynasties, the rebel Woodland Alliance, and the Denizens caught in the middle. The players are **vagabonds**: outcasts and wanderers who take jobs, steal, fight and choose sides, clearing by clearing. It is Powered by the Apocalypse: the fiction leads, and dice decide only when a move says so.

## Moves
Roll **2d6 + stat** (Charm, Cunning, Finesse, Luck, Might; -1 to +3):
- **10+** strong hit: you do it.
- **7-9** weak hit: you do it, with a cost, a complication or a hard choice.
- **6-** miss: the GM makes a move, often harsh.
The roll asks for any **forward** (one-time) or **ongoing** bonus. Basic moves: Figure Someone Out, Persuade, Trick, Read a Tense Situation, Attempt a Roguish Feat, Trust Fate, Wreck Something, Meet Someone New. Each playbook adds its own.

## Fighting
Weapon moves: **Engage in a Melee** (Might), **Grapple an Enemy** (Might), **Target Someone** (Finesse). On a hit, trade harm with the enemy; **weapon skills** (Cleave, Parry, Quick Shot, Vicious Strike...) let you pick extra effects. Weapons have ranges: intimate, close, far.

## Harm
Three tracks, usually four boxes each: **Injury**, **Exhaustion** and **Depletion** (supplies). Items have **wear** boxes, marked to use some weapon skills or to absorb harm. A full injury or exhaustion track takes the vagabond **out** of the scene. Rest clears exhaustion (the sheet's button), resupplying clears depletion, and injury heals with care and time.

## Reputation
Each faction tracks the vagabond's **prestige** (help) and **notoriety** (harm). Enough of either moves their **reputation** from -3 to +3, which adds to dealings with that faction, or brings hunters.

## NPCs
NPCs and groups have injury, exhaustion, wear and **morale**; a broken group flees. Their attacks list the harm they deal, and using one on the battlemap rolls nothing. The GM never rolls; NPC actions happen as GM moves.
