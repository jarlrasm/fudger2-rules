# Playing Daggerheart

## Duality dice
An action roll is **two d12s, Hope (yellow) and Fear (purple), plus a trait** (and an Experience's +2 if one applies). Each roll asks for the Difficulty; meet or beat it to succeed. Leave it blank when only the GM knows it, and the roll shows just the total.

- If the Hope die is higher, the roll is **with Hope**: the player gains a Hope.
- If the Fear die is higher, it is **with Fear**: the GM gains a Fear and the spotlight can swing to them.
- Doubles are a **critical success**: it succeeds whatever the total, the player gains a Hope and clears a Stress.
- **Advantage** adds a d6, **disadvantage** subtracts one (Shift and Ctrl).
- Spend Hope to use an Experience, help an ally (they add a d6), or trigger Hope features.

## The spotlight
There is no initiative. The spotlight moves to whoever the fiction points at. It moves to the GM when a player rolls with Fear, fails, or hands it over; the GM spends Fear to take extra moves or use adversary features.

## Damage
Attacks roll the weapon trait against the adversary's Difficulty (adversaries roll d20 + ATK against the player's Evasion; their ATK roll asks for it, and a natural 20 is a critical hit). Damage is the weapon die times Proficiency, plus modifiers.

- Compare damage to the **thresholds**: below Major marks 1 HP, Major marks 2, Severe marks 3.
- **Armor slots** can each reduce the HP marked by one.
- Stress is marked for strain and some features. With no Stress left to mark, mark HP instead.
- When the last HP is marked, the character makes a **death move**: blaze of glory, avoid death (with a scar), or risk it all.

## Rest
A short rest lets each character take two downtime moves (clear some HP, Stress or armor, or gain Hope). A long rest clears more and allows projects. Use Countdowns for clocks of looming events.

## Style
Heroic, collaborative fantasy. Ask the players questions about the world, and make Fear an honest source of drama rather than punishment.
