# Playing Draw Steel

## Power rolls
Every test and ability uses a **power roll**: **2d10 + a characteristic** (Might, Agility, Reason, Intuition or Presence, from −5 to +5). The total gives a tier:

- **Tier 1**: 11 or less
- **Tier 2**: 12 to 16
- **Tier 3**: 17 or more

A natural **19 or 20** on the dice is a **critical**: always tier 3, and on an ability roll the hero gets an extra main action.

**Edges and banes**: one edge adds +2, one bane −2. A **double edge** moves the result up a tier instead, a double bane down a tier. An edge and a bane cancel. Set the Roll slider on the sheet (Shift for an edge, Ctrl for a bane).

**Tests** outside combat use the same tiers: the GM says what each tier means. A skill the hero has adds +2.

Abilities never miss outright: each tier has its own effect, written on the ability. Fudger shows the effect of the tier reached.

## Combat
- **Turn order**: no initiative. Roll a d10: on 6 or more the heroes choose a hero to go first; otherwise the GM picks a monster. Sides then alternate, each picking who acts next.
- Each turn: a **main action**, a **maneuver** and a **move action**.
- **Free strikes** hit creatures that move away without disengaging.
- **Heroic resources** (Wrath, Piety, Essence, Ferocity, Discipline, Insight, Focus, Clarity, Drama) rise each turn and pay for heroic abilities. **Surges** add damage or extend potencies. **Victories** count encounters won since the last respite.

## Stamina
At half stamina or less a hero is **winded**. At 0 they are **dying** (can still act, but bleed), and at minus their winded value they die. Spending a **Recovery** as a main action regains a third of max stamina. A respite restores recoveries and stamina.

## Monsters
Monsters have a level, organization (minion to solo), role and **EV** (encounter value) for budgeting. Their abilities list a fixed power-roll bonus and three tiers like heroes'. Minions share a stamina pool.
