# Playing Black Crusade

## The game
The Warhammer 40,000 far future, seen from the other side. The players are **heretics** in the Screaming Vortex and the Calixis Sector's shadow: humans who have turned from the Emperor (Apostate, Heretek, Renegade, Psyker) and **Chaos Space Marines** of the traitor legions (Chosen, Forsaken, Sorcerer, Champion). They gather followers, scheme against each other and their rivals, and seek the favour of the Dark Gods: **Khorne** (blood and war), **Nurgle** (decay and endurance), **Slaanesh** (excess and perfection) and **Tzeentch** (change and sorcery). The end of every heretic is to be claimed: as a **Daemon Prince**, or as a mindless **Chaos Spawn**.

## Tests
Roll **d100** and get the **target** or less. The target is a characteristic (or a skill) plus the difficulty:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Trivial | +60 |
| Easy | +30 |
| Routine | +20 |
| Ordinary | +10 |
| Challenging | +0 |
| Difficult | -10 |
| Hard | -20 |
| Very Hard | -30 |
| Hellish | -60 |

01 always succeeds and 100 always fails. A success is **one degree of success**, plus one for every step the target's tens digit is above the roll's tens digit (45 against 23 is 3 degrees); a failure counts **degrees of failure** the same way. **Untrained** skills test at -20, and Forbidden Lore can't be tried untrained. Skill ranks: Known (the characteristic), Trained +10, Experienced +20, Veteran +30. Each level of **fatigue** gives -10 to tests; more fatigue than the Toughness bonus and the heretic passes out.

A characteristic's **bonus** is its tens digit, multiplied by its **Unnatural** multiplier. Chaos Space Marines have Unnatural Strength and Toughness (x2), and power armour adds to Strength first. Strength bonus adds to melee damage, Toughness bonus soaks damage, Agility bonus gives initiative and movement.

## Infamy
Infamy takes the place of Fate. The **Infamy** characteristic is the heretic's renown among the servants of Chaos; it rises when compacts succeed, and can be bought with XP (+5 for 500 XP, up to 40). The **Infamy bonus** is the number of **Infamy points** each session (*New session*). Spend one to reroll a test (a failed test offers *Spend Infamy: reroll*), add a degree of success, heal 1d5 wounds, shake off fatigue or Stunning, or act first. Burning Infamy cheats death. Getting hold of gear is an **Acquisition**: an Infamy test that asks the item's Availability (Ubiquitous +70 to Unique -70) and Craftsmanship (Poor +10, Good -10, Best -20).

## Alignment
Every advance (characteristic, skill, talent, power) is aligned to a god or to none. Strength is Khorne's, Toughness Nurgle's, Fellowship Slaanesh's and Willpower Tzeentch's; the other characteristics and Infamy are unaligned. Note each advance bought under its god in **Advances by god** (archetype advances don't count). Whenever Corruption reaches a new multiple of 10, press **Check alignment**: a god whose advances lead every other god's by 5 or more claims the heretic, otherwise the heretic is Unaligned.

Each god's advances are then **True** (the heretic's own god), **Allied** or **Opposed** (Khorne and Nurgle are allies, as are Slaanesh and Tzeentch; for an Unaligned heretic everything is Allied). Costs in XP:

| | Characteristic (4 advances) | Skill (Known to Veteran) | Talent (tier 1 to 3) |
|---|---|---|---|
| True | 100 / 250 / 500 / 750 | 100 / 200 / 400 / 600 | 200 / 300 / 400 |
| Allied | 250 / 500 / 750 / 1000 | 200 / 350 / 500 / 750 | 250 / 500 / 750 |
| Opposed | 500 / 750 / 1000 / 2500 | 250 / 500 / 750 / 1000 | 500 / 750 / 1000 |

A heretic Aligned to Khorne cannot use psychic powers.

## Corruption and the gifts of the gods
Deeds, the Warp and daemons give **Corruption**. At 10 Corruption, and every 20 after for humans (every 30 for Chaos Space Marines), the gods grant a **Gift of the Gods**: a roll on the gifts table, often a mutation. An Aligned heretic can make an Infamy test to receive the god's reward instead. Keep gifts, mutations and marks in the **Gifts** list. At **100 Corruption** the heretic is claimed: if Infamy has reached the gamemaster's target (*Daemon Prince at*), the heretic becomes a Daemon Prince; otherwise a Chaos Spawn. Either way the character leaves play.

## Compacts
Each endeavour of the warband is a **Compact**: one heretic is the **Anointed** who leads it, it may be dedicated to a god, and it has objectives (primary, secondary, and tertiary ones that serve a single heretic). Each heretic puts in resources. Completed objectives earn Infamy and XP, which the gamemaster sets; the Compact sheet adds up what is done.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: 1d10 + Agility bonus, highest first. A turn is a full action or two half actions; movement is the Agility bonus in metres for a half move, double for a full move, triple to charge and six times to run.
- **Attacks**: Weapon Skill in melee, Ballistic Skill at range. The attack asks the kind of attack and the difficulty (range, cover, size). Melee: standard +10, all-out +30, charge +20, guarded -10, swift +0 (an extra hit per two degrees beyond the first), lightning -10 (an extra hit per degree beyond the first), called shot -20. Ranged: standard +10, semi-auto +0 (an extra hit per two degrees beyond the first), full auto -10 (an extra hit per degree beyond the first), both up to the rate of fire, called shot -20. Ranged weapons **jam** on 96 or more (94 for bursts).
- **Hit location**: the attack roll's digits reversed: 01-10 head, 11-20 right arm, 21-30 left arm, 31-70 body, 71-85 right leg, 86-00 left leg.
- **Damage**: the weapon's damage (plus Strength bonus in melee). The damage roll asks the target's armour on that location (less the weapon's Penetration) and Toughness bonus, and takes them off. Tearing weapons roll an extra die and drop the lowest.
- **Zealous Hatred**: a natural 10 on a damage die. Roll 1d5 (the roll window's button) and apply that result from the critical table for the location and damage type, even if no damage got through.
- Damage past 0 wounds is **critical damage**: read the critical effects by location and damage type. Targets may **Dodge** or **Parry**.

## Hordes
Masses of cultists, mutants and guardsmen are **Hordes**. A horde has **Magnitude** instead of Wounds. Each hit that does any damage takes 1 Magnitude; the attack result counts the hits, adding a weapon's Blast value; Zealous Hatred takes 1 more. A horde's attacks add 1d10 damage per 10 Magnitude, up to 2d10 (the sheet adds them). Once it has lost a quarter of its Magnitude, a horde that is not Fearless tests Willpower or breaks.

## Healing
*Night of rest* clears fatigue and heals 1 wound for a lightly damaged heretic (damage no more than twice the Toughness bonus). Heavier wounds need Medicae; critical damage must be treated first.

## Psykers and sorcerers
A psyker uses a power with a **Focus Power test**: Willpower plus 5 per psy rating used, against the power's difficulty. The test asks how to manifest it:
- **Fettered**: half the psy rating (rounded up), and no Psychic Phenomena.
- **Unfettered**: the full psy rating; doubles (11, 22 ... 99) bring Psychic Phenomena.
- **Push**: the rating plus 1 or more (most psykers up to +3), and Psychic Phenomena always.

Every power sustained beyond the first gives -10. When Phenomena come, the roll window's button rolls d100 on the **Psychic Phenomena** table; 75 or more is **Perils of the Warp**, rolled the same way.
