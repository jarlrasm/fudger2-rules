# Playing Into the Odd (Remastered)

## The game
Into the Odd is about explorers in an industrial age of smoke, steam and weird science. They are greedy, desperate people who go into the Underground beneath the city, and into the wilds beyond it, for Arcana: strange devices with powers nobody understands. There are no classes or skills. Players describe what they do; the Referee says what happens. Danger should be clear before it strikes, and smart play beats dice.

## Saves
Most actions just happen. Call for a **save** only when there is a risk: **d20 equal to or under STR, DEX or WIL** (the Save column). A 1 always succeeds, a 20 always fails. The side facing the consequence rolls.
- STR: muscle, endurance, poison. DEX: speed, stealth, aim. WIL: nerve, charm, resisting strange forces.

## Turns
Players act before enemies. When the characters might be surprised, use Call for initiative: each rolls a DEX save; those who fail act after the enemy in the first round (the tracker puts passed characters first, enemies next, surprised characters last). Otherwise just let the players go first, in any order. On a turn a character moves and does one thing.

## Attacks and damage
- **Attacks always hit.** Roll the weapon die and subtract the target's **Armour** (0 to 3). The roll asks how the attack is made and the target's Armour.
- **Impaired** (through cover, while grappled, blinded): d4. **Enhanced** (a helpless target, after a daring stunt): d12. **Two weapons**: roll both, keep the best. Several attackers on one target do the same: roll all, keep the highest.
- **Blast** weapons hit everything in the area; roll for each target.
- Damage comes off **HP** first. HP is how well you avoid harm, not health. Past 0 HP, the rest comes off **STR**, and the target saves STR (with the new score) or takes **Critical Damage**: it cannot act until an ally tends it and it takes a short rest, and dies within the hour if left alone. Monsters with Critical Damage are dead or out.
- STR 0 is death, DEX 0 paralysis, WIL 0 a broken mind until a full rest.

## Rest
- **Short rest** (a few minutes and a drink): all HP back. **Full rest** (a week of downtime somewhere comfortable): all ability scores back. A **deprived** character (no food, water or warmth) gets nothing from rest. The sheet's buttons do both.
- Three or more **Bulky** items drop a character to 0 HP.

## Arcana
Using an Arcanum is an action. Each does one strange thing; some have limited uses or ask for a price. Make up new ones freely and keep them odd.

## Enemies
Monsters have HP, Armour, STR/DEX/WIL and an attack die. **Morale**: a group saves WIL when it has lost half its number (a lone enemy at 0 HP); fail and it flees. **Detachments** are twenty or more fighting as one: their attacks on individuals are enhanced, individuals' attacks on them impaired unless Blast. First reactions: the character in front saves WIL to avoid a bad one.

## Advancement
After surviving expeditions a character rises in level. Use the Level up roll: a d20 for each ability, +1 where it beats the score, and a d6 more max HP.

## Other rolls
A **luck roll** is a d6: high favours the players. Falls and blasts deal d4 to d12, d20 only for something extraordinary.
