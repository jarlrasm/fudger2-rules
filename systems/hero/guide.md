# Playing Hero System 6th edition

## The game
The Hero System is a toolkit: characters, powers and gear are all built from points, so it can run any genre. *Champions* is its superhero setting. A character sheet holds characteristics, skills, powers bought with modifiers, and complications that give points back.

## Rolling
- **Skill and characteristic rolls**: 3d6 at or under the target. A characteristic roll is 9 + CHAR/5; most skills use that plus levels bought. Familiarities and flat skills start at 8-. The result says how much the roll was made or missed by.
- **Attack rolls**: 3d6. An attack hits a target whose DCV is **11 + OCV - roll** or less. The result names the highest DCV hit, so nobody needs to ask the target's DCV first. 3 always hits, 18 always misses. Mental attacks use OMCV against DMCV.

## Speed and phases
A turn has 12 segments. SPD decides in which segments a character acts (the *Phases* field). In each segment, characters with a phase act from highest DEX down. After segment 12 everyone takes a free Recovery.

## Damage
- **Normal damage** (most punches and blasts): the dice total is STUN. Each die is also 0 BODY on a 1, 1 on 2 to 5, and 2 on a 6.
- **Killing damage** (blades, bullets, claws, marked *K*): the total is BODY; STUN is BODY times 1d3.
- Subtract the matching defense from each: PD for physical, ED for energy. Against killing damage only **resistant** defenses (rPD, rED) stop BODY.
- Losing more STUN than CON in one hit **stuns**: the character loses their next phase. At 0 STUN they are knocked out; below 0 BODY they are dying, and at negative BODY equal to their starting BODY they are dead.
- A **Recovery** (spending a phase, or after segment 12) regains REC in STUN and END. The sheet's *Recovery* and *Full rest* buttons do that.

## Endurance
Powers and STR cost END: 1 per 10 active points used, unless bought down.

## Range
A ranged attack reaches 10 m per active point. The battlemap uses that from each power's active points.
