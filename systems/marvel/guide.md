# Playing the Marvel Multiverse Role-Playing Game

## The d616
Every check rolls three six-sided dice: two normal dice and the red **Marvel die**, and adds an ability score. On the Marvel die the 1 is the Marvel logo (**M**) and counts as 6.

- Meet or beat the **target number** (TN) or the opponent's **defense** to succeed.
- A success with **M** on the Marvel die is a **Fantastic success**: something extra happens, and attacks deal double damage.
- 6, 6 and M is an **Ultimate Fantastic success**.
- **Edge** means rolling an extra die and keeping the better; **trouble**, the worse. Set Edge or Trouble on the sheet (hold Shift or Ctrl while rolling): Fudger rolls a third normal die as the reroll. With an edge it replaces the lowest die if it is better, with trouble the highest die if it is worse. When that die is the Marvel die, the reroll counts as a Marvel die (1 is M), so an edge can bring an M and trouble can take one away. Edges and troubles cancel each other.

## Abilities
**M**elee, **A**gility, **R**esilience, **V**igilance, **E**go, **L**ogic. Each has a defense (score + 10) that others roll against. Melee and Agility attacks are physical, Ego and Logic attacks are mental or psychic.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: a d616 + Vigilance; act from highest to lowest.
- Distances are in spaces; Fudger counts a space as 1 m. Melee attacks reach the next space, and Agility, Ego and Logic attacks reach 20 spaces unless the power says otherwise.
- Each turn: one standard action, one movement action and a reaction.
- **Damage** = Marvel die × the attacker's rank + the attack's ability score, doubled on a Fantastic success. Physical damage (Melee, Agility) reduces **Health**, mental (Ego, Logic) reduces **Focus**, each after damage reduction.
- At 0 Health a character is knocked out; at 0 Focus demoralized. Deep negative Health means dying.

## Karma
Each character has Karma equal to their rank. Spend one to gain an edge on a roll. Karma recovers each session and on Fantastic initiative.

## Powers
Powers come from power sets and list their action, cost (often Focus) and effect. Traits and tags shape what a hero can do outside combat.
