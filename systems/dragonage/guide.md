# Dragon Age RPG

## Ability tests
Roll 3d6, one of them the **Dragon die** (a different colour), and add the ability (−2 to 4 or more at the start) plus 2 if a focus applies. Meet or beat the target number: 7 routine, 9 easy, 11 average, 13 challenging, 15 hard, 17 formidable. In an opposed test both roll and the higher total wins. On a successful test, any doubles among the three dice give **stunt points** equal to the Dragon die, spent on stunts. The Dragon die also measures how well a success went.

## Combat
Initiative is a Dexterity test (less the armor penalty) at the start of combat; the order stays the same each round. On a turn a character has a major and a minor action, or two minor actions.

An attack is a test against the target's **Defense** (10 + Dexterity + shield). Melee weapons use Strength (axes, bludgeons, heavy blades, polearms, spears) or Dexterity (brawling, light blades, staves); bows use Dexterity. A hit deals the weapon's dice plus Strength (melee) or Perception (ranged), minus the target's **armor rating**. Worn armor's **armor penalty** comes off Speed and every Dexterity test, Dexterity attacks and Initiative included. Weapon groups not trained give −2 to hit and half damage. Doubles on a hit give stunt points: Lightning Attack (an extra attack), Pierce Armor, Knock Prone, Mighty Blow (+1d6 damage) and so on.

## Health and rest
At 0 Health a character is dying and dies after Constitution rounds (minimum 1) unless helped. After a fight a **breather** heals 5 + Constitution + level. A night's rest restores Health and mana.

## Magic
Mages have Mana and cast spells by spending its cost and making a casting test (Magic, plus 2 for the lore focus of the spell's school, such as Spirit Lore for a Spirit spell) against the spell's target number. Victims resist with a test against the mage's **spellpower** (10 + Magic + 2 for that school's lore focus). The sheet's Spell Rolls work both out per spell. Spell stunts come from doubles on the casting test. Mages risk the Fade: watch for blood magic and possession.

## NPCs
Adversaries have the same eight abilities, Health, Defense, armor rating and Speed, with attacks written as a bonus and damage, and powers. Their threat runs from minor to legendary.
