# Playing Cairn (2nd edition)

## The game
Cairn is adventure in a dark, fairy-tale wood full of old ruins, strange folk and stranger magic. Characters are ordinary people with a background and some gear. There are no classes or skill lists: players describe what they do, and the Warden says what happens. Danger is real and telegraphed; clever play matters more than dice.

## Saves
Roll a **save** only when a choice is risky: **d20 equal to or under STR, DEX or WIL**. A 1 always succeeds, a 20 always fails. When two sides oppose, whoever is most at risk saves.

## Hit Protection and damage
- **HP** is how well you avoid harm, not health. A few moments' rest restores it (the Short rest button), unless you are **deprived**.
- **Attacks always hit**: roll the weapon die and take off the target's **Armor** (never more than 3). The roll asks whether the attack is **impaired** (d4: through cover, bound hands) or **enhanced** (d12: helpless target, daring move), and the target's Armor.
- Several attackers on one target roll all their dice and keep the highest.
- Damage past 0 HP comes off **STR**, and the target makes a **STR save** against **critical damage**. A PC who fails can only crawl and dies within the hour without aid; an NPC dies.
- Damage that leaves a PC at **exactly 0 HP** gives a **scar** by the HP lost: a lasting mark, and often a boost to max HP or an attribute.
- STR 0 is death, DEX 0 paralysis, WIL 0 delirium.

## Combat order
Player characters act first. In the **first round** each must pass a **DEX save** to act. Then the enemies, and so on. Enemies check **morale** (a WIL save) at their first casualty and at half their number.

## Inventory and Fatigue
**Ten slots**: four on the body and in the hands, six in the backpack. Bulky items take two, petty ones none. A full inventory drops you to 0 HP. **Fatigue** fills a slot until a full night's rest (Full rest button). Being **deprived** for more than a day adds Fatigue each day. A week's rest with a healer restores lost attributes.

## Magic
**Spellbooks** hold one spell each. Read one aloud with both hands and add a Fatigue. Given time and safety the effect can be enhanced; under pressure the Warden may ask for a WIL save. **Scrolls** are single-use and cause no Fatigue. **Relics** have charges and a recharge condition.
