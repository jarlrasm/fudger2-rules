# Unknown Armies 3rd edition: play guide

Unknown Armies is horror in the occult underground of the modern world. The player characters are a cabal of obsessed people trying to force a change on the world, and the cost is their minds. Play around the cabal's objective: what they want, who stands in the way, and what they will do to get it.

## Rolls

- Roll d100 at or under an ability, an identity or a relationship. The roll shows the tens and units dice.
- Doubles at or under the target (11, 22, 33...) are a **matched success**: the best kind of success. Doubles over it are a **matched failure**: something goes wrong.
- **01** is a crit and **00** a fumble, whatever the target.
- **Flip-flop**: swap the two dice, so 82 becomes 28. Only allowed when a passion, a feature or magick says so. The roll window offers the Flip-flop button; use it only when it is allowed. Tick the passion's Used box when a passion gave it.
- Easy tasks need no roll when the character has a fitting identity or 15% or more and no one is pushing back. Use the Modifier slider for aiming (+10% or +20%), called shots (-20% or -30%) and other situations.

## Abilities, identities and relationships

- Ten abilities sit on the five shock meters, two each: Fitness and Dodge (Helplessness), Status and Pursuit (Isolation), Knowledge and Lie (Self), Notice and Secrecy (Unnatural), Connect and Struggle (Violence). Each hardened notch on a meter takes 5% from the upper ability and gives it to the lower one: 0 notches give 60% and 20%.
- Identities ("I'm a cop, of course I can make an arrest") have a percentage. An identity can stand in for one ability, and features such as Uses firearms, Provides initiative or Provides wound threshold.
- Relationships (Favorite, Guru, Mentor, Responsibility, Protégé) are rolled instead of their linked ability when dealing with that person.

## Shock

- A shock has a meter and a rank from 1 to 10. Roll the meter's Stress check, which rolls the defending ability (Status against Helplessness, Connect against Isolation, Notice against Self, Knowledge against the Unnatural, Fitness against Violence) and asks the rank.
- A rank at or below the meter's hardened notches needs no check: the character is used to it.
- Success: the character holds and marks a hardened notch. Failure: mark a failed notch, and the character fights, flees or freezes, as the player chooses, for the scene.
- Coercion is an attack on a meter: Connect for Helplessness, Status for Isolation, Knowledge for Self, Secrecy for the Unnatural, Struggle for Violence. A success makes the target give in or take a rank 1 check (rank 2 on a matched success or crit), +1 for each passion in play on either side.

## Fights

- Act from the highest Initiative down: Dodge, or an identity that substitutes for Dodge or provides initiative.
- Attacks ask whether the target is dodging: -10% if the target's Dodge is lower than the attacker's skill, -20% if it is as high or higher.
- Fists and feet: wounds equal to the two dice added (a 0 is 10). Crit: the target is out or dead. Matched failure: -10% next roll.
- Melee weapons: the dice added, +3 each for big, heavy or cleaving weapons (cleaving deals 1 wound on a miss). Matched success: wounds equal to the roll. Crit: the target dies. Matched failure: disarmed. Fumble: 20 wounds to yourself.
- Guns need an identity that uses firearms; without one, only suppressive fire with Struggle. A hit deals wounds equal to the roll, up to the gun's damage cap; a crit the full cap; a fumble jams the gun. Full auto removes the cap: see the book.
- Wounds count up against the wound threshold (50, or an identity's): sore at half, badly hurt at three quarters, unconscious at nine tenths, dead at the threshold. Seeing or doing violence is a Violence shock.

## Magick

- Adepts gain minor, significant and major charges by acting out their school's paradigm and lose them when they break a taboo; spells spend charges. Track them on the sheet; library Spells and Schools hold your own write-ups.
- Avatars channel an archetype: the archetype identity's percentage opens channels at 1-50%, 51-70%, 71-90% and 91%+. Breaking a taboo costs percentage.
- Rituals and artifacts work for anyone who follows the steps or holds the object.

## The cabal

Make a Cabal sheet for the group's objective: its scale (local, weighty, cosmic), path of milestones and closure. Progress goes up in percentage points as the cabal achieves milestones. At 100% the objective happens. The cabal can push to finish early by rolling under the progress, but a failure loses it all.

## Sheet fields that matter

Passions (Rage, Noble, Fear), the shock meters with their hardened and failed notches, identities, wounds against the threshold, and the adept's charges or the avatar's percentage. The New session button clears the passions' Used marks.
