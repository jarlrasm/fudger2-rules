# Playing Sentinel Comics: the Roleplaying Game

## Traits and dice
Heroes have **powers** (what people can't do) and **qualities** (skills, knowledge, nature), each a die from d4 to d12. A d4 stands in when nothing fits. Each hero also has three **status dice**, one for each zone (Green, Yellow, Red), set by the personality.

## Rolling
Every roll is three dice: **one power, one quality and the status die** of the current zone. Sort them into **Min, Mid and Max**. The action says which one is the **effect**: basic actions use the **Mid** die; abilities may use Min or Max, or more than one die for several effects. Add the boosts and hinders that apply.

On the sheet, set Action, Effect die and Paired die (the other trait) in the Dice pool, then roll a power or quality. Ability rows roll their own power, quality, action and die. The roll asks for the total of boosts and hinders and shows all three dice.

## Actions
- **Overcome** a challenge: 0 or less fails; 1 to 3 fails or succeeds with a **major twist**; 4 to 7 succeeds with a **minor twist**; 8 to 11 succeeds; 12 or more succeeds beyond expectations.
- **Attack**: the effect is damage. Attacks never miss.
- **Defend**: the effect is taken off each attack against you until your next turn (the sheet's Defense).
- **Boost** or **Hinder**: a bonus or penalty of 1 (effect 1-3), 2 (4-7), 3 (8-11) or 4 (12+) on a later roll. It is used up by one roll unless the ability makes it persistent.
- **Recover**: regain the effect in health, where an ability allows it.

## Health and zones
Health falls through the **Green**, **Yellow** and **Red** ranges to **Out** at 0. The hero's zone is the worse of the health zone and the **scene tracker's** zone. It picks the status die and unlocks abilities: Yellow abilities from Yellow on, Red abilities only in Red. An Out hero may only use the **Out ability**. Health comes back only through abilities or a scene spent recovering (the Recovered button).

## Principles, twists and hero points
Each hero has two **principles** with a Green ability for overcoming, and minor and major twists. Taking a twist (to turn a failure into a success with a complication, or because a principle calls for it) earns a **hero point**. Spend hero points on rerolls, +1 bonuses and the like.

## Opposition
- **Minions** are one die, d4 to d12, rolled for any action. Attacked, they roll the die as a save against the damage: lower and the minion is gone; equal or higher and the die shrinks a size (a d4 is then gone).
- **Lieutenants** are one die too, but tougher: a failed save shrinks the die, a made save does nothing, and damage of twice the die size or more defeats them outright.
- **Villains** are built like heroes, with health, zones, powers, qualities and abilities by zone, and may bring minion forms.
- The **environment** takes a turn each round with the die of the scene's zone and its actions by zone.

## The scene
The **scene tracker** has Green, Yellow and Red boxes; tick one (Next round) after everyone has acted. When the last box is ticked, time is up and the environment's consequence happens. Challenges have boxes ticked by successful Overcomes.

## Turn order
No initiative roll: whoever acts picks who goes next, hero or villain, and the environment acts once per round.
