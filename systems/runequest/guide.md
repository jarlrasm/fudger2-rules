# Playing RuneQuest: Roleplaying in Glorantha

## The game
Glorantha is a bronze-age world of myth where gods and Runes are real. The adventurers belong to clans, tribes and cults, and their Runes and passions shape who they are as much as their skills. Magic is everywhere: everyone knows some spirit magic, and initiates of a cult can call on Rune magic.

## Rolls
Roll **d100 at or under** the skill, Rune or passion:
- **Critical success**: a twentieth of the chance or less.
- **Special success**: a fifth of the chance or less.
- **Success**: at or under the chance. 01-05 always succeeds.
- **Failure**: over it. 96-00 always fails.
- **Fumble**: the top end of the failure range (00 always).

Skills get a **category modifier** (Agility, Communication, Knowledge, Magic, Manipulation, Perception, Stealth) from the characteristics. **Runes** and **passions** can be rolled to act as the Rune would, and a success **augments** another roll.

**Resistance rolls** (such as POW against POW): 50% plus 5% for each point the active characteristic beats the passive one.

## Combat
- Melee rounds are split into **strike ranks** 1 to 12. Each fighter's SR is DEX SR + SIZ SR + weapon SR; lowest acts first. Spells add a strike rank per magic point.
- An attack rolls the weapon skill; the defender may parry or dodge. A **special** success impales, slashes or crushes for extra damage; a **critical** does maximum special damage and ignores armor.
- Roll **d20 for hit location**: 1-4 right leg, 5-8 left leg, 9-11 abdomen, 12 chest, 13-15 right arm, 16-18 left arm, 19-20 head. Armor on the location absorbs damage; the rest comes off the location and the total hit points.
- A limb at 0 hit points is useless; at twice its points it is severed or maimed. Head, chest or abdomen at 0 and the adventurer is down. Total hit points at 0 or less means death without healing magic.

## Magic
- **Magic points** equal POW and come back over a day.
- **Spirit magic** costs magic points. **Rune magic** spends Rune points, regained by worship at the cult's holy day.
- **Spirit combat** pits Spirit Combat skill against spirits, who attack magic points.

## Healing
Healing magic is common. Without it, each wounded location heals by the **healing rate** (from CON) each week.

## Experience
Successful skills get an experience check; in **Sacred Time**, between seasons, roll over the skill to improve it.
