# Playing the Cosmere RPG

## Tests
Roll **d20 + skill modifier** (ranks + attribute) against a **DC** set by the GM (5 easy, 10 average, 15 hard, 20 very hard) or a target's **defense**.

- **Advantage**: roll two d20 and keep the better; **disadvantage**, the worse. Set it on the sheet (Shift or Ctrl while rolling).
- A natural 20 brings an **opportunity**, a natural 1 a **complication**.
- **Raising the stakes**: when the GM says so, a **plot die** rolls along (set Stakes to Raised, or hold Alt). **C+2** and **C+4** add 2 or 4 to the roll but bring a complication; **O** is an opportunity; blank faces do nothing.
- Opportunities give the player a benefit (an extra effect, a clue); complications let the GM add a twist.

## Defenses
- **Physical** = 10 + Strength + Speed
- **Cognitive** = 10 + Intellect + Willpower
- **Spiritual** = 10 + Awareness + Presence

Attacks and many talents target one of them.

## Rounds
No initiative roll. Each round every hero picks a **fast turn** (2 actions, before the adversaries' fast turns) or a **slow turn** (3 actions, after them). Adversaries choose the same way. Reactions cost Focus.

## Damage
A hit deals the weapon's damage plus the attribute (Strength for heavy weapons, Speed for light), minus the target's **deflect** (armor). At 0 health a character is unconscious and gains an **injury**. Rest restores health with the recovery die. **Focus** (2 + Willpower) powers reactions and many talents; **Investiture** fuels surges for Radiants.

## Adversaries
Adversaries are minions, rivals or bosses with fixed defenses, skills and attacks. Minions fall at the first hit that damages them.
