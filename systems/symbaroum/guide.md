# Playing Symbaroum

## Tests
Characters have eight attributes from 5 to 15: **Accurate, Cunning, Discreet, Persuasive, Quick, Resolute, Strong, Vigilant**. A test rolls **d20 equal to or under** the attribute.

When someone opposes the test, their attribute modifies it: the target number changes by **10 − the opposing attribute**. Opposed by Vigilant 13, a Discreet 12 sneak needs 9 or less. Fudger asks for the opposing value (10 when unopposed). A **1** is a critical success, a **20** a fumble.

**Players roll everything.** The GM never rolls for enemies: players roll their attacks against the enemy's Defense, and roll their own Defense (Quick) against the enemy's Accurate.

## Combat
- **Initiative** is Quick, ties broken by Vigilant. No roll.
- An attack hits on a successful Accurate test against the target's Defense. **Damage** is the weapon's die (plus bonuses) minus the target's **armor** die.
- Damage reduces **Toughness** (Strong, at least 10). Damage higher than the **pain threshold** (half Strong) lets the attacker knock the target down or impose another effect.
- At 0 Toughness a character is **dying**: they make a death test each turn: a d20 of 10 or less is a success, higher a failure. Three successes and they stabilise, three failures and they die.
- Armor's **impeding** value lowers Defense and Discreet tests.

## Corruption
Mystical powers, artifacts and dark places give **corruption**. Temporary corruption fades after the scene; permanent corruption stays. At the **corruption threshold** (half Resolute) a character becomes blight-marked; at corruption equal to Resolute they become an **abomination** and are lost.

## Abilities
Abilities, mystical powers and rituals have three levels: **Novice, Adept, Master**, each adding to the ones before.
