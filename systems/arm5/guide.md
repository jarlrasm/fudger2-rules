# Playing Ars Magica 5th edition

## The game
Mythic Europe, 1220: the medieval world as its people believed it, with faeries in the forests, demons at the crossroads and angels in the churches. The players are **magi** of the Order of Hermes, wizards bound by a code, who live together in a **covenant**. Each player usually also has a **companion** (a knight, a scholar, a priest) and shares the covenant's **grogs** (guards and servants). Stories alternate with seasons of study, lab work and politics.

## Rolling
A roll is **characteristic + ability + die** against an **ease factor** (6 is easy, 9 average, 12 hard, 15 very hard).
- A **simple die** is a d10, where 0 counts as 10.
- Under stress, use a **stress die**: on a **1**, roll again and double (and again, doubling each time); on a **0** the die counts as zero and the character risks a **botch**. Each roll first asks how many **botch dice**; the push button then rolls them. Any 0 on them is a botch, and more zeros make it worse.
- Fatigue and wounds give a penalty that the sheet takes off every total.

## Magic
- Fifteen Arts: five **Techniques** (Creo, Intellego, Muto, Perdo, Rego) and ten **Forms** (Animal, Aquam, Auram, Corpus, Herbam, Ignem, Imaginem, Mentem, Terram, Vim). A spell names one of each, such as CrIg for creating fire.
- **Formulaic spells**: casting total = Technique + Form + Stamina + aura + stress die. Reaching the spell's level casts it; falling short by 10 or less still casts it, at the cost of a fatigue level. The *Formulaic spells* rows roll this and say which.
- **Spontaneous spells**: Technique + Form + Stamina + aura, halved with a stress die (costs a fatigue level), or divided by five without one.
- **Ritual spells** take time and vis. **Requisites** use the lower of two Arts.
- **Parma Magica** x5 is the magus's magic resistance, plus the Form of the attacking spell.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: Quickness + weapon Init - Encumbrance + simple die.
- **Attack** (Dexterity + weapon ability + Atk) and **Defense** (Quickness + ability + Dfn) both roll stress dice. If the attack is higher, the difference is the attack advantage, added to **damage** (Strength + Dam).
- Damage minus **soak** (Stamina + armor) becomes a wound, by size: 1-5 light (-1), 6-10 medium (-3), 11-15 heavy (-5), 16-20 incapacitating, 21+ dead (for size 0).
- Fatigue goes from Fresh through Winded, Weary (-1), Tired (-3) and Dazed (-5) to Unconscious; a rest restores it (the *Rest* button). Wounds heal through recovery rolls over days and weeks.
