# Playing Legend of the Five Rings (4th edition)

## Rolls
Every roll is written **XkY**: roll X d10s and add up the best Y. Skill rolls are **skill rank + trait, keep the trait** (Kenjutsu 3 with Agility 3 is 6k3). Trait rolls are trait k trait, ring rolls ring k ring. A **10 explodes**: roll again and add. A skill the character has no rank in is **unskilled**: 10s don't explode and no raises may be called.

**Ten Dice Rule**: nobody rolls more than 10 dice. Every two rolled dice above ten become one more kept die; once 10 dice are kept, each further die adds +2. Fudger does this for you and shows the pool, such as 10k10+4.

**TN** (target number): 5 very easy, 10 easy, 15 average, 20 hard, 25 very hard, 30 heroic, 40 legendary. Leave the TN empty when only you know it; the roll then shows the total.

**Raises**: before rolling, the player may call raises, each adding 5 to the TN for a better effect (more damage, faster, more impressive). At most the Void ring in raises. The roll asks for raises and TN together, and adds the **wound penalty** to the TN.

**Void points**: as many as the Void ring. Spend one for +1k1 on a roll (hold Shift or set Spend Void, then tick a box), +10 Armor TN for a round, or to ignore 10 wounds. A night's rest restores them.

**Emphasis**: a skill emphasis lets the roller reroll 1s once. Use the "Emphasis: reroll 1s" button on the result when an emphasis applies.

## Insight
Insight is Rings x 10 plus every skill rank (Courtier and Etiquette add mastery bonuses). Insight Rank is 1 below 150, 2 at 150 and one more every 25. School rank usually follows it.

## Skirmish
- **Initiative**: Insight Rank + Reflexes, keep Reflexes, rolled once; highest first.
- **Stances** each round: Attack, Full Attack (+2k1 on attacks, -10 Armor TN), Defense (adds Air ring and Defense rank to Armor TN), Full Defense (roll Defense/Reflexes, add half to Armor TN, no attacks), Center (no action now, a bonus next round).
- **Attack**: weapon skill + Agility (Kyujutsu: Reflexes), keep the trait, against the target's **Armor TN** (5 + Reflexes x 5 + armor). Raises called on the attack add rolled damage dice (+1k0 each).
- **Damage**: the weapon's damage (katana 3k2) with Strength added to the rolled dice (a bow uses its own Strength). Exploding, Ten Dice Rule. Subtract the target's **Reduction**, add the rest to Wounds taken.

## Wounds
Healthy holds Earth x 5 wounds; each later rank (Nicked, Grazed, Hurt, Injured, Crippled, Down, Out) holds Earth x 2 more. The current rank adds its penalty to every TN: +0, +3, +5, +10, +15, +20, +40. Down can barely act; Out is unconscious, and more wounds kill. Natural healing per day of rest: Stamina x 2 + Insight Rank (the Night of rest button).

NPCs either use wound ranks from Earth like PCs, or a stat block's simpler ladder (such as 15: +5, 30: +10, 45: Dead) set with Wounds per rank, Ranks and Penalty step.

## The iaijutsu duel
1. **Assessment**: Iaijutsu/Awareness against TN 10 + 5 x the opponent's Insight Rank. Learn facts about the opponent; beating their total by 10 gives +1k1 on Focus. Either may concede.
2. **Focus**: contested Iaijutsu/Void. Win by 5 or more to strike first, with a free raise for each further 5; otherwise both strike at once.
3. **Strike**: Iaijutsu/Reflexes against Armor TN, then the sword's damage. Duels are often to first blood.

## Honor, Glory, Status and Taint
Each runs 0 to 10 in ranks and points. Honor Rank is added to rolls against fear and temptation; Fear rank X means a Willpower roll (plus Honor Rank) against TN 5 + 5 x X. Shadowlands Taint grows from contact with the Shadowlands.

## Magic and techniques
Shugenja (tick Spellcaster) cast with **Ring + School Rank, keep the Ring** against TN 5 + 5 x the spell's Mastery Level, and have spell slots per element equal to the ring, plus Void slots for any element. Kata are martial stances, kiho are monks' ki techniques; school techniques come one per school rank.
