# Playing Torg Eternity

## Rolling
Roll a d20 and look up the bonus on the bonus chart, then add it to the attribute or skill. The sheet does this. A 10 or 20 rolls again and adds, so results can climb high. A natural 1 is a mishap.

Compare the total with a Difficulty Number (DN). 10 is standard, 12 challenging, 14 hard, 16 very hard, 18 heroic. Meeting the DN succeeds. Beating it by 5 or more is a good success, and by 10 or more an outstanding success.

## Combat
1. Roll the attack skill against the target's defence (usually Dodge or a melee skill, and at least 8).
2. A hit deals the weapon's damage plus bonus. Compare it with the target's Toughness:
   - below Toughness: no effect, or one Shock with a strong hit
   - equal or more: a Shock
   - 5 or more over: a wound and two Shock, and every further 5 adds a wound
3. Track Shock and Wounds taken on the sheet. Shock equal to the Shock value knocks a character out. Too many wounds kill.
4. Stymied and Weakened are conditions that give penalties until cleared.

## Movement
`Move` on the sheet is how many metres a character moves in a round and can still act. Running covers about three times that but gives a penalty to other actions that round. Difficult ground halves movement. Divide by the map's square size, usually 1 or 2 metres, to get squares.

## Possibilities
Possibilities let a hero reroll and add, or soak damage. Heroes are meant to be bold, so give them chances to spend them.
