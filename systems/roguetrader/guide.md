# Playing Rogue Trader

## The game
The Warhammer 40,000 far future, out past the edge of the Imperium. The players are the household of a **Rogue Trader**, a dynasty that holds an ancient Warrant of Trade: the Lord-Captain and their officers (Arch-militant, Astropath, Explorator, Missionary, Navigator, Seneschal, Void-master). They own a voidship and chase profit, glory and lost worlds among xenos, pirates, heretics and the Warp. Play swings between exploration, trade and intrigue, ground combat and ship battles.

## Tests
Roll **d100** and get the **target** or less. The target is a characteristic (or a skill) plus the difficulty:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Trivial | +60 |
| Easy | +30 |
| Routine | +20 |
| Ordinary | +10 |
| Challenging | +0 |
| Difficult | -10 |
| Hard | -20 |
| Very Hard | -30 |
| Hellish | -60 |

Every full 10 the roll is under the target is a **degree of success**; every full 10 over is a **degree of failure**. A roll of 100 always fails. Skills: **basic** skills untrained test at half the characteristic, **advanced** skills can't be used untrained (the sheet shows 0), Trained uses the full characteristic, and mastery adds +10 or +20. Each level of **fatigue** gives -10 to tests; more fatigue than the Toughness bonus and the character passes out.

A characteristic's **bonus** is its tens digit, multiplied by any Unnatural characteristic. Strength bonus adds to melee damage, Toughness bonus soaks damage, Agility bonus gives initiative and movement.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: 1d10 + Agility bonus, highest first. A turn is a full action or two half actions; movement is the Agility bonus in metres for a half move, double for a full move, triple to charge and six times to run.
- **Attacks**: Weapon Skill in melee, Ballistic Skill at range. The attack roll asks the kind of attack (standard attack +10, all-out attack +20, charge +10, semi-auto burst +0, full-auto burst -10, called shot -20) and the difficulty (range, cover, size). A semi-auto burst scores an extra hit per two degrees of success, full auto one per degree, up to the weapon's rate of fire. Ranged weapons **jam** on 96 or more (94 for bursts).
- **Hit location**: the attack roll's digits reversed: 01-10 head, 11-20 right arm, 21-30 left arm, 31-70 body, 71-85 right leg, 86-00 left leg.
- **Damage**: the weapon's damage (plus Strength bonus in melee). The damage roll asks the target's armour on that location (less the weapon's Penetration) and Toughness bonus, and takes them off. Tearing weapons roll an extra die and drop the lowest. A natural 10 on a damage die is **Righteous Fury**: test the attack again, and on a success add 1d10 (the roll window's button), again on another 10.
- Damage past 0 wounds is **critical damage**: read the critical effects by location and damage type. Targets may **Dodge** or **Parry** once a turn.

## Fate, insanity and corruption
- **Fate points** come back each session (*New session*). Spend one to reroll a test (a failed test offers *Spend Fate: reroll*), add a degree of success, heal 1d5 wounds, remove fatigue or act first in a round. Burning one saves the character from death.
- **Insanity** points come from horror and the Warp's sights, **Corruption** from daemonic and heretical taint. Rising totals bring disorders, malignancies and mutations.

## Healing
*Night of rest* clears fatigue and heals 1 wound for a lightly damaged character (damage no more than twice the Toughness bonus). Heavily damaged characters heal much slower and need Medicae; critically damaged ones need care before healing at all.

## Profit Factor and Endeavours
The dynasty's **Profit Factor** stands for its wealth and influence. To get something, test Profit Factor: the roll asks the item's **availability** (Ubiquitous +70 to Unique -70) and any other modifier (better craftsmanship and larger quantities make it harder). **Endeavours** are the dynasty's great undertakings: completing objectives earns Achievement Points, and a finished Endeavour raises Profit Factor. Misfortunes lower it.

## Psykers and Navigators
Astropaths and Navigators use powers with a **power test**: Willpower plus 5 per psy rating used, against the difficulty. Doubles on an unfettered or pushed power bring Psychic Phenomena.

## Voidships
Make the dynasty's ship as a **Spaceship**. Its hull gives Speed, Manoeuvrability, Detection, Armour, void shields, turrets and Hull Integrity; **components** use power and space. The crew rating (Incompetent 20 to Elite 60) is the target for crew tests; named officers use their own skills plus the ship's modifiers.
- **Initiative**: 1d10 + Detection bonus.
- **Fire**: a gunnery test (crew rating or the gunner's Ballistic Skill). A macrobattery scores 1 hit plus 1 per degree of success, a lance 1 hit plus 1 per three degrees, each up to the weapon's Strength. Torpedoes are launched and resolved as a salvo.
- **Damage**: each void shield stops one hit a turn (*New turn* raises them again). Roll damage for the hits that got through; macrobattery damage is reduced by Armour, lances ignore it. What is left comes off **Hull Integrity**; at 0 the ship is crippled and criticals follow. Fires aboard eat crew and morale.
