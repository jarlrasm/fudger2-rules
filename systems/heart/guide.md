# Heart: The City Beneath

## Rolling
When a character does something risky, roll a pool of d10s: one die, plus one if they have a fitting **skill** (Compel, Delve, Discern, Endure, Evade, Hunt, Kill, Mend, Sneak), plus one if they have a fitting **domain** (Cursed, Desolate, Haven, Occult, Religion, Technology, Warren, Wild), plus one for help. A **knack** that applies gives mastery: the test is one step less difficult. Keep the highest die. **Difficulty** removes the highest dice first: Risky removes one, Dangerous two; with no dice left the result is a 1.

- **10**: critical success, with increased effect.
- **8–9**: success.
- **6–7**: success at a cost: take stress.
- **2–5**: failure: take stress.
- **1**: critical failure: take double stress and things go badly.

The sheet asks for extra dice and the difficulty; pick one step lower for a knack.

## Stress, protection and fallout
Stress is a die (d4 to d12, larger for nastier sources) rolled and marked on one of five **resistances**: Blood (the body), Mind, Echo (the Heart's influence), Fortune (luck and reputation) and Supplies. Protection on that resistance is taken off. When it matters, the GM rolls a d12 for **fallout**: equal to or under the character's total stress, they suffer it, minor at 6 total stress or less and major above 6. Fallout resolves by clearing some stress into a lasting consequence (an injury, a rumour, lost supplies, a mutation...). Resting in a haven clears stress.

## Combat
Kill tests deal the weapon's stress die to the adversary's resistance, minus its protection. Adversaries deal their own stress dice back when players fail or succeed at a cost. Weapon tags change how the die is rolled or what it hits: **Brutal** rolls the stress die twice and keeps the higher, **Piercing** ignores protection (the sheet does both), and Ranged, Spread and the rest are for the GM to judge.

## Landmarks and delves
The City Beneath is made of **landmarks** (havens, ruins, horrors) linked by **delves**, dangerous journeys with a resistance the delvers wear down with Delve tests while the GM deals stress. Landmarks have a tier (depth), domains, haunts and resources.

## Callings and beats
Each character follows a **calling** (Adventure, Enlightenment, Forced, Heartsblood, Penitent) with beats: hit them to earn minor, major and eventually zenith abilities from the class.
