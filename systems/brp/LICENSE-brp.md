# Basic Roleplaying library licence

`brp.json` holds the weapons (160, from the weapon tables of the Equipment chapter) and creatures (52, from the Creatures chapter) of **Basic Roleplaying: Universal Game Engine**.

- Source: the Basic Roleplaying ORC Content Document, https://www.chaosium.com/content/orclicense/BasicRoleplaying-ORC-Content-Document.rtf (fetched 2026-11-25; the same text as the PDF next to it).
- Changes: converted to Fudger library items by `tools/brp_import.py`. Weapon table columns are moved into fields and the table title becomes the weapon's category (the source's "Historic Meleee Waapons" is spelled "Historic Melee Weapons"). Creature stat blocks keep the average characteristics, hit points, move, damage modifier, armor, attacks, skills and powers; the two werewolf forms are named "Werewolf, Hybrid Form" and "Werewolf, Wolf Form". Footnote markers are dropped.
- Trademarks: Basic Roleplaying, Chaosium Inc. and the Chaosium logo are trademarks of Chaosium Inc. and are not licensed. The "Powered by BRP" logo the document asks for is not shipped; the credit below names it in text.
