# Playing Basic Roleplaying (Universal Game Engine)

## The game
Basic Roleplaying is Chaosium's classic percentile system, the engine behind Call of Cthulhu and RuneQuest, written to run any setting: fantasy, horror, science fiction, superheroes, history. Characters are defined by characteristics and skills rated in percent; they improve the skills they use.

## Skill rolls
Roll **d100 equal to or under** the skill.
- **Critical**: a twentieth of the skill or less (rounded up). **Special**: a fifth or less. **Success**: at or under. **Failure**: over, and **96-00 always fails**.
- **Fumble**: the top 5% of the failure chance: 96-00 for skills up to 20, shrinking to only 00 above 80.
- **Difficulty**: Easy doubles the skill, Difficult halves it; the roll offers them and asks for any other modifier.
- **Characteristic rolls** are a characteristic times five: Effort (STR), Stamina (CON), Idea (INT), Luck (POW), Agility (DEX), Charm (CHA).
- **Opposed** rolls: both roll, the better level of success wins; ties go to the higher roll under the skill. **Resistance** rolls (characteristic against characteristic) use 50% plus 5% per point of difference.
- A successful roll in play earns an **experience check**; between sessions roll over the skill (plus the experience bonus) to raise it.

## Combat
- Characters act in **DEX order**, highest first (the DEX Rank roll puts them in the initiative list).
- **Attack** with the weapon skill; the defender may **parry** or **dodge**. Special and critical successes do extra damage: bleeding, crushing or impaling by weapon type; a critical ignores armor.
- **Damage**: the weapon's dice plus the **damage modifier** (from STR + SIZ; thrown weapons add half), minus armor, off **hit points**.
- A single wound of half the hit points or more is a **major wound**. At 2 hit points or less the character falls unconscious; at 0 they die at the end of the next round without help.

## Healing and power
Hit points heal 1D3 a week (First Aid and Medicine help). **Power points** (POW) fuel magic, psychic abilities, sorcery and super powers, and come back over a day of rest.

## Optional rules
Hit locations, fatigue points, sanity, allegiance, passions and the Education characteristic are all optional; the sheet has fields for fatigue and sanity.
