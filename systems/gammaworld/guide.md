# Gamma World (1st edition)

Gamma Terra, centuries after the Ancients' world burned: tribes of pure strain humans, mutated humanoids, thinking animals and plants pick through ruins full of robots still following orders, radiation and devices nobody understands. Play it gonzo and dangerous; the dice are deadly and the setting is strange.

This system follows the 1978 TSR rules. The 2010 seventh edition (Wizards of the Coast, built on D&D 4th edition with Alpha Mutation cards and two random origins per character) plays very differently and is not covered.

## Characters
- Six attributes, 3d6 each (4d6 keeping three is a common option): MS Mental Strength, IN Intelligence, DX Dexterity, CH Charisma, CN Constitution, PS Physical Strength.
- **Type.** Pure Strain Humans have no mutations, get +3 Charisma, and robots and computers obey them when they show proper identification. Humanoids and mutated animals roll d4 physical and d4 mental mutations on the sheet's Mutation Tables (d100; animals use their own column; (D) is a defect). Add each mutation as a Physical or Mental Mutation item; set Attack to Yes for those that attack.
- **Hit points** are a d6 for each point of Constitution (Roll HP). They do not grow with level.
- DX over 15 adds 1 to hit per point, under 6 takes 1 off. PS over 15 adds 1 damage per point, under 6 takes 1 off. IN over 15 takes 1 off artifact rolls per point (good), under 7 adds 1. Charisma sets reactions, follower morale and the most followers.

## Combat
- A melee turn is 10 seconds; movement is about 12 m a turn. Distances are in metres.
- Roll d6 for surprise: 1-2 is surprised. Then each combatant rolls d6 for initiative, plus Dexterity over 15; highest strikes first.
- **Physical attacks** read the weapon's class (1 club to 16 fusion rifle) against the target's descending Armour Class (10 unprotected to 1 powered assault armour) on d20. The roll asks for the AC; left empty it says the worst AC it hits. Beyond a weapon's range, up to double, attacks are at -5. Creatures without weapons attack with their hit dice instead.
- **Mental attacks** read the attacker's Mental Strength against the defender's: d20 at or over 10 plus the defender's MS minus the attacker's. A character can't make a mental and a physical attack in the same turn. Each mental attack or defence uses a mutation's own range, uses and damage.
- There are no critical hits or fumbles. At 0 hit points a character is dead. A full day of rest heals 1 hit point (Day of Rest); some mutations and medical artifacts heal far more.
- **Morale**: d10 plus Charisma's morale adjustment; intelligent creatures hold on 3 or more, animals on 5. Check again each round once it starts.

## Radiation and poison
Each source has an intensity (radiation) or strength (poison) of 3 to 18, read against the victim's Constitution-based resistance. The roll asks for the number and gives damage dice, a new mutation (radiation only) or death. Heightened Constitution adds 3 against radiation and makes poison resistance 18.

## Artifacts
Every device of the Ancients follows one of three use charts: A simple, B complex, C very complex. Each turn spent tinkering rolls d10 plus the Intelligence modifier (B adds 1, C adds 2): 3 or less figures it out, up to 10 means keep trying (the roll offers Try again), and 11 or more is a mishap the GM describes. Mark an artifact Figured Out once it works. Tech Level I is primitive, II medieval to early industrial, III the Ancients' high technology.

## Creatures, robots and alliances
Creature sheets hold AC, hit dice (d6s), MS and CN, morale, attacks and mutations; Generation rolls a creature's mutation for each generation it has mutated. Robots have their own sheet with power source and programming. Cryptic Alliances are the secret societies that fight over the future; use their sheet for goals, enemies and secret signs.

## Experience
Points come from foes beaten (one per hit point) and artifacts figured out. Each new level rolls d10 for a small bonus (Level Bonus).

## Approximations
The book's matrices are not reproduced. The sheet uses formulas that follow their shape: weapon class against AC needs 20 minus AC minus half of (WC - 1); hit dice against AC needs 21 minus AC minus HD; radiation kills at 6 or more over resistance and mutates at 3-5 over; poison kills at 4 or more over. The artifact roll is a simplified stand-in for the flowcharts, and the level bonus table is a guess at its shape. Where the book is at hand, use its numbers.
