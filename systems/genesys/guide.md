# Playing Genesys

## Narrative dice
A check builds a pool of special dice and reads their **symbols**, not a total. Fudger rolls ordinary dice for each type, shows each die's symbols (S success, A advantage, F failure, T threat) and works out the net result.

- **Positive dice:** Ability (green d8) and Proficiency (yellow d12) from the character; Boost (blue d6) from advantages in the situation. The higher of characteristic and skill ranks is the number of dice, and the lower is how many of them are upgraded to Proficiency.
- **Negative dice:** Difficulty (purple d8, 2 for an average task, up to 5 for formidable), Challenge (red d12, upgraded difficulty), Setback (black d6). Fudger asks for the difficulty dice and then the setback dice when you roll (a negative setback number adds that many Boost dice instead). Upgrades in the **Check** row turns Ability dice into Proficiency, and Challenge turns Difficulty dice into Challenge dice.
- **Successes** cancel **failures**: one net success means the check succeeds. **Advantages** and **threats** cancel too and add good or bad side effects whatever the outcome.
- A **Triumph** counts as a success and something great happens; a **Despair** counts as a failure and something terrible happens.

## Story points
Players and the GM share a pool of Story Points. Spend one to upgrade a die in your own pool (or the opponent's, for the GM), or to add a convenient detail to the scene. The point then goes to the other side.

## Combat
Initiative is a Cool check (if ready) or Vigilance check (if surprised). Sort by successes, then advantages, and give the slots to either side as they like.

1. A turn has one action and one maneuver (moving, aiming, drawing a weapon). Spend strain for a second maneuver.
2. Attacks are skill checks against a difficulty set by range (melee and brawl: 2; ranged: Short 1, Medium 2, Long 3, Extreme 4, Engaged 2), and the target's Melee or Ranged Defense adds that many setback dice. Fudger asks for both. Melee weapons written +N deal Brawn + N. Each net success adds 1 damage. Spend advantages to trigger a Critical (the weapon's Critical rating) or other effects.
3. Damage minus Soak becomes wounds. Exceeding the wound threshold incapacitates the target and causes a Critical Injury. Strain over the threshold knocks the character out.

## Style
The dice tell a story, so ask the players to describe what their advantages, threats, Triumphs and Despairs mean. Genesys fits any setting; choose one and add your own talents and gear.
