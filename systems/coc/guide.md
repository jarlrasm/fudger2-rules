# Playing Call of Cthulhu (7th edition)

## Rolling
Every check is a d100 roll **at or under** a skill or characteristic. The sheet shows each value with its half and fifth.

- **Regular** success is at or under the value, **Hard** at or under half, **Extreme** at or under a fifth. A 01 is a critical.
- A **fumble** is 100, or 96–100 when the value is under 50.
- The Keeper says which level a task needs. Opposed rolls compare levels, and the higher value breaks ties.
- **Bonus and penalty dice:** set the Dice slider before rolling (hold Shift for one bonus die, Ctrl for one penalty die). Each extra tens die is rolled, and the best (bonus) or worst (penalty) tens digit is kept.
- **Pushing:** after a failed roll the window offers *Push the roll*. Only push when the player explains how they try harder, and decide what goes badly wrong if the push fails too.
- **Luck** can be spent point for point to turn a near miss into a success (not on Sanity, Luck or combat rolls). Lower Current Luck by hand.
- Tick **Improve** on a skill after a success with it. At the end of the session, roll d100 for each ticked skill; a roll over the skill (or over 95) adds 1d10.

## Sanity
When an investigator sees something terrible, roll Sanity (the Current value). The loss is written like `1/1d6`: the first part on a success, the second on a failure. Losing 5 or more at once can bring **Temporary Insanity** after an INT roll; losing a fifth of current Sanity in one day brings **Indefinite Insanity**. Max Sanity is 99 minus Cthulhu Mythos.

## Combat
Act in DEX order (highest first); readied firearms go first. Roll the DEX "Initiative" field to put the character in the tracker.

1. Attacks roll Fighting or Firearms from the Attacks list. The defender may **Dodge** or **fight back** (opposed rolls) against melee, but only take cover against guns.
2. Damage is the weapon's dice, plus the damage bonus for melee and thrown weapons. An Extreme success deals maximum damage, and impaling weapons add a damage roll.
3. A single hit of half max HP or more is a **Major Wound**: tick it and make a CON roll to stay conscious. At 0 HP with a major wound the investigator is **Dying** and needs First Aid, then Medicine.

## Style
Investigation first, fighting last. Hand out clues readily, keep the horror mostly unseen, and let the Mythos cost the investigators something every time they get closer to the truth.
