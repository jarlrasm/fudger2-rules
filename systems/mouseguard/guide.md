# Mouse Guard 2nd edition

## Tests
A test rolls a skill or ability in d6s. Every 4, 5 or 6 is a success (a sword); 1 to 3 are failures (a snake). The GM sets an **Obstacle**; meet it to pass. On a failure the GM chooses: the mouse gets what they wanted but takes a condition, or a **twist** makes the situation worse. Help from other mice, gear, wises and traits add dice. Without the skill a mouse uses **Beginner's Luck**: half their Will (mental) or Health (physical) in dice. Using **Nature** instead of a skill risks taxing it. Versus tests compare successes.

Successes and failures are logged as passes and fails toward advancement.

## Fate, Persona and checks
Spending **Fate** makes the roll's 6s explode (roll another die for each). **Persona** adds a die, or taps Nature to add Nature's rating. Both are earned at the end of a session by playing the mouse's Belief, Goal and Instinct. **Checks** come from using traits against yourself and buy actions in the players' turn.

## Conditions
Hungry and Thirsty, Angry, Tired, Injured, Sick and Dead. Injured and Sick take a die off most tests (the sheet takes them off); Hungry and Tired lower conflict dispositions; Angry stops the use of wises and beneficial traits. Recover them in the players' turn with tests or at the end of a mission.

## Conflicts
Fights, chases, arguments and journeys are conflicts. Each side rolls for **disposition** (an ability plus a skill, plus successes), then scripts three actions a round: Attack (versus Defend), Defend (restore disposition), Feint (beats Attack) and Maneuver (impede or gain position). A side at 0 disposition loses; the winner pays a compromise depending on how much disposition it lost. Weapons give bonus dice or successes to particular actions.

## The mission
Each session is the GM's turn (a mission with obstacles: animals, weather, wilderness, mice) followed by the players' turn, where checks buy recovery, research and personal business.

## Animals and weather
Animals have a Nature rating with descriptors, which they roll for anything in their nature, a disposition and weapons such as teeth and claws.
