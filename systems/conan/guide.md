# Playing Conan: Adventures in an Age Undreamed Of

## The game
The Hyborian Age of Robert E. Howard's stories: decadent kingdoms, ruined temples, sorcerers who bargain with things from outside, and heroes who live by the sword. Play is fast and violent, with the GM's **Doom** pool and the players' **Momentum** pushing and pulling the story.

## Tests
- Roll **2d20** against a **target number**: attribute + the skill's **Expertise**. Each die at or under it is a success; each at or under the skill's **Focus** is two, and a 1 is always two.
- The GM sets the **difficulty**, the successes needed (0 to 5); the roll asks for it. Successes beyond it become **Momentum**, shared by the group.
- Each test asks for **extra d20s**: up to three more, bought with Momentum (players) or by giving the GM Doom. A **Fortune** point adds a die that already shows 1 (two successes): set *Fortune dice* on the sheet before the test and take the points off Fortune. No test has more than five dice.
- A **20** is a **complication** (19 or 20 without Expertise in the skill); the GM may take Doom instead.

## Combat
- No initiative roll: the player characters act first, in any order; the GM may spend Doom to let an enemy go before them.
- **Melee**: Agility + Melee. **Ranged**: Coordination + Ranged Weapons. Defenders can **parry** (Coordination + Parry) or dodge to make it a struggle.
- **Damage** rolls **combat dice** (d6: 1, 2, blank, blank, Effect, Effect; an Effect counts 1 and triggers weapon qualities) plus the Brawn bonus (melee) or Awareness bonus (ranged). The attack rows roll both, and the damage roll's d20 gives the **hit location** (1-2 head, 3-5 right arm, 6-8 left arm, 9-14 torso, 15-17 right leg, 18-20 left leg).
- Armor at the location hit and Courage **soak**. Damage takes **Vigor** (physical) or **Resolve** (mental). Five or more in one hit, or dropping to 0, inflicts a **wound** or **trauma**; each raises the difficulty of related tests, and the fifth kills or breaks.
- After a fight, the *Catch your breath* button restores Vigor and Resolve. Wounds need Healing.

## NPCs
Foes are **Minions** (one wound), **Toughened** (two) or **Nemeses** (full rules). They test with **fields of expertise** (Combat, Fortitude, Knowledge, Movement, Senses, Social) instead of skills.
