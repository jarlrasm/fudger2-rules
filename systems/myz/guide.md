# Playing Mutant: Year Zero

The old world is gone. The player characters are mutants of the Ark, a ruined shelter where the last people live under their bosses. They go out into the Zone to scavenge grub, water, bullets and artifacts, and come back to build the Ark up.

## Rolling dice
Roll **base dice** (yellow) for the attribute's current score, **skill dice** (green) for the skill level and **gear dice** (black) for a weapon's or tool's bonus. Every **6** is a success; one is enough, and extra successes buy extra effects or damage. The rolls ask for a **modifier**: it adds or removes skill dice. Below zero skill dice you roll **negative dice** (red), and each 6 on them takes away a success.

- **Pushing:** once per roll the player may push: reroll every die that is not a 6, and not a 1 on base or gear dice. After the push, each 1 on base dice is a point of **trauma** to the attribute (Strength takes damage, Agility fatigue, Wits confusion, Empathy doubt) and gives a **Mutation Point**. Each 1 on gear dice lowers the gear bonus by one. Negative dice are not rerolled.
- **Specialist skill:** each role has one (Enforcer Intimidate, Gearhead Jury-Rig, Stalker Find the Path, Fixer Make a Deal, Dog Handler Sic a Dog, Chronicler Inspire, Boss Command, Slave Shake It Off), rolled with the role's key attribute.

## Mutations
Every mutant has one or two mutations (library items). Using one spends Mutation Points: the *Use Mutation* field asks how many and rolls a die for each. Any 1 means the mutation misfires; read its misfire. Mutation Points top out at 10.

## Trauma, conditions and healing
An attribute at 0 means **broken**: broken by damage, roll D66 on *Critical Injury* and note it under Critical Injuries; a lethal one kills unless healed in time. A broken character hit again can be finished off. Heal is rolled to bring a broken friend back.

Each day a mutant needs a ration of grub, a ration of water and sleep, and shelter from the cold. Without them they become **starving**, **dehydrated**, **sleepless** or **hypothermic** and cannot heal: the *Rest* button heals a point of damage and fatigue, *Sleep* a point of confusion, and *Closeness*, a moment of trust with another person, a point of doubt.

## The Rot
The Zone is full of the Rot. Rot Points come from rot areas and from rotten grub and water. Each time a mutant gets one, roll *Rot Roll* (a die for every Rot Point, permanent ones too): each 1 is a point of damage. In the Ark, *Wash Off* clears the temporary Rot Points; permanent rot stays.

## Combat
Everyone rolls **Initiative** (D6 plus Agility); highest acts first. Each round a character has an **action** and a **maneuver**.

1. Fight (Strength) or Shoot (Agility), plus the weapon's bonus as gear dice. The Attacks list builds the roll from the attached weapons.
2. A hit does the weapon's damage, plus one for each extra success.
3. Armour rolls its rating as dice: each 6 stops a point of damage.

Guns use a bullet for each shot, and bullets are also the Zone's money. Threats (creatures) roll their Dice field for skills and attacks.

## The Ark
Make an **Ark** entity for the group. Its development levels (Food Supply, Culture, Technology, Warfare) rise when the people finish **projects** (library items with work points the players earn by rolling skills between expeditions) or bring home **artifacts** with a DEV bonus. Bosses rule the Ark with their gangs and want things from the player characters. **Vehicles** have resilience, armour, fuel and a gear bonus for the driver's Move roll.

## Style
Hard, dirty survival with dark humour. Give the players short expeditions into Zone sectors, hard choices about what to bring home, and Ark politics when they get back. This sheet carries no rules text from the book; look up mutations, talents, critical injuries and threats there.
