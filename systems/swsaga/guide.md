# Playing Star Wars Saga Edition

## Checks
Roll d20, add the bonus and compare with a DC: 10 easy, 15 average, 20 hard, 25 very hard, 30 heroic. A skill's bonus is half the heroic level, the ability modifier, +5 if trained and +5 more for Skill Focus, less condition and armor penalties. Every check on the sheet asks for the DC; leave it empty when only you know it, and the roll shows the total. A natural 20 or 1 on a skill check means nothing special, except on Use the Force, where a natural 20 brings back all the hero's spent Force powers.

## Force Points and Destiny
A hero gets 5 plus half their level in Force Points each level. Spending one adds 1d6 to an attack, skill or ability check (from 8th level 2d6 and from 15th 3d6, keeping the highest die). When a roll fails or has no DC and the hero has points left, the roll window offers Spend a Force Point, which rolls the die, adds it and takes the point off the sheet. A Force Point can also move the hero one step up the condition track. Destiny Points are rarer: spent at the turning points of the hero's destiny for an automatic critical hit, an automatic miss against them, or triple Force Point dice. A rising Dark Side Score marks a hero slipping towards the dark side; when it equals Wisdom they have fallen.

## Combat
1. Call initiative. Each side rolls Initiative, d20 plus the Initiative skill. Highest goes first. A turn has a standard, a move and a swift action.
2. Attacks are d20 plus base attack bonus and Strength (melee) or Dexterity (ranged) against the target's Reflex Defense. The roll asks first for a modifier (range penalties: short -2, medium -5, long -10; aiming, cover and other situations) and then for the target's Reflex. A natural 20 always hits and is a critical hit that doubles the damage; a natural 1 always misses. Attacks check the hero's feats for the matching Weapon Proficiency and take -5 without it.
3. Damage is the weapon's dice plus half the heroic level, plus Strength in melee (twice it with a two-handed weapon). Fortitude and Will Defense are targeted by poisons, Force powers, persuasion and the like.
4. When one hit deals damage equal to or above the target's damage threshold (its Fortitude Defense, plus size for big creatures and vehicles), it also moves one step down the condition track. At 0 hit points a hero falls unconscious, or dies if that hit also beat the threshold.

## Condition track
The steps are -1, -2, -5, -10 and helpless, and the penalty applies to attacks, defenses, skill and ability checks; the sheet subtracts it. Three swift actions (Recover) move one step back up, unless the condition is persistent (disease, poison, some Force powers). Catch a Second Wind heals a quarter of maximum hit points or the Constitution score, whichever is more, once a day when the hero is at half hit points or less. A night of rest heals hit points equal to the level and restores the Second Wind.

## The Force
Force Sensitive heroes trained in Use the Force pick Force powers. Each power is a Use the Force check against a DC or the target's Defense, and the power's entry says what each result does. A power is spent once used and comes back after a rest or a natural 20 on Use the Force.

## Creatures, vehicles and ships
Creatures use a stat block with their defenses, hit points, threshold, condition track and attacks already totalled. Vehicles and Spaceships have their own sheets: Reflex, Fortitude, hull hit points, damage reduction, shield rating, damage threshold and a condition track that ends in disabled. Shields take their rating off each hit; a hit that gets through the shields lowers them by 5, and Recharge shields brings 5 back. Ship weapons attack the target's Reflex like any other attack. Distances are in squares of 1.5 metres.
