# Playing Deadlands Classic

The Weird West of 1876: the Reckoning has let horrors loose, ghost rock powers mad science, and the posse stands between the frontier and the dark. Play it as horror first and western second. Fear feeds the Reckoners and turns places into Deadlands.

## Traits and aptitudes
Each of the ten traits is a number of dice of one type, such as 3d8. Corporeal: Deftness, Nimbleness, Quickness, Strength, Vigor. Mental: Cognition, Knowledge, Mien, Smarts, Spirit. An aptitude (skill) sits under a trait and rolls as many dice as its level, of that trait's die type: Shootin' 4 under Deftness d10 rolls 4d10. Without the aptitude, roll the trait's own dice at -4.

## Rolling
Roll the dice and **keep the highest single die**. A die that shows its top face **aces**: roll it again and add. Compare with the TN: Foolproof 3, Fair 5 (usual), Onerous 7, Hard 9, Incredible 11. Every full 5 over the TN is a **raise**, which makes the result better (a shot lands where the shooter wanted, a task goes faster).

Every roll on the sheet asks for a modifier and the TN; leave the TN empty when only the Marshal knows it and judge the total. The worst wound level is taken off automatically.

**Going bust:** if half or more of the dice rolled show 1, the roll goes bust: something goes wrong, whatever the total. A huckster's bust is backlash from the manitou.

## Fate chips
Players draw three chips from the pot at the start of a session. After a roll, a chip adds one more die of the same type (the roll window offers it, spending white chips first, then red, then blue). Chips can also buy off wounds just taken: white one level, red two, blue three. When a red chip is spent, the Marshal draws a chip too. At the end of the session, Cash In turns chips into bounty points (white 1, red 2, blue 3). Hand out chips for good play and brave choices.

## Initiative
Each round every combatant rolls Quickness (Action Cards on the sheet) against Fair (5) and draws one action card, plus one for a success and each raise; a bust draws just one. Cards act from Ace down, suits break ties (spades, hearts, diamonds, clubs). A red joker is a bonus; a black joker is bad news. A hero may keep one card up his sleeve for later rounds. The app's Deal initiative button deals one card from the 54-card deck to each combatant; deal extra cards by hand when a roll earns them.

## Combat and damage
- Shootin', Throwin' and Bow roll against Fair (5), -1 for each range increment past the first. Fightin' rolls against Fair (5) adjusted for the defender's skill.
- Damage dice are added together and ace too. STR in a weapon's damage adds the Strength dice. The Damage roll in Attacks rolls them and a d20 hit location (legs, guts, gizzards, arms, noggin), and asks for the target's Size.
- Each full Size in damage (6 for a man) is one wound level in that location: Light, Heavy, Serious, Critical, Maimed. Mark it under Wounds. The worst level anywhere is the penalty to every roll. A maimed head or guts kills; a maimed limb is useless.
- Wounds also cost Wind, as do running, cold and some hexes. Wind is Vigor plus Spirit die types; at 0 the hero drops. A wounded hero may have to make a Vigor check or be Stunned. Catch Your Breath restores Wind after the fight.

## Fear and Grit
Abominations have a Terror number: those who see one roll Guts against it, adding Grit. Failure means fear, fleeing or worse, and gives the Marshal a chip.

## Arcane backgrounds
- **Hucksters** roll Hexslingin', then draw five cards plus one per raise and build the best poker hand. The hex's power depends on the hand; a bust is backlash.
- **The blessed** roll Faith against the miracle's TN.
- **Mad scientists** build gizmos with Mad science and risk malfunctions.
- **Shamans** earn appeasement with rituals and spend it on favors from the spirits.

## Sheet notes
Edges, Hindrances, weapons, gear and arcane powers are library items attached to the character. Attacks lists one row per weapon. Creatures have their ten traits, a few key aptitudes, attacks with their own dice, Terror, Size and a single wound level. Vehicles track Durability and damage taken.
