# Playing Pirate Borg

## Tests
Roll **d20 + ability** (Strength, Agility, Presence, Toughness or Spirit) against a **DR**: 6 very easy, 8 easy, 10 routine, **12 normal**, 14 difficult, 16 really hard, 18 nearly impossible. The sheet asks for the DR and fills in 12.

- A natural **20** is a crit and a natural **1** a fumble, whatever the DR.
- **Devil's Luck** (the Luck field): spend one to reroll any die, deal maximum damage, take d6 less damage, cancel a crit or fumble, or lower a DR by 4. While a pirate has luck, every test offers *Spend Devil's Luck*, which rerolls it and takes one off. Luck comes back (roll the class's Luck Die) after six hours of rest, but only once it has run out.

## Combat
Players roll everything; enemies do not roll to hit.

- **Initiative:** each round roll d6 (Which Side): 1–3 the enemies act first, 4–6 the pirates. Inside a side each rolls d6 + Agility.
- **Attack:** melee uses Strength, ranged and black powder use Presence, DR12. A crit deals double damage and drops the target's armour a tier; a fumble breaks or drops the weapon. Black powder needs reloading and ignores armour.
- **Defend:** test Agility (Defence) DR12. A failure takes the enemy's damage minus armour; a fumble doubles it.
- **Armour:** tier 1 −d2, tier 2 −d4 (Agility and Defence DR+2), tier 3 −d6 (Agility DR+4, Defence DR+2, sinks you in water). A metal lined hat or morion takes 1 more off.
- **Morale:** when the leader dies, half the group falls or a lone enemy drops to a third of its HP, roll 2d6 against Morale. Higher, and it flees (1–3 on d6) or surrenders. No Morale means it fights to the end.
- **Reaction:** roll 2d6 when meeting someone not yet hostile: low is hostile, 7–8 indifferent, high helpful.

## Damage and death
At 0 HP a pirate is **broken**; below 0 dead. Rest heals d4 (short, with food and drink) or d6 (six hours).

## Rituals and relics
Roll d4 + Spirit each dawn for ritual uses. Casting is a Spirit test DR12: a failure is a mystical mishap and no more rituals until dawn (tick Rituals Lost). After using a relic, test Spirit DR12: a failure stuns and the relic sleeps until dawn; a fumble destroys it and costs d2 HP. Neither works in tier 2–3 armour or holding cold iron. The Dawn button clears both.

## Ships
Each vessel is a **Ship** with HP (condition, crew and morale together, about five times a person's), Hull (armour for the ship), Agility, Skill (the crew), Speed in 50 ft hexes, crew limits and cargo.

- Ships roll **d6 + Agility** for initiative each round, highest first; pirate ships win ties.
- The captain moves the ship; every other pirate takes one crew action: fire broadsides or small arms (Skill test DR12), ram (Agility test), come about, full sail, repair, board. A crit doubles damage and lowers the target's hull a tier; a fumble misfires.
- Cannon damage to a person is five times as much; a person's damage to a ship a fifth.
- At 0 HP the ship is derelict. Ship morale: 2d6 + Skill, under 4 the crew surrenders or mutinies.

## Style
The Dark Caribbean is cursed and drowning. Undead crews, sea monsters and greedy governors; treasure is always a bad idea, and so is the sea.
