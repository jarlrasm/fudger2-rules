# Spire: The City Must Fall

The players are dark elves (drow) in a secret cell of the resistance, working to bring down the high elves (aelfir) who rule Spire, a mile-high city stacked on itself. Every action costs them something: blood, sanity, money, cover or reputation.

## Rolling
When the outcome is uncertain and failing would matter, the player rolls D10s: one die, plus one for a fitting **skill** (Compel, Deceive, Fight, Fix, Investigate, Pursue, Resist, Sneak, Steal), plus one for a fitting **domain** (Academia, Commerce, Crime, High Society, Low Society, Occult, Order, Religion, Technology), plus one for **mastery**, which counts only once per roll. Helpers with a fitting skill or domain add a die each and share the stress. Keep the highest die.

**Difficulty** (0 Standard, 1 Risky, 2 Dangerous; an enemy's difficulty) takes dice away before rolling. You always roll at least one die; each die of difficulty that cannot be removed makes the result one step worse.

- **10**: critical success. No stress, and +1 stress dealt for each 10.
- **8–9**: success. No stress.
- **6–7**: success at a cost: take stress.
- **2–5**: failure: take stress.
- **1**: critical failure: take double stress.

The sheet asks for extra dice and then the difficulty.

## Stress
The GM sets the stress die: 1 for a slap, D3 for minor danger, D6 for serious, D8 for deadly. The player marks it on a fitting resistance: **Blood** (body), **Mind** (sanity), **Silver** (money and gear), **Shadow** (cover from the authorities) or **Reputation** (standing). Extra slots from class, durance and advances fill first and don't count towards fallout. **Armour** soaks Blood stress from attacks and clears at the end of each situation; **Piercing** weapons get past it.

## Fallout
After a character takes stress, the GM rolls a D10. If it is **under** the character's total stress, they suffer fallout, by total stress: **minor** at 2–4 (clear 3 stress), **moderate** at 5–8 (clear 5), **severe** at 9 or more (clear 7). Fallout is a lasting problem tied to the resistance: a broken arm, a nervous tic, a debt, a wanted poster, a betrayal. Severe fallout can kill or remove a character. Record it in Fallout taken.

Stress also clears through the class's **refresh** (D3, D6 or D8 stress for doing it partly, mostly or fully) and by **laying low**, which clears everything while the city's plots move on without the cell.

## Combat
Fight tests use the weapon's stress die against the enemy. Enemies have a **difficulty** (dice off every roll against them), a **resistance** (stress until they are at the cell's mercy) and their weapons. They hurt the characters when the players fail or succeed at a cost. Tags: **Brutal** rolls two stress dice and keeps the higher; **Piercing** ignores armour; **Ranged** and **Extreme Range** reach further; **Spread** splits stress onto nearby foes; **Stunning**, **Reload**, **One-shot**, **Tiring**, **Unreliable**, **Dangerous** and **Concealable** are for the GM to apply. The battlemap treats melee as 2 m and ranged as 40 m.

## Bonds and organisations
Bonds are people and groups at **Individual**, **Street** or **City** level who help the cell. A favour marks stress on the bond (1 to D8 by size), and a bond can't act against a group above its own level. Organisations are their own entity type, with level, difficulty, resistance and stress.

## Advancement
Change the city in a small way for a **Low** advance, a moderate way for **Medium**, a huge one for **High**. Advances come from the character's class, or another class at one grade lower.
