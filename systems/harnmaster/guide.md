# HârnMaster 3: play guide

Gritty medieval fantasy on the island of Hârn. Characters are fragile, combat is short and dangerous, and injuries linger for weeks.

## Rolls

- **Skills** are rolled on d100 at or under the effective mastery level (EML). EML is the mastery level (ML) less 5 for every point of penalty, held between 5 and 95. A roll that ends in 0 or 5 is critical, so every roll is one of four results: critical success (CS), marginal success (MS), marginal failure (MF) or critical failure (CF). Harder or easier tasks move the EML by 10 or 20; the roll asks for that modifier.
- **Attributes** (STR, STA, DEX, AGL, EYE, HRG, SML, VOI, INT, AUR, WIL, CML) are rolled on 3d6 at or under the attribute, 4d6 or 5d6 when the task is hard. The roll asks how many dice.
- **Skill base** (SB) is the average of three attributes plus sunsign bonuses. A skill opens at SB times its multiplier; the sheet fills that in and the player raises ML as the character improves.

## Penalties

- **Universal penalty** (UP) is the sum of all injury levels plus blood loss and fatigue. Every skill and attribute roll loses 5 per point (attributes lose 1 per point).
- **Physical penalty** (PP) is UP plus encumbrance (load in pounds divided by Endurance). Physical and combat skills and STR, STA, DEX, AGL use it.

## Combat

- **Order**: fighters act in order of Initiative EML, highest first.
- **Melee**: the attacker rolls the weapon's AML. The defender chooses: Block (roll the weapon's or shield's DML), Counterstrike (roll their own AML), Dodge (roll Dodge) or Ignore. Roll the attack and pick the defender's result in the prompt; the roll reads the melee table:
  - **A\*n**: the attack hits. Roll n d6 plus the weapon's impact for that aspect (blunt, edged or piercing). The roll does this and rolls the location (mid aim).
  - **D\*n**: in a counterstrike, the defender hits back with n d6 plus their weapon's impact.
  - **Block**, **Miss**: no harm. **DTA**: the defender gets a free extra action. **Fumble** or **Stumble**: the fighter rolls DEX or AGL or drops the weapon or falls.
- **Missiles** use their own table: Block, Dodge or Ignore; CF is a wild shot.
- **Aiming** high or low changes the location and costs EML; the GM rolls the location then.

## Injury

- Take the armour on the struck location for the blow's aspect (Armour locations on the sheet) off the impact. The rest is the effective impact: 1-4 is usually minor (M1), 5-8 and 9-12 serious (S2, S3), 13 and more grievous (G4, G5) or, on the skull, neck, thorax and abdomen, a kill result (K4, K5).
- Write each injury in the Injuries list with its level. Grievous wounds bleed (tick Bleeding): add blood loss over time until treated.
- After any injury, roll **Shock**: 1d6 per point of UP; over Endurance and the character is in shock, out of the fight. A K4 or K5 result calls for a **Kill roll** of 4d6 or 5d6 against Endurance.
- Hand and arm injuries can force a fumble roll, leg and hip injuries a stumble roll.

## Healing

- Every few days of rest, roll each injury's Healing roll (Endurance times its healing rate). CS heals two levels, MS one, MF nothing, CF makes it worse (infection).
- The Rest button clears fatigue after a night's sleep.

## Magic

- Shek-Pvar mages roll their convocation's EML less 5 per spell level. Priests roll their ritual EML less 5 per invocation circle and spend Piety. Psionic talents roll their own EML.

## Creatures

Creatures have a short block: Endurance, Initiative, Awareness, Dodge, natural armour by aspect, and attacks with AML and impact. Track their UP and shock the same way.
