# TinyD6 (Tiny Dungeon 2e)

## Tests
Anything risky is a **Test**: roll 2d6, and if any die shows 5 or 6 it succeeds. **Advantage** (a fitting trait, the family trade, a clever plan) rolls 3d6; **Disadvantage** (bad footing, an unfamiliar weapon) rolls 1d6. They cancel each other and never stack, so a roll is always 1, 2 or 3 dice. The sheet asks for Advantage (+1) or Disadvantage (−1) and then what a die must show, 5 by default. Use 6 only for something truly hard; there is no difficulty number otherwise. The result says how many dice hit; one is enough.

## Characters
A character is a **heritage** (Human, Dwarf, Elf, Fae, Goblin, Karhu, Salimar, Treefolk...), which sets starting Hit Points (usually 6, fewer for small folk, more for big ones) and gives one heritage trait, plus three **traits** chosen from the list. Traits give Advantage in their area or let the character do something special. Each character is proficient with one **weapon group** (Light Melee, Heavy Melee or Ranged) and has one **mastered weapon**, a **family trade** (Advantage on tests it covers) and a **belief**.

## Combat
Everyone rolls 2d6 for **initiative**, highest first, ties to the players. Each turn has **two actions**, in any mix:
- **Attack**: proficient group 2d6, other groups 1d6 (Disadvantage), the mastered weapon 3d6 (Advantage). Any 5 or 6 hits. A hit takes **1 Hit Point** unless a trait or the GM says otherwise.
- **Evade**: until your next turn, each time you are hit, test 1d6 (Evade on the sheet); a success means the attack misses. Tick Evading.
- **Focus**: your next attack or test succeeds on 4, 5 or 6 (the prompt starts at 4 while Focused is ticked). It lasts until you use it or the fight ends; untick it after.
- **Move** about 25 feet, or make another **Test**.

Light melee weapons reach the next square (5 ft), heavy melee 10 ft; ranged weapons use the range written on the weapon (150 ft if none).

## Hit Points and rest
At 0 Hit Points a character drops and is dying; allies must help them quickly. Heal 1 gives back a point (a healer, a potion, a short rest); Rest restores all Hit Points after a safe night.

## Creatures
Creatures have a few Hit Points (3 is typical, big ones more), roll 1 to 3 dice to attack (usually 2), and deal 1 damage per hit. Give them a trait or two that changes a rule. Players can Evade their attacks.

## Other TinyD6 games
The same core runs Tiny Frontiers, Tiny Supers, Tiny Gunslingers and others; swap the heritages and gear, keep the dice. Experience is handed out by the GM and spent on new traits.
