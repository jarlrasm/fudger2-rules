# Playing GURPS Lite

## Success rolls
Roll 3d6 and try to roll **at or under** the skill or attribute. 3 or 4 is always a critical success, and 17 or 18 a critical failure. Modifiers raise or lower the target, not the roll. Margin of success is how far under the target the roll was.

Attributes: ST (strength), DX (dexterity), IQ (intelligence), HT (health), with Will and Perception based on IQ.

## Quick contests
Both sides roll their skill. The one with the better margin of success wins.

## Combat
1. Turns go in order of Basic Speed.
2. An attack rolls the weapon skill. The defender may then roll an active defence: Dodge, Block or Parry. Success avoids the hit.
3. Damage is rolled from the weapon (based on ST for muscle weapons). Subtract the Damage Resistance of the hit location. What is left comes off HP. Cutting and impaling wounds hurt more.
4. Below 1/3 HP a character moves and dodges at half. At 0 HP they roll HT each turn to stay conscious, and at -1 x HP they roll HT to avoid death.

## Movement
A turn is one second, and a hex is one yard. `Move` under Encumbrance (Basic Move, lowered by the load) is how many hexes a character can move in a turn.
- The Move manoeuvre goes up to the full Move, and sprinting in a straight line adds a bit more.
- Attacking allows a step of one hex. A Move and Attack goes up to full Move but with a heavy penalty to hit.
- Below 1/3 HP or FP, Move is halved.
- Bad footing costs extra per hex.

## Encumbrance
The sheet works out Encumbrance from carried weight. Higher levels lower Move and Dodge.
