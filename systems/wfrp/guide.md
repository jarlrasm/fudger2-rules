# Playing Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay (4th edition)

## Rolling
Tests are d100 **at or under** a skill (characteristic plus advances) or a characteristic. Each test asks for the difficulty modifier: Very Easy 60, Easy 40, Average 20, Challenging 0, Difficult −10, Hard −20, Very Hard −30. Leave it blank when only the GM knows it: the roll then shows the Success Levels against the bare skill, and each 10 of modifier moves them by one.

- **Success Levels (SL):** the tens digit of the target minus the tens digit of the roll. The roll shows it. Dramatic tests care about SL; simple tests only about pass or fail.
- 01–05 always succeed and 96–00 always fail.
- **Opposed tests:** both roll, and the higher SL wins.
- **Doubles** (11, 22 …): a critical on a hit in combat, a fumble on a miss.
- **Fortune** points reroll a test or add +1 SL. **Resolve** points shrug off a condition or ignore a Critical Wound. **Fate** and **Resilience** are burnt to survive death or remove a mutation.

## Combat
Act in Initiative order (roll the Initiative field to join the tracker; it uses the I characteristic).

1. The attacker tests a melee skill or WS (or a ranged skill or BS) from the Attacks list; the defender opposes with a melee skill or Dodge. The higher SL hits.
2. Damage = weapon damage (melee weapons add the Strength Bonus, already shown) + the winner's SL − the target's Toughness Bonus − armour on the hit location (the units and tens of the roll reversed). At least 1 Wound.
3. Winning a test gives **Advantage** (+10 to tests per point); losing or taking a wound clears it.
4. At 0 Wounds a character falls **Prone**, and further damage causes Critical Wounds.
5. Conditions stack as numbers: each level of Stunned, Fatigued and so on gives −10 to tests. **Bleeding** loses a Wound each round.

## Style
The Old World is grim and the characters are ordinary people out of their depth. Let fights be short and dangerous, money scarce, and Chaos close.
