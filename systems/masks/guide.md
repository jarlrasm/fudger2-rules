# Playing Masks: A New Generation

## Labels
Young heroes are defined by five **labels**, from −2 (or −3) to +3 (or +4): **Danger**, **Freak**, **Savior**, **Superior** and **Mundane**. They describe how the hero sees themself right now. When someone with **influence** over a hero tells them who they are, one label shifts up and another down. A label pushed past +3 or −2 means marking a condition and taking a step back.

## Moves
When a hero does something that triggers a move, roll **2d6 + label**:
- **10+**: they do it.
- **7–9**: they do it, with a cost or a hard choice.
- **6−**: a miss: mark **potential** and the GM makes a move. Five potential gives an advancement.

Forward on the sheet adds to the next roll. The basic moves:
- **Directly Engage a Threat** (Danger), **Unleash Your Powers** (Freak), **Defend Someone** (Savior), **Assess the Situation** and **Provoke Someone** (Superior), **Comfort or Support** and **Pierce the Mask** (Mundane).
- **Take a Powerful Blow**: roll 2d6 **plus** the conditions marked. Here a high roll is bad: on a miss the hero stands strong.

## Conditions
There are no hit points. Heroes mark conditions, each giving −2 to certain moves (the sheet subtracts it):
- **Afraid**: Directly Engage a Threat
- **Angry**: Comfort or Support, Pierce the Mask
- **Guilty**: Provoke Someone, Assess the Situation
- **Hopeless**: Unleash Your Powers
- **Insecure**: Defend Someone, Reject Influence

Each condition is cleared by a specific action (running away, breaking something, apologising…). A hero with all five marked is taken out.

## The team
The **Team pool** holds points the heroes spend to help each other (+1 to a roll) or act selflessly. NPC villains have conditions and drives instead of stats; when they mark enough conditions or the story says so, they are taken out.

## Style
Masks is about teenage heroes finding out who they are. Make the adults tell them who to be, give villains reasons, and let feelings matter as much as fights.
