# Warhammer Age of Sigmar: Soulbound

## Tests
A test rolls a pool of d6s: the attribute (Body, Mind or Soul) plus the skill's Training (0 to 3). The GM sets a **Difficulty Number** written as target:complexity, such as 4:2. Every die at or above the target is a success, and the test needs as many successes as the complexity. Typical targets: 2 very easy, 3 easy, 4 normal, 5 hard, 6 very hard. **Focus** (0 to 3) is spent after the roll: each point adds 1 to a die, so the sheet lifts the nearest misses into successes. Successes beyond the complexity make the result better. The sheet asks for bonus or penalty dice and the DN; with no DN it counts successes at 4+.

## Combat
Initiative is a fixed number (Mind plus Awareness and Reflexes Training); highest acts first, and players and foes alternate. Each turn a character can move and take an action; Mettle buys extra actions and heroic feats and refills each round.

Attacks compare the attacker's **Melee** (or **Accuracy** at range) with the target's **Defence**, each rated Poor, Average, Good, Great, Superb or Extraordinary. Equal ratings give DN 4:1; one step better 3:1, two or more 2:1; one step worse 5:1, two or more 6:1. A hit deals the weapon's damage plus 1 per extra success, less the target's Armour. Damage comes off **Toughness**; at 0 Toughness hits cause wounds (minor, serious, deadly), and when the wounds carried (minor 1, serious 2, deadly 3 each) reach Max Wounds, half of Body + Mind + Soul rounded up, the character falls. Minions fall to any damage.

Ranges are zones: Close is the same zone, Short the next one, then Medium and Long.

## Resources
**Mettle** is per character. The party shares **Soulfire** for heroic deeds (re-roll, extra damage, ignore a wound). The GM tracks **Doom**, which rises as Chaos gains ground and makes foes nastier. Keep Soulfire and Doom in the campaign's notes.

## Rest
After a fight, a breather restores Toughness and Mettle. A day of rest heals minor wounds; serious and deadly wounds need Medicine and time.

## Magic and miracles
Spells are cast with Channelling (Soul) against the spell's DN; miracles are invoked with Devotion. Extra successes can overcast the spell. Failing badly with magic can bring a Miscast.
