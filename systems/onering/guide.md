# Playing The One Ring

## Rolls
Roll the **Feat die** (d12) and a number of **Success dice** (d6) equal to the skill or proficiency rating. Add them up and compare with the **Target Number**: 20 minus the attribute the skill uses (Strength, Heart or Wits). The sheet works the TN out.

- The **Rune** (12) on the Feat die is an automatic success. The **Eye** (11) counts as 0. When the Loremaster rolls for an adversary it is the other way round: the Eye succeeds and the Rune counts 0.
- Each **6** on a Success die makes a success better: one is a great success, two or more an extraordinary success.
- **Favoured** skills roll two Feat dice and keep the best; **ill-favoured** rolls keep the worst. Hold Shift or Ctrl, or use the Roll slider.
- **Weary** heroes count Success dice of 1 to 3 as 0. A hero is weary when Endurance drops to Load plus Fatigue plus Treasure or lower. **Miserable** heroes fail whenever the Eye comes up; a hero is miserable when Shadow plus Shadow Scars reaches Hope.
- Spend 1 **Hope** to add a Success die (two when Inspired). Rolls ask for these Hope dice; take the Hope off the sheet yourself.

## Combat
Each round heroes choose a stance: forward, open, defensive or rearward. Forward heroes act first, then open, defensive and rearward; adversaries act after the heroes. The sheet's Stance sets the hero's Initiative. Forward attacks are favoured and defensive ones ill-favoured.

1. A hero attacks with a combat proficiency. The TN is their **Strength TN plus the enemy's Parry**; the sheet asks for Hope dice and the Parry. Brawling uses the best proficiency less one die.
2. A hit deals the weapon's Damage in Endurance. A Feat die of 10 or the Rune on a hit (10 or the Eye for an adversary) is a **Piercing Blow**: the target rolls Armour (plus a helm) against the weapon's Injury, or is wounded.
3. An adversary's attack TN is the hero's **Parry**. Adversaries spend Hate or Resolve on fell abilities.
4. At 0 Endurance a hero drops unconscious. A wounded hero hit again by a piercing blow is dying.

## Journeys and the Shadow
Journeys use roles (guide, hunter, look-out, scout) and Travel rolls to resist fatigue. Shadow points come from dread, greed, misdeeds and sorcery; when Shadow reaches Hope a hero is **Miserable**, and a bout of madness may follow.

## Fellowship
Heroes share a fellowship pool to recover Hope. Councils, audiences and hard choices matter as much as fighting.

## Style
Middle-earth is fading. Make victories small and costly, the road long, and kindness rewarded.
