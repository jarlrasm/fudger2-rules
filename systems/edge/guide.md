# Playing Over the Edge

## Traits
Characters are free-form: a main trait, side traits, and a trouble, all written in plain words. Fight, Sway and Weird are rolled directly.

## Rolling
Roll 2d6 and add them. For a trait that covers what the character does, the gamemaster may allow more dice and keep the best two. A higher total is better.

Set a difficulty by how hard the task is, about 7 for ordinary, 9 for hard, 11 for very hard. In conflicts, both sides roll and the higher total wins.

## Harm
Mark Strikes when a character is hurt. Three strikes take a character out of the action. Let a trouble cause problems now and then: it is the hook that makes the character interesting.

## Movement
There are no distances in the rules. Movement is told as story: say who can reach whom before something happens, and use a Fight roll for a contested chase. On a battlemap, about 5 squares a round of walking and 15 of running is a fair rule of thumb if a square is a metre.

## Style
The setting is a strange island where conspiracies overlap. Keep the weirdness up and reward odd ideas.
