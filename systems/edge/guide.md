# Playing Over the Edge

## Traits
Characters are free-form: a main trait, side traits, and a trouble, all written in plain words. Fight, Sway and Weird are rolled directly.

## Rolling
Traits are rated in dice: 2 is ordinary, 3 good, 4 exceptional. Roll that many d6 for the trait that covers what the character does and add them all; a character with no fitting trait rolls 2. A higher total is better. Clicking a trait or Fight, Sway or Weird rolls its dice.

An edge, such as good gear, help or a clever plan, gives a bonus die: roll one more d6 and drop the lowest. A handicap, such as a wound or bad footing, gives a penalty die: roll one more d6 and drop the highest. A bonus and a penalty cancel. Set the Roll slider under Basics to Bonus die or Penalty die before rolling (or hold Shift or Ctrl), and the roll drops the right die and says which.

Set a difficulty by how hard the task is, about 7 for ordinary, 9 for hard, 11 for very hard. Each roll asks for it and says whether the total meets it. In conflicts, leave it blank: both sides roll and the higher total wins.

## Harm
Mark Strikes when a character is hurt. Three strikes take a character out of the action. Let a trouble cause problems now and then: it is the hook that makes the character interesting.

## Movement
There are no distances in the rules. Movement is told as story: say who can reach whom before something happens, and use a Fight roll for a contested chase. On a battlemap, about 5 squares a round of walking and 15 of running is a fair rule of thumb if a square is a metre.

## Style
The setting is a strange island where conspiracies overlap. Keep the weirdness up and reward odd ideas.
