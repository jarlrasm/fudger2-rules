# Playing Knave (2nd edition)

## The game
Knave is an old-school dungeon and wilderness game. Characters have no classes: six ability bonuses, two careers and what they carry. Treasure brought home is experience. The GM describes a dangerous world and asks for rolls only when an action is risky and can't be settled by thinking it through.

## Checks
Roll **d20 plus the ability bonus** (0 to 10) against **11 plus a difficulty** of 0 to 10, 5 when unsure (so 16). Against a creature, the difficulty is its matching ability, or its level. Each **advantage** is +5 and each **disadvantage** -5; they cancel out. The check roll asks the net advantages (+1, -1, ...) and the difficulty. A career that fits gives +5 outside combat.

- STR: melee, lifting, grappling. DEX: dodging, sneaking, deft hands. CON: toughness, poison and cold; item slots are 10 + CON.
- INT: lore, alchemy, locks; spells a day. WIS: ranged attacks, noticing, foraging, resisting magic. CHA: persuasion, leadership, initiative, blessings and companions.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: the sides' leaders roll CHA against each other; each side acts together. Surprise is WIS against WIS.
- A round is a move (40') and an action.
- **Attacks**: melee d20 + STR, ranged d20 + WIS (-5 while in melee), against the target's **AC** (11 + armour points). Monsters, hirelings and NPCs add their level. A **natural 1 breaks the weapon**. A total of **21 or more** also allows a free maneuver (disarm, trip, push).
- **Damage**: the weapon die (punch d2). A **power attack** rolls the damage dice twice and breaks the weapon. A **sneak attack** on an unaware foe always hits and does **direct damage**, which skips HP. Fire is direct damage too.
- **Armour**: seven pieces (gambeson, helmet, shield, mail, breastplate, arm and leg plate), one armour point and one slot each, so AC 18 at most.

## Hit points and wounds
Max HP starts at a d6. Once HP is gone, every further point of damage fills an **item slot with a wound**, from the last slot upwards; what was in it is dropped. A PC with every slot wounded dies. Monsters die at 0 HP.
**Night of rest**: two watches of sleep after a meal restore all HP and the day's spells. **Safe haven**: a night in a safe place also heals one wound.

## Item slots
10 + CON slots. Most items take one; two-handed weapons two; spellbooks and relics one each; 500 coins one. Going over the slots leaves the character over-encumbered (slowed; the GM decides).

## Magic
A **spellbook** holds one spell. A character can cast up to **INT spells a day**, and each book once a day. Spell power grows with INT; as a guide, damage spells deal INT × d6. Targets may resist with WIS. **Relics** grant blessings while carried, at most CHA active.

## Levels
XP comes from treasure brought back to safety: 2,000 for level 2, doubling each level to 500,000 for level 10. On a new level, add 1 to three different abilities and use the **HP reroll** (level × d6; if not higher than the old max, the max goes up by 1).

## Hirelings and morale
Hirelings cost 300 coins a month, carry 10 slots and avoid danger; mercenaries fight. Monsters and hirelings check **morale** on 2d6: above their Morale they break and flee. Companions never check morale; a character can have at most CHA companions in a lifetime.

## Sheet fields that matter
Abilities (bonus and check roll), HP, Wounds, AC, Slots and Slots used, Spells cast, and the Attacks list built from carried weapons.
