# D&D 5e Gameplay Toolbox

Optional rules from the Gameplay Toolbox chapter of the SRD 5.2.1: traps, poisons, curses, magical contagions, and fear and mental stress. The library holds every trap, poison, curse and contagion the chapter gives as an example.

## Traps
A trap is a library item. Its sheet shows the severity (nuisance or deadly) for a range of levels, the trigger, the duration, the save and DC, and the check and DC that find it. **Damage roll** rolls the damage and says what a save does: half for traps that say so, none for the rest. **Higher levels** lists stronger versions with their own damage roll; use the row that fits the party, since a nuisance trap for high levels can kill low-level characters. The Poisoned Darts damage is per dart: roll 1d3 for the number of darts, then the damage once per dart.

Finding a trap is usually a Search action (Wisdom (Perception)) or a Study action (Intelligence (Investigation)) against the Detect DC. The description says how to disarm, avoid or escape it. Use traps rarely, and give the party a fair way to notice and beat them.

## Poisons
Add a poison to a character or creature, whether they carry a dose or suffer from it. The four types are contact (bare skin), ingested (a whole dose swallowed; a partial dose can give Advantage or half damage), inhaled (a 5-foot cube of powder or gas; holding your breath doesn't help) and injury (put on a weapon or ammunition as a Bonus Action, delivered by Piercing or Slashing damage). The poison's sheet rolls its damage and lists the conditions a failed save gives; tick those under Conditions.

Harvesting poison from a dead or Incapacitated venomous creature takes 1d6 minutes and a DC 20 Intelligence (Nature) check with a Poisoner's Kit. Failing by 5 or more exposes the harvester to the poison.

## Curses and magical contagions
Add a curse or contagion to the character or creature it affects. Bestow Curse is the yardstick for curse strength: one that lasts a minute is like a level 3 spell, one that lasts until dispelled like a level 9 spell. Narrative curses come from breaking a taboo; the victim should know why and be able to learn how to end it.

A contagion's sheet gives the onset, how to fight it off and how it spreads. Three days of rest that wouldn't break a Long Rest, then a DC 15 Constitution save, give Advantage on saves against a contagion for 24 hours. Count successful saves and Exhaustion on the sheet (Conditions, Stress notes or the character's notes).

## Affliction saves
Every attached poison, curse and contagion with a save DC gets a row under **Affliction saves**. Its roll uses the character's saving throw for that ability, the sheet's Advantage or Disadvantage and the Exhaustion penalty, and shows Success or Failure against the DC.

## Fear and mental stress
Discuss these with the players before using them.
- **Fear save:** a Wisdom save against something supernaturally frightful. The roll asks for the DC: 10 for a harmless but scary apparition, 15 for a trap that shows a character's worst fear, 20 for a glimpse into a hellish realm. A failure gives the Frightened condition; the creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns. The GM may add more: it must Dash away, attacks against it have Advantage, or it can only move, act or take a Bonus Action each turn.
- **Mental stress save:** a Wisdom save (sometimes Intelligence or Charisma) against intense mental strain. Pick DC 10 with 1d6, DC 15 with 3d6 or DC 20 with 9d6 psychic damage. A failure deals the damage; on a success the GM may allow half instead of none.
- **Prolonged effect:** set it when stress lingers. Short-term: Frightened, Incapacitated or Stunned for 1d10 minutes. Long-term: Disadvantage on some or all ability checks for 1d10 x 10 hours. Indefinite: like long-term until Greater Restoration. Calm Emotions suppresses any of them, and Lesser Restoration ends the short and long ones. **Effect duration** rolls how long it lasts; write what it does in Stress notes.
