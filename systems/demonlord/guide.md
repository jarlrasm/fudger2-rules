# Playing Shadow of the Demon Lord

## The game
The world is ending. The Demon Lord presses against reality, the empire is collapsing and the dead walk. The characters start as ordinary people (level 0) and grow into heroes, or are broken by madness and corruption. Play is fast and deadly, and a whole campaign is often about ten adventures long.

## Rolls
- A **challenge roll** is d20 + attribute modifier (attribute - 10). **10 or more succeeds.**
- **Boons and banes**: circumstances add boons (good) or banes (bad). They cancel one for one. Roll a d6 for each one left; only the **highest** counts, added for boons and subtracted for banes. Set Boons on the sheet before rolling (negative for banes); diseased, fatigued, frightened, impaired and poisoned each add a bane on their own.
- An **attack roll** must reach the target's Defense, or the attribute the attack names. A total of **20 or more that beats the target by 5** deals an extra 1d6 damage.

## Turns
No initiative roll. Each round, a character picks a **fast turn** (one action or a move, before the enemies) or a **slow turn** (an action and a move, after the enemies' fast turns).

## Damage and healing
Damage adds up against **Health**. At half Health you are **injured**; with damage equal to Health you are **incapacitated** and make a **fate roll** (d6) at the end of each round: 1 you die, 6 you heal 1 damage and wake up. Taking more damage while incapacitated kills you.

- **Catch your breath** once a day to heal your **healing rate** (a quarter of Health).
- A night's **rest** heals your healing rate again, ends fatigue and restores spell castings.

## Insanity and corruption
Horror adds **Insanity**; when it reaches your Will, you go mad for a while. Dark deeds and dark magic add **Corruption**, which leaves marks of darkness and changes your fate when you die.

## Magic
Magicians and priests learn spells from **traditions**. **Power** sets how many castings each spell rank gets per rest. Attack spells use an attack roll, usually Intellect or Will against a defense or attribute.
