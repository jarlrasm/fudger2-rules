# Playing Mausritter

## The game
The players are mice: small, brave and desperate adventurers in a world built for bigger creatures. A spoon is a treasure, a cat is a feudal lord, a crow is a sorcerer. There are no classes or skill lists. Players describe what their mice do and the GM says what happens. Make the world feel huge, give clear warning of danger, and reward clever, bold play. A good plan often needs no roll at all.

## Saves
Ask for a **save** only when an action is risky, the outcome uncertain and failure has a cost: **d20 equal to or under the current STR, DEX or WIL**. There is no automatic success or failure on a 1 or 20. **Advantage** rolls 2d20 and keeps the lowest, **disadvantage** the highest; the save roll asks which. **Injured** gives disadvantage on STR and DEX saves, **Drained** on WIL saves, and being **encumbered** on every save; the sheet adds these itself. In an **opposed save** both sides roll and the lowest success wins. For chance outside any save, make a **luck roll**: an X-in-6 chance on a d6 (on the creature sheet).

Tell the players what failing costs: d4 to d20 damage (d6 is dangerous, d8 can kill a new mouse), a Condition (Exhausted or Frightened), lost time, a lost item, or usage marked on gear.

## Combat
- **Order**: ambushers go first. Otherwise each mouse makes a DEX save (the Initiative roll): **Ahead** acts before the enemies, **Behind** after them. Keep the order.
- A turn is a move of up to 12" and one action. Squares on an unscaled map are 6".
- **Attacks always hit**: roll the weapon die and take off the target's **Armour**. The attack roll asks if it is **impaired** (d4: into cover, while grappled) or **enhanced** (d12: a good gambit or a helpless target), then the target's Armour. Medium weapons do d6 in one paw, d8 in both; two light weapons roll both dice and keep the best.
- Damage comes off **HP** first, then off **STR**. After STR damage the target makes a **STR save**; on a failure it takes **critical damage**: it gets the **Injured** Condition and is **incapacitated** until an ally tends it and it takes a short rest. Untended for 6 Turns, it dies. Creatures with their own critical damage use that instead.
- STR 0 is death, DEX 0 means unable to move, WIL 0 insensible.
- Enemies make a WIL save for **morale** when outmatched, on their first critical damage, and when an ally flees or falls.
- **Warbands** (20 or more mice) fight warband-scale beasts like cats. They ignore damage from single creatures unless it is huge, and their attacks on them are enhanced. A warband that takes critical damage is broken; at half STR it saves WIL or routs.

## Inventory and Conditions
Ten slots: two **paw**, two **body**, six **pack**. Getting something from the pack in a fight takes the action. Two-handed weapons and armour take two slots. **Conditions** take a slot each until cleared. More items than slots means **encumbered**: no running, all saves at disadvantage (the sheet's Over the limit list). Most items have **three usage dots**: after a fight roll d6 per weapon, armour or ammunition used, marking one on 4-6; torches every 6 Turns; rations after a meal.

The usual Conditions: **Exhausted** (clears after a long rest), **Frightened** (short rest), **Hungry** (a meal), **Injured** and **Drained** (full rest).

## Rest
- **Short rest** (a Turn): d6+1 HP back, clears Frightened.
- **Long rest** (a Watch, with a meal): all HP back, clears Exhausted, Frightened and Hungry. If HP was already full, d6 back to one attribute.
- **Full rest** (a week in safety, about 20p): HP and attributes restored, most Conditions cleared.
The sheet's buttons do the clearing.

## Magic
Spells are spirits held in carved tablets, carried in a slot. Pick a **power** up to the usage dots left and roll that many d6: **[DICE]** is the power and **[SUM]** the total. Each 4-6 marks a usage dot; when all are marked the spell is depleted until its recharge is done. Each **6** is a **miscast**: d6 WIL damage per six, then a WIL save or the **Drained** Condition. The Cast button in a spell's slot rolls all of this.

## Advancement
1 XP per pip of treasure brought home to a settlement, and 1 per 10 pips spent for the community. Levels at 1,000, 3,000 and 6,000 XP, then every 5,000. On a new Level roll d20 per attribute (higher raises it by 1) and the new Hit Dice: higher than HP replaces it, otherwise HP goes up by 1. From Level 2 a mouse has **Grit**: that many Conditions can be put in the Grit space and ignored until cleared.

## Hirelings and factions
Hirelings cost a day of asking and a WIL save (or 20p); they have six slots and make WIL saves for morale. Factions roll d6 between sessions, plus their relevant resources, minus a targeted rival's: 4-5 is one progress mark, 6+ two.
