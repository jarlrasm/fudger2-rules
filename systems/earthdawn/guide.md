# Earthdawn 4th edition

## Steps and tests
Every test rolls a **step**: a combination of dice. Step 3 is d4, 4 is d6, 5 is d8, 6 is d10, 7 is d12, 8 is 2d6, 9 is d8+d6, 10 is 2d8, 11 is d10+d8, 12 is 2d10, 13 is d12+d10, 14 is 2d12, and from 13 up each 7 steps adds another d12. Every die explodes: a maximum roll is rolled again and added. An attribute's value gives its step (10 to 12 is step 5). Talents and skills add their rank to an attribute's step. The total is compared with a **Target Number** set by the GM or by a Defense: meet it to succeed, and each full 5 above it is an extra success. If every die shows 1 (the **Rule of One**) the test fails whatever the total. The sheet asks for a step bonus or penalty (wounds are already taken off) and the Target Number.

## Combat
Everyone rolls Initiative (Dexterity step, minus armor's initiative penalty and 1 per wound) every round, highest first. Attacks use Dexterity plus Melee Weapons, Missile Weapons, Throwing Weapons or Unarmed Combat against the target's **Physical Defense**; spells usually go against **Mystic Defense**, and social talents against **Social Defense**. Extra successes on an attack add 2 to damage each, or let an armor-defeating hit ignore Physical Armor. Damage is Strength step plus the weapon's damage step, less Physical Armor (Mystic Armor against magic). A single hit that exceeds the **Wound Threshold** after armor also causes a wound: −1 to all tests per wound, and a Knockdown test against the damage above the threshold. At the Unconsciousness Rating the character falls; at the Death Rating they die.

## Strain, Karma and recovery
Some talents cost Strain: damage the adept takes to use them. Karma (a d6, step 4) is added to tests the Discipline allows, one point at a time: hold Shift while rolling (or set **Spend Karma** on the sheet) to add the yellow Karma die, then lower Karma by 1. A character has a few **Recovery tests** a day: roll the Toughness step and remove that much damage (a wound needs a full Recovery test to remove). A night's rest refreshes Recovery tests and Karma.

## Magic
Magicians weave threads (a thread-weaving test per thread) before casting; casting is a Spellcasting test against the target's Mystic Defense. Raw magic, without threads, risks the attention of Horrors.

## Creatures
Creatures list attributes as steps, Initiative, Defenses, Armor, Unconsciousness and Death Ratings, Wound Threshold, Knockdown and attacks as attack and damage steps, plus powers.
