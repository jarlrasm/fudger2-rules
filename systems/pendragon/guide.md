# Playing King Arthur Pendragon 6th edition

## The game
The players are knights in the Britain of King Arthur. A campaign spans decades: each year brings adventure, a Winter Phase of family, estate and ageing, and in time the knight's heir rides out instead. What matters is Glory, and the personality the dice give the knight: traits and passions often decide what a knight does, not the player.

## Rolling
Everything is **1d20 against a value**:
- Below the value: success. **Equal** to it: a **critical**. A natural **20** is a **fumble** (unless the value is 20).
- A value over 20 adds the excess to the roll; a total of 20 or more is a critical, and it cannot fail.
- Each roll asks for a **modifier**, such as +5 for an easy task or -10 for a hard one. Leave it at 0 otherwise.
- **Opposed rolls** (combat, contests): both roll; a success beats a failure, and between two successes the higher roll wins. A critical beats a plain success. A winner with a lower success than the loser's is a partial success.

## Traits and passions
- Thirteen **trait pairs** (Valorous/Cowardly and so on) always add to 20. When a choice tests a knight's nature, the GM calls for a roll: success means the knight acts by the trait; failure means rolling its opposite. 16+ is famous.
- **Passions** (Loyalty, Love, Hate, Honor, Hospitality) can be rolled to be **inspired** (+5 to a related skill or trait for the scene) or, on a critical, **impassioned** (+10). A failure is a crisis: the knight may be overcome or despair.
- Successful rolls in play tick **Check** boxes; the knight may improve them in the Winter Phase.

## Combat and damage
- Each round both fighters roll their weapon skill opposed. The winner rolls **damage** (STR+SIZ / 6 in d6, plus or minus the weapon's extra dice). A critical hit adds **4d6**.
- The loser's **armor** protection comes off the damage; on a partial success the shield's protection does too.
- Damage above **Knockdown** (SIZ) calls for a DEX roll to stay up.
- A single wound of **Major Wound** (CON) or more knocks the knight out and marks them **Debilitated**. At or below **Unconscious** they fall; at 0 hit points they are mortally wounded and die by midnight without First Aid.
- **First Aid** on a fresh wound restores the healing rate (double on a critical). Each week a knight regains the healing rate, unless Debilitated: that needs a successful Chirurgery first. The sheet's *Week of healing* button adds it.
- Initiative only matters when actions clash: then an opposed DEX roll decides.

## Glory
Glory comes from battles, tournaments, deeds and courtly skill. The *Glory roll* field rolls against Glory / 1,000 for being recognized.
