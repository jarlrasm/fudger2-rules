# Dungeon World content

`dungeonworld.json` contains moves, classes, equipment and monsters from *Dungeon World* by Sage LaTorra and Adam Koebel, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The data comes from the Dungeon World Data project (https://github.com/Vindexus/DungeonWorldData) and was converted with `tools/dungeonworld_import.py`, changing format and structure only.
