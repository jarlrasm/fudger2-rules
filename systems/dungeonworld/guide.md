# Playing Dungeon World

## Moves
Players describe what their characters do. When that matches a **move**, they roll **2d6 + the stat** the move names:

- **10+** strong hit: they do it.
- **7–9** weak hit: they do it, but with a cost, a hard choice or a complication.
- **6−** miss: they mark XP, and the GM makes a move, as hard as they like.

Forward (+1 to the next roll) and Ongoing (a bonus that lasts) are on the sheet and added to every roll; clear Forward after it is used. **Debilities** (Weak, Shaky, Sick, Stunned, Confused, Scarred) take 1 off that stat's modifier.

## The GM's job
Describe the situation, follow the rules, make moves when the players miss or give you a golden opportunity, and ask questions. GM moves include revealing an unwelcome truth, dealing damage, separating them, using up their resources, and showing signs of an approaching threat. Never say the name of your move; just make it happen in the fiction.

## Fighting
There is no initiative and no turns: the spotlight moves between players.

- **Hack and Slash** (STR, or DEX with a precise weapon) in melee: on a 10+ deal damage and avoid the enemy's attack, or take it to deal +1d6. On a 7–9 deal damage and the enemy attacks you.
- **Volley** (DEX) at range: on a 7–9 choose to be in danger, lose ammo, or deal less damage.
- Damage rolls the class's damage die (weapons tagged damage +1 add to it), minus the target's armor. Monster damage is written like d10+2, b[2d8] (roll two, keep the best) or w[2d6] (keep the worst); the sheet's Damage Roll reads it. A weapon tagged both close or reach and near or far gets a Hack and Slash and a Volley row. At 0 HP make the **Last Breath** move.

## Advancement
Mark XP on every miss. At the end of a session, mark XP for alignment and bonds. When XP fills the track (level + 7), level up during a rest.

## Style
Play to find out what happens. Fill the characters' lives with adventure, make the world fantastic, and be a fan of the characters.
