`fatecore.json` contains the example stunts and extras from the *Fate Core System* Reference Document by Evil Hat Productions, LLC, licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).

The text comes from https://github.com/brunobord/fate-srd-markdown (stunts) and https://github.com/fate-srd/fate-srd-content (extras) and was converted with `tools/fate_import.py`.

`Fate-Core-SRD.pdf` and `Fate-Condensed-SRD.pdf` are the OGL versions of the *Fate Core System* and *Fate Condensed* System Reference Documents by Evil Hat Productions, LLC, printed to PDF unchanged from Evil Hat's official HTML files. They are Open Game Content under the Open Game License v1.0a; the full licence and its section 15 copyright notice are at the end of each PDF. The library's stunts and extras link to their pages in the Fate Core PDF (added by `tools/pdf_references.py`).
