`Fate-Core-SRD.pdf` and `Fate-Condensed-SRD.pdf` are the OGL versions of the *Fate Core System* and *Fate Condensed* System Reference Documents by Evil Hat Productions, LLC, printed to PDF unchanged from Evil Hat's official HTML files. The library's stunts and extras link to their pages in the Fate Core PDF (added by `tools/pdf_references.py`).
