# Venture City superpowers

Based on the power rules of the Venture City SRD. Characters are made as in Fate Core, plus a power suite. Powers use the ordinary Fate skill rolls; a power changes which skill applies, adds a bonus, or allows something no one else can do.

## Building a suite
- Every player character gets **three power stunts** that can only be spent on powers, on top of the usual free stunts and refresh. Main NPCs are built the same way; supporting NPCs get three or four stunts of powers, nameless NPCs one or two and no special or collateral damage effects.
- Most characters have one power suite, two at most. A suite can do many related things.
- **Basic power:** one stunt. Pick it from the Powers library.
- **Enhancement:** one stunt each. Master enhancements (usually +2 to a roll) can be bought again and again.
- **Power synergy:** another basic power folded into the same suite, one stunt. The suggested synergies are only suggestions if the player can explain the link.
- **Power theme:** colours the suite (fire, shadow, technology). The first is free, each further theme one stunt.
- **Special effects:** the suite starts with two from the general list (forced movement, area attack, inflict condition, extra movement, physical or mental recovery, extra action). Each further stunt or refresh buys two more. **Improved special effects** belong to one power and cost one stunt each.
- **Drawback:** pick one aspect from the suite's powers or make one up. It works like a second trouble.
- **Collateral damage effect:** pick one from the suite's powers, or build one from a power the character doesn't have.

On the sheet, set **Enhancements bought** and **Improved effects bought** on each attached power, and **Effect Buys** in Power Budget. Power Budget spends the power stunts first, then the free stunts, then refresh, one per stunt, and shows the refresh left; **Set Refresh** copies it to Refresh. Power stunts left unspent are lost, and refresh shouldn't go below 1.

## In play
- Roll the skill the power names, with its bonuses, against the opposition as usual. Add the bonuses from Master enhancements by hand (invoking is still +2 on top).
- **Special effect:** on a success with style using a power, take a special effect instead of the boost. A fate point adds one to any success, even one that already has a special effect. Effects come on top of the normal result.
- **Collateral damage:** the character may use it at any time. The GM then puts a situation aspect on the area for the damage done (record it in Situation Aspects); it can be invoked or compelled like any other.
- Drawbacks are compelled like trouble.
