# Playing Fate Core

## Rolling
Roll **4dF** (four Fudge dice, −4 to +4) and add a skill rating. Read the total on the ladder: Legendary +8, Epic +7, Fantastic +6, Superb +5, Great +4, Good +3, Fair +2, Average +1, Mediocre +0, Poor −1, Terrible −2.

- Compare to the opposition (a difficulty or the other side's roll). Each roll asks for the **Opposition** and names the outcome: **fail** (below), **tie**, **succeed** (1–2 shifts above), or **succeed with style** (3+). Pick the Opposition on the ladder, or leave it at Not set when only the GM knows it, and the roll shows just the result on the ladder.
- The four actions: **overcome** an obstacle, **create an advantage** (make or discover an aspect with free invokes), **attack**, and **defend**.

## Aspects and fate points
Aspects are short phrases that are always true.

- **Invoke** an aspect by spending a fate point (or a free invoke) for +2 or a reroll. A character's roll offers **Invoke for a reroll**, which rolls the four dice again and takes a fate point off the sheet (for a free invoke, add the point back).
- The GM **compels** an aspect to complicate a character's life. Accepting earns a fate point; refusing costs one.
- **Refresh** is how many fate points each player starts a session with.
- Track temporary aspects in Situation Aspects, with their free invokes.

## Conflict
Turn order in physical conflicts follows Notice, then Athletics, then Physique on a tie (roll the Initiative field to join the tracker in that order; its number is Notice x 10000 + Athletics x 100 + Physique).

1. An attack's shifts over the defence are hits that must be absorbed.
2. Absorb a hit by checking one stress box whose value is at least the shifts (boxes are worth 1, 2, 3 and 4), or by taking a **consequence**: mild absorbs 2, moderate 4, severe 6. A box and consequences can be combined for one hit. A consequence is a new aspect others can invoke.
3. A character who can't absorb a hit is **taken out**, and the attacker decides what happens. A character can **concede** before the roll to choose their own fate and earn fate points.
4. Stress clears at the end of a scene. Consequences take time to heal.

## Style
Characters are proactive, competent and dramatic. Make the aspects matter, say yes to good ideas, and let failure move the story forward.
