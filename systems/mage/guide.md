# Playing Mage: the Ascension (20th anniversary edition)

## The game
The Awakened know that reality is consensus: what people believe holds the world in shape. Mages bend it with will and practice, through rituals, prayer, science or code. The Traditions fight to keep wonder alive, the Technocracy to keep the world safe and predictable, and both fear the Nephandi and the mad Marauders. Every act of magick pushes against reality, and reality pushes back with **Paradox**.

## Rolls
Roll **attribute + ability** d10s against a **difficulty** (usually 6, from 3 easy to 9 very hard). Each die at or above the difficulty is a success; each **1** cancels one success. With no successes and any 1s, the roll is a **botch**. The prompt asks for dice added or taken off and the difficulty.
- One success is enough; more give better results.
- **Willpower**: spend a point before the roll for one automatic success that 1s can't cancel (hold Shift while rolling, then lower Willpower).
- Untrained: a Talent rolls the attribute alone, a Skill at +1 difficulty (the prompt starts at 7), a Knowledge not at all. The sheet does this for abilities with no dots.
- **Wound penalties** come off the pool: Hurt and Injured -1, Wounded and Mauled -2, Crippled -5.

## Magick
Roll **Arete**. The effect needs dots in the right **Spheres** (Correspondence, Entropy, Forces, Life, Matter, Mind, Prime, Spirit, Time).
- **Coincidental** magick (could pass as chance): difficulty = highest Sphere used + 3.
- **Vulgar** magick (obviously impossible): highest Sphere + 4, and it gains **Paradox**; more with Sleepers watching.
- **Wound penalties** apply to Arete too.
- Spending **Quintessence** lowers the difficulty by 1 a point (up to 3). Instruments and time help too.
- The Magick roll offers the difficulties 3 to 9 with the effects that give them; left unset it rolls at 6.
- The successes decide how strong, how long and how far the effect goes. A botch on vulgar magick brings a Paradox backlash.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: Dexterity + Wits + 1d10, highest first.
- **Attacks**: Dexterity + Brawl, Melee or Firearms (difficulty 6, or the weapon's; the prompt starts at the weapon's).
- **Damage**: roll the weapon's damage dice (Strength + bonus in melee), difficulty 6; each success is a health level. The target soaks with Stamina (bashing, and lethal with the right magick).
- **Health**: seven levels from Bruised to Incapacitated. Damage is bashing, lethal or aggravated.

## Recovery
Willpower returns by a point a night and by acting on the Nature. Bashing damage heals in hours, lethal in days, aggravated in weeks, unless Life magick helps.
