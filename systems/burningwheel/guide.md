# Playing Burning Wheel Gold

## The game
Burning Wheel is fantasy about characters who want things badly. Each player writes **Beliefs** (what the character stands for and means to do) and **Instincts**; the GM's job is to challenge those Beliefs. Rolls are rare and decisive: you say what you want and how, the GM sets the obstacle, and failure always moves the story somewhere interesting. Let it ride: a test isn't rolled again unless things change.

## Tests
Roll **d6s equal to the exponent** of the stat or skill.
- **Shade** sets what counts as a success: **black** 4-6, **grey** 3-6, **white** 2-6.
- Meet or beat the **Obstacle (Ob)** to succeed; extra successes are the margin. The roll asks for the Ob and for advantage dice or penalties.
- **Help** and FoRKs (related skills) add a die each; tools and circumstances add advantage dice or raise the Ob.
- **Beginner's Luck**: without the skill, roll the root stat at double Ob.
- **Wounds** are applied for you: each superficial wound adds +1 Ob; Light -1D, Midi -2D, Severe -3D, Traumatic -4D.

## Artha
- **Fate**: make a roll open-ended (6s roll again and can succeed). Hold Shift while rolling (or switch Spend Fate on).
- **Persona**: +1D a point (up to three), or tap Nature.
- **Deeds**: double the base dice, or reroll every failure.
Artha is earned at the end of a session for playing Beliefs, Instincts and Traits, even against the character's interest.

## Advancement
Log each test as **routine**, **difficult** or **challenging** against the exponent (the Tests column). Enough of the right kinds raise the exponent.

## Conflict
Big conflicts use **Duel of Wits** (arguments), **Range and Cover** (shooting and chases) or **Fight!** (melee). In Fight! each side scripts three actions a volley; **Reflexes** decides how many actions a fighter gets. Hits deal the weapon's **Incidental, Mark or Superb** damage by margin; armor dice can stop it. **Steel** tests check nerve: failing loses actions to Hesitation.

## Healing
Wounds take time and a Treatment test; there is no quick rest. Resources and Circles tests find gear and people.
