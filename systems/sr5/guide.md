# Playing Shadowrun (5th edition)

## Tests
Roll a pool of **d6s**: skill rating + linked attribute, plus or minus situational dice. Every **5 or 6 is a hit**. Each test asks for bonus or penalty dice and then the threshold: Easy 1, Average 2, Hard 4, Extreme 6 (leave it blank when only the GM knows, or for opposed tests the GM resolves).

- **Limits:** hits above the test's limit are lost. Physical = (Strength x 2 + Body + Reaction) / 3, Mental = (Logic x 2 + Intuition + Willpower) / 3, Social = (Charisma x 2 + Willpower + Essence) / 3, all rounded up. Weapons use their Accuracy, spells their Force, complex forms their Level, matrix skills the deck's Attack, Sleaze or Data Processing, vehicle tests Handling. The sheet caps hits for you and shows the limit.
- **Glitch:** more than half the dice show 1. A glitch with no hits is a **critical glitch**.
- **Defaulting:** an unrated skill rolls the attribute - 1, except skills that cannot default (magic, resonance and most technical skills).
- **Skill groups:** a group rating counts for every skill in the group.
- **Wound modifiers:** -1 die for every 3 boxes filled on each monitor, taken off every pool and Initiative (not damage resistance).
- **Opposed tests:** both sides roll; the active side needs more hits. Net hits measure how well it went.

## Edge
Edge is an attribute; Edge Points are spent and come back when the GM says (often at the start of a session).
- **Push the Limit:** before rolling, switch it on in Attributes (or hold Shift). The roll adds Edge dice that explode on a 6 and ignores the limit. Take the point off by hand. Initiative with it on is a **Blitz** (5D6).
- **Second Chance:** after a roll without Push the Limit, the roll window offers *Second Chance (1 Edge)*: the misses are rerolled and the point is spent. It cannot remove a glitch.
- Seize the Initiative, Close Call and Dead Man's Trigger are handled by hand.

## Combat
**Initiative** = Reaction + Intuition + 1D6 (more dice from wired reflexes and the like, 5D6 at most). Astral: Intuition x 2 + 2D6. Matrix VR: Data Processing + Intuition + 3D6 cold sim, 4D6 hot sim. Highest goes first; after each pass subtract 10 and those still above 0 go again.

1. **Attack:** weapon skill + Agility, capped by Accuracy, from the Attacks list (battlemap actions). The defender rolls **Defense** (Reaction + Intuition; Full Defense adds Willpower) with the attacker's hits as the threshold; matching them is a miss.
2. **Damage:** weapon DV + the attacker's net hits. If the modified DV is less than the target's armor after AP, Physical damage becomes Stun.
3. **Damage Resistance:** Body + Armor (armor less AP). It asks the AP and the modified DV and says how many boxes to mark.
4. Full Stun monitor: unconscious, and more stun spills into Physical. Full Physical: down and dying; overflow boxes up to Body, past that, death.

## Magic, Matrix and rigging
- **Spellcasting** + Magic, asking the Force (the limit). Resist Drain with Willpower + Logic (hermetic) or Charisma (shaman); Drain is Stun unless the hits beat Magic. Spirits are their own entity type: pick type and Force and the sheet works out attributes; Services are tracked.
- **Essence** starts at 6 and drops by each augmentation's cost; it caps Magic and Resonance and lowers the Social limit.
- **Matrix:** deck attributes Attack, Sleaze, Data Processing and Firewall are the limits of matrix skills. Watch the Overwatch Score.
- **Vehicles and drones** are their own entity type with Handling, Speed, Accel, Body, Armor, Pilot and Sensor. Autopilot tests are Pilot + autosoft; a rigger uses their own skill + Reaction (or Logic for jumped-in gunnery).

## Healing
Stun heals with rest (Body + Willpower per hour); Physical takes days (Body + Willpower per day) or First Aid and Medicine. The sheet has *Stun healed* and *Fully healed* buttons; enter partial healing by hand.

## Style
Professional criminals doing deniable jobs for megacorps, dragons and worse. Plan, do the legwork, run, get paid, and expect betrayal.
