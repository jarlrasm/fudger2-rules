# Fudge Psi

Based on the sample psionic system in the Fudge SRD (section 7.3).

## Powers and skills
Each psi power is bought on its own and added from the library, which holds the narrow powers from the SRD chart. Each power has two ratings:
- **Power:** how strong it is, such as range, weight or number of targets. Bought at Terrible, and raised two skill levels per step. Some uses need a minimum Power level. Three or more levels above that minimum gives +1.
- **Skill:** how well the psi controls it. It starts at Terrible and is at most Fair when play begins.

A power with no skill can't be used. **Psychic Reservoir** is the psi's raw energy, Fair by default.

## Using a power
The player describes the effect, and the GM sets a Difficulty and any minimum Power. Click the power's skill in Psi rolls. The roll asks for:
- **Psi modifiers:** +1 for double concentration time, -1 for half, +1 for Power 3+ above the minimum, -2 per level of Reservoir drained for desperation psionics, and drugs, devices or places.
- **Difficulty**, or Not set when only the GM knows it.

The Reservoir penalty is added on its own: Mediocre -1, Poor -2, Terrible -3. Below Terrible, psi can't be used at all.

An attack on an unwilling target is opposed by the target's Will, or by a psi defence such as Mind Shield. Ties keep things as they were. The psi still needs to meet the Difficulty.

**Terrible or worse** backfires, often as the opposite of what was wanted, and drops Psychic Reservoir one level. Lower it on the sheet.

## Augmenting
After a roll, **Augment** rolls the same skill again:
- Good: sacrifice 1 level of Reservoir for +1 to the result.
- Great: sacrifice up to 2 levels.
- Superb: sacrifice up to 3 levels.
- Fair to Poor: no change.
- Terrible or worse: lose a level of Reservoir and the result drops a level.

In a psionic duel, both sides take turns augmenting until one fails to change the result or drops below Terrible. A defender with no psi augments on Will -2.

## Desperation
A psi may drain Reservoir before the roll to raise Power by one level per level drained, enough to try a use their Power is too weak for. Each level drained gives -2 to the skill. Put it in Psi modifiers and lower the Reservoir on the sheet.

## Recovery
Below Terrible, the psi passes out (a Good Con roll each round wakes them) and may suffer afflictions until the Reservoir is back to Terrible. Reservoir comes back one level per week of rest, or faster in psi-rich games.
