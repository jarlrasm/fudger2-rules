# Fudge Magic

Based on the sample magic system in the Fudge SRD (section 7.1). It replaces the older Spells extension and keeps its Spells list.

## Who can cast
Only magicians, characters with at least one level of **Magic Potential**, can cast. Each level is bought for one specialization, such as Healing Magic, Illusion or Combat Magic. A spell must be governed by at least one specialization the magician has at +1 or more on the sheet. Broad specializations make strong magicians, so agree with the players how broad they may be.

**Spell casting** is a skill. It starts at Terrible when learned and can't be raised above Fair (the GM may allow Good for a gift).

## Casting
1. The player describes the effect. The GM names its **potency**: Trivial (Mediocre or Poor), Average (Fair), Potent (Good) or more (Great, Superb). It assumes three levels of fitting Potential: make the spell a step easier for each level above three, and a step harder for each below.
2. Add **casting modifiers**, at most up to Great: +1 for taking a long time, -1 for casting fast, +1 or +2 for extra effort (more Fatigue), +1 for real magical formulae or a physical component, +1 or +2 for several magicians who all know the spell, -1 for each casting of the same effect in this area in the last day, and +1 or -1 for rich or poor mana.
3. Click Spell casting. The roll asks for the modifiers and the potency.

Results:
- **Better than the potency:** the spell works as described, better with a higher margin. Usually -1 Fatigue.
- **Equal:** a watered-down version, such as a shorter, weaker or late effect, or a harmless side effect.
- **Worse:** the spell fails. The magician is stunned for a round (no action or defence) and loses at least 1 Fatigue.
- **Terrible** always fails.
- **A rolled -4** always fails, whatever the level. The magician faints and loses one level of Current Potential in a fitting specialization. A Good Con roll each round wakes them. At -1 Current Potential that specialization can't be used, the magician falls into a coma, and they take a Scratch on a failed Con roll each active day until it is back at 0.

A spell that controls a being (mind, body or spirit) then needs an opposed roll against the target's Will or magic resistance. A spell that must hit, such as a fire bolt, uses Throwing or a similar skill.

## Recovery
- **Fatigue** comes back with rest: press Rest. Below Terrible, the magician passes out.
- **Current Potential** comes back one level per week of rest, or per day in epic games. Set it by hand.

## Spells
Spells in the library are examples with a specialization, potency, casting time and duration. Players may try any effect; the GM sets the potency.
