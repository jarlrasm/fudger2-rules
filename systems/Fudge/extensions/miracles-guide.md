# Fudge Miracles

Based on the sample miracle system in the Fudge SRD (section 7.2).

## Divine Favor
Divine Favor is bought in steps, each from one deity. A character may have several steps from one deity, or favor from several deities in a polytheistic world. Add each deity under Divine Favor with its steps.

A character who acts against the deity loses all favor from that deity until they atone. Atonement can be instant for a merciful god or take a month for a strict one. Set Steps to 0 meanwhile.

## Petitioning
A petition is a precise prayer, such as "My friend is dying, please heal him", not a spell from a list. There is no fixed list of miracles. The library's examples show what each result may bring.

Petitioning skill is Fair with favor and Poor without (use **Without favor**). It can't be raised. Click Petition. The roll asks for modifiers:
- **+1** for: strict obedience to the deity, a petition that furthers the deity's aims or lies in their sphere, or a simple but vital request after the petitioner has tried everything else.
- **-1 or more** for: behaviour against the deity's wishes, a petition against their aims or using an element hostile to them, too vague a request, a trivial one, a petitioner who hasn't tried to help themselves, or praying too often.

If **Deity angry** is ticked, the -1 for a last petition of Poor or worse is added on its own. Untick it after the next petition.

## Results
- **Good or better:** granted. A better result brings a stronger miracle: a Good result heals one wound level, and Superb heals every wound.
- **Fair or Mediocre:** no answer, but no harm.
- **Poor or worse:** the deity is angry, so tick Deity angry. An evil deity may answer with something the petitioner won't enjoy.

Each step of favor gives one more try in the same petition: **Pray again** rolls once more. The petitioner may stop at any time, and the last roll counts, even if it is worse.
