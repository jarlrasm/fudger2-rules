# Fudge Supers and Cybernetics

Based on the Fudge SRD sections on superpowers (2.8), cybernetic enhancements (2.9) and Scale (2.3 and 4.58).

## Powers
Add powers from the library or write your own. Each has a **Level** on the ladder (Fair by default), rolled under Power rolls to use it, for example to hit with an energy blast or to fly through a storm. A **Trait** and **Bonus** raise an attribute or skill; Trait bonuses lists them, so add them when rolling that trait.

How many powers a character gets depends on the campaign. Each costs one supernormal power (two gifts). Potent ones cost two or more. A fault tied to a power, such as "flies only while intangible", can lower its cost to one gift. Note it in Limits.

## Implants
Artificial limbs, organs and neural links. An implant that only gives an attribute bonus costs as much as that bonus. One that goes beyond human limits costs a supernormal power. Implants sometimes fail, which can earn a free skill level; note how in Failure, and use it when the story wants trouble.

## Scale
Humans are Scale 0. Each step of **Strength Scale** is about 1.5 times as strong, and each step of **Mass Scale** soaks wounds like armour. Mass usually equals Strength. Examples: a leprechaun might be -4, an ogre 3 and a dragon 9. **Speed Scale** steps are about 1.2 times as fast.

In combat, add the attacker's Strength Scale to the offensive factors and the defender's Mass Scale to the defensive factors. Weapons and armour are scaled to their users. A tiny attacker can rarely hurt a giant: allow a point of damage on a critical, or a damage roll when Scale alone stops a Scratch. Scale-piercing weapons such as harpoons halve or ignore the target's Scale.

In superhero games, one supernormal power can buy a block of Scale, such as Scale 4 for one power. Strength is then raised or lowered as normal from there.
