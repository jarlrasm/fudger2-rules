# Playing Werewolf: the Apocalypse 5th edition

## The game
The players are **Garou**, werewolves who fight for a world that is already dying: against the corporations and corruption of the Wyrm, against their own Rage, and against despair. Each Garou belongs to a **tribe** and was born under an **auspice** (moon phase) that gives them a role. Play is about the pack, the spirits and the cost of violence.

## Rolling
Build a pool of d10s from **attribute + skill**. Each 6 or more is a success; each **pair of 10s** adds two more (a critical). The GM sets a difficulty (successes needed); leave it blank when only the GM knows it.

- **Rage dice**: as many dice of the pool as the character's Rage are red Rage dice. If **two or more Rage dice show 1 or 2**, the result is **brutal**: a violent action succeeds with four extra successes, but anything else fails.
- **Willpower**: after a roll, reroll up to three normal (not Rage) dice for 1 superficial Willpower damage. The sheet marks it.
- A **Rage check** is a d10: on 1 to 5 the Garou loses 1 Rage. Make one when shifting or using many gifts. At 0 Rage the Garou can't shift; at 5 they risk frenzy.

## Forms
- **Homid** (human) and **Lupus** (wolf) are natural forms.
- **Glabro** and **Hispo** add two dice to physical pools.
- **Crinos**, the war form, adds four dice to physical pools and extra health, and its claws deal aggravated damage. It also brings the Delirium on mortals who see it, and frenzy is close.

## Damage
Health boxes are Stamina + 3 (more in war forms); Willpower boxes are Composure + Resolve. Damage is **superficial** (halved) or **aggravated** (silver, claws, fire). When the track fills with damage the character is impaired, and aggravated damage past it can kill. Garou regenerate superficial damage quickly in shifted forms.

## Harano and Hauglosk
Two trackers of spiritual decline: **Harano** is despair at the world's death, **Hauglosk** is fanatic zeal. Both can end a Garou's story at 5.

## Gifts and renown
Gifts are spirit powers learned from the tribe, the auspice or as native gifts. **Renown** (Glory, Honor, Wisdom) earns rank and access to more gifts.
