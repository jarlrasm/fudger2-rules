# Playing Chronicles of Darkness (2nd edition)

## The game
The modern world, but darker: something moves in the shadows, and most people never see it. The characters are ordinary mortals who have glimpsed the supernatural (ghosts, monsters, conspiracies) and must decide what to do about it. Play is personal horror and investigation; violence is fast and dangerous.

## Dice pools
Roll a pool of d10s, usually **attribute + skill**, plus or minus modifiers.
- Each die showing **8, 9 or 10** is a success. **10s roll again** (10-again); some rules give 9-again or 8-again.
- One success is enough for most actions. **Five or more** is an **exceptional success**.
- An **unskilled** roll takes -1 (Physical and Social skills) or -3 (Mental skills).
- If penalties bring the pool to 0 or less, roll a single **chance die**: only a 10 succeeds, and a 1 is a **dramatic failure**.
- **Willpower**: spend a point for +3 dice on a roll (or +2 Defense).
- Every roll first asks for a **dice bonus or penalty**: equipment, Willpower (+3) and the GM's modifiers, added up. A melee or unarmed attack also asks for the **target's Defense**, which comes off the pool. If the pool drops to 0 or less, it rolls a chance die.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: Dexterity + Composure + d10, highest first.
- **Melee**: Strength + Brawl (unarmed) or Weaponry, minus the target's **Defense** (lower of Wits and Dexterity, + Athletics).
- **Ranged**: Dexterity + Firearms; Defense doesn't apply unless the target is close or takes cover.
- **Damage** = successes + the weapon's damage. Armor takes some of it.

## Health
**Health** = Size + Stamina. Damage is **bashing** (fists, falls), **lethal** (blades, bullets) or **aggravated** (fire, the supernatural). When the last three boxes fill, rolls take -1, -2 and -3. A full track of bashing knocks you out; of lethal, you bleed out; of aggravated, you die.

Bashing heals in minutes, lethal in days, aggravated in weeks.

## Willpower, Integrity and Beats
- **Willpower** = Resolve + Composure. Regain all of it by acting on your Virtue at a cost, one by indulging your Vice, and one each night's sleep.
- **Integrity** measures how well the character holds together; **breaking points** (horrors, violence) can lower it and bring conditions.
- **Conditions** (Shaken, Frightened, Guilty...) change rolls until resolved; resolving one earns a **Beat**. Five Beats make an Experience.
