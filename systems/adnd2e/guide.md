# AD&D 2nd edition

## Attacks
Each character has a THAC0, the number needed on d20 to hit Armor Class 0. Armor Class is descending: 10 is unarmored, plate and shield is 2, and lower is better. To hit, roll d20 plus adjustments (Strength for melee, Dexterity's missile adjustment for missiles, magic bonuses) and compare with THAC0 minus the target's AC. A natural 20 always hits and a natural 1 always misses. The sheet asks for the target's AC; left empty, it says the lowest AC the roll hits.

THAC0 follows the class group: warriors improve by 1 a level, priests by 2 every 3 levels, rogues by 1 every 2 levels, wizards by 1 every 3 levels. Monsters' THAC0 follows their hit dice.

## Initiative
Each side (or each combatant) rolls d10 every round and adds weapon speed factors, spell casting times or other adjustments. The lowest goes first. Spellcasters who are hit before their spell goes off lose it.

## Saving throws
Five categories: Paralyzation/Poison/Death Magic, Rod/Staff/Wand, Petrification/Polymorph, Breath Weapon and Spell. Roll d20 at or over the number. Use the first category in that order that applies. Wisdom's magical defense adjustment helps against charms and illusions; Dexterity can help against some area effects.

## Ability checks and proficiencies
An ability check is d20 at or under the score. A nonweapon proficiency check is d20 at or under the ability score plus the proficiency's modifier. Thief abilities are percentile chances.

## Damage and healing
Weapons deal their dice (small/medium targets, or large ones) plus Strength's damage adjustment for melee and thrown weapons. At 0 hit points a character falls; at -10 they die (the optional rule most groups use). Rest heals 1 hit point a day, 3 a day with complete bed rest; curing spells heal faster. Spellcasters rest and study or pray to memorize spells again.

## Monsters
Stat blocks list AC, movement, hit dice, THAC0, number of attacks, damage per attack, special attacks and defenses, magic resistance, size, morale and XP. Morale is checked on 2d10 when things go badly; a roll above it means the creature flees or surrenders.

## Experience
Experience comes from defeating foes, finding treasure and doing what the class does best. A prime requisite of 16 or more gives 10% more.
