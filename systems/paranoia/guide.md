# Playing Paranoia (Perfect Edition)

## The game
The Computer is your friend. It runs a sealed underground city where everyone is happy, because unhappiness is treason. The players are **troubleshooters**: low-clearance clones sent to find and eliminate traitors, mutants and Commies, while secretly being mutants and members of secret societies themselves. Missions go wrong, teammates accuse each other, and clones die. It's a comedy: play it fast, play it unfair, and keep secrets.

## Citizens
Each citizen has a name like **Kim-R-BUD-1**: first name, clearance letter, home sector, clone number. Clearance runs Infrared, Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Indigo, Violet, Ultraviolet; anything above your clearance is treason to see. Every citizen has **six clones**; when one dies, the next arrives with the same memories and **Treason Stars**.

## NODE rolls
The **NODE** is **stat + skill** (Violence, Brains, Chutzpah, Mechanics; skills -5 to +5) plus equipment.
- Roll that many d6 and the red **Computer die**. Each **5 or 6** is a success; the Computer die counts a 5 as a success too.
- A **negative NODE**: roll that many dice and each die **under 5 takes a success away**.
- The GM sets a Difficulty (successes needed); the prompt asks for it and for equipment bonuses.
- A **C** on the Computer die means Friend Computer takes an interest: an announcement, an inspection, a malfunction or a reward, at the GM's whim.
- **Moxie** (0-8): spend it to reroll or to power a mutant power. At 0 the citizen **loses it** and acts on their worst instinct.

## Treason and XP
Accusations, overheard opinions and failed orders earn **Treason Stars**. At five, Internal Security terminates the citizen on sight. **XP points** come from pleasing the Computer and buy gear, clearance and replacement clones.

## Combat
No initiative: the GM decides, usually whoever speaks first. Attack with Violence + Guns, Melee or Throw. A hit wounds by the weapon's damage: **Hurt**, **Injured** (-1 NODE), **Maimed** (-2 NODE), **Dead**. A dead clone is replaced (Next clone on the sheet). Armor and cover raise the Difficulty.

## Secrets
Secret society, mutant power and secret orders stay in the editor view and private notes; never say them aloud unless it's funny.
