# Playing Warhammer Fantasy Roleplay 2nd edition

## The game
The Old World, mostly the Empire, in the years after the Storm of Chaos. The characters are ordinary people (rat catchers, soldiers, tollkeepers, apprentice wizards) pulled into danger. Life is short, wounds are lasting, and Chaos corrupts quietly. Play it grim but with gallows humour, and let the characters climb from humble careers to better ones.

## Characters
- **Race**: Human, Dwarf, Elf or Halfling. Each main characteristic starts at 2d10 plus the race's base (20 for a Human, more or less by race). Starting Wounds, Fate Points and Movement come from the race too (Movement: Human 4, Dwarf 3, Elf 5, Halfling 4).
- **Main profile**: WS (Weapon Skill), BS (Ballistic Skill), S (Strength), T (Toughness), Ag (Agility), Int (Intelligence), WP (Will Power), Fel (Fellowship), all percentages.
- **Secondary profile**: A (Attacks), W (Wounds), SB and TB (the tens digit of S and T), M (Movement), Mag (Magic), IP (Insanity Points), FP (Fate Points).
- **Careers**: each has an **advance scheme**, how far each characteristic may grow while in it (+5% steps in the main profile, +1 in the secondary). The sheet's Advance box holds the scheme, Taken what has been bought. Every advance, skill or talent costs 100 experience points. Changing career costs 100 XP through a listed exit, or 200 otherwise.

## Tests
Roll **d100** at or under the target: the characteristic or skill after the difficulty.

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Very Easy | +30 |
| Easy | +20 |
| Routine | +10 |
| Average | +0 |
| Challenging | -10 |
| Hard | -20 |
| Very Hard | -30 |

Every full 10 under the target is a **degree of success** (and every full 10 over a degree of failure, when it matters). In an opposed test the success with more degrees wins; on a tie, the lower roll.

**Skills**: a basic skill the character lacks is tested at half the characteristic. An advanced skill can't be used untrained (the sheet shows 0). Taking a skill once gives the full characteristic; taking it again +10%, a third time +20%. Talents can add more (the Bonus box).

**Fortune Points**: as many as the character's Fate Points, refilled each day (the **New day** button). Spend one to reroll a failed test (the roll window offers it), to parry or dodge an extra time, to roll 2d10 for initiative, or for an extra half action. **Fate Points** are spent for good to escape death.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: Agility + 1d10, highest first. A turn is two half actions or one full action. Move is twice M in yards, a charge four times, a run six times; a square is 2 yards.
- **Attacks**: test WS in melee, BS at range. Reverse the digits of a hit for the location: 01-15 head, 16-35 right arm, 36-55 left arm, 56-80 body, 81-90 right leg, 91-00 left leg. The attack roll names it.
- **Defence**: once a round, a hit can be parried (a WS test, with a weapon ready) or dodged (Dodge Blow, melee only). A Fortune Point buys one more.
- **Damage**: 1d10 plus the weapon's damage (most melee weapons deal the Strength Bonus, give or take one). The target takes off its Toughness Bonus and the armour points on that location; the damage roll asks for both. Unarmed blows deal SB-4 and armour counts double. Impact weapons roll two d10 and keep the higher; Armour Piercing ignores 1 AP.
- **Ulric's Fury**: a 10 on the damage die lets the attacker test the attack again (same target); a hit adds another 1d10, and a further 10 goes on. The damage roll's button does it.
- **Critical hits**: damage past 0 Wounds is the **Critical Value** (+1 to +10). Roll Critical hit with that value and the location: d100 on the critical table gives a Critical Effect from 1 to 10, worst at low rolls and high values. 1-5 are setbacks (dropped weapons, lost actions, damaged armour, stunning); 6-8 maim and bleed (20% chance of death each round until treated, and a Toughness test after the fight to keep the limb); 9-10 kill. Each critical hit also gives 1 Insanity Point. With the **Sudden Death** option, a 6 or more, or any Critical Value of +5 or more, kills.
- **Down**: at 0 Wounds every hit is a critical hit. The sheet marks Heavily wounded at 3 Wounds or fewer.

## Healing
A **lightly wounded** character (more than 3 Wounds left) heals 1 Wound a day (**New day**). A **heavily wounded** one (3 or fewer) heals 1 a week (**Week of rest**). A successful Heal test helps more: roughly 1d10 Wounds for the lightly wounded, 1 for the heavily wounded, once per injury. Maimed limbs and bleeding need medical attention.

## Fear, terror and insanity
Fear and Terror are Will Power tests (the sheet's Fear test and Terror test). A failed Fear test freezes the character until a later test succeeds; a failed Terror test also gives 1 Insanity Point and sends them cowering or fleeing. The GM can ask a Will Power test after other horrors, with Insanity Points at stake. Once a character has 6 or more Insanity Points, they test Will Power (Insanity test): a failure gives an insanity and takes 6 points away; a success holds until more points come.

## Magic
A caster rolls up to **Mag** d10 (the casting roll asks how many) and adds bonuses: a successful Channelling test (a half action), the spell's ingredient. Reaching the **Casting Number** casts the spell. Arcane casters take off armour penalties (leather -1, mail -3, plate or a helmet -5, a shield -1). All ones always fail, with a Will Power test or 1 Insanity Point.
- **Tzeentch's Curse** (arcane casters): doubles on the casting dice bring a minor Chaos manifestation, triples a major one, four of a kind a catastrophic one; each set counts. The spell still works if the total was reached. The GM rolls on the book's manifestation tables.
- **Divine casters** (priests) don't suffer the Curse; doubles or triples instead risk the Wrath of the Gods.

## The sheet
Main and Secondary Profile at the top, then Status (Wounds, Fortune Points, Initiative, Critical hit, New day and Week of rest), Conditions, skills, Attacks (each weapon's test and damage, with Ulric's Fury), Fear and Insanity, Magic with a casting roll per spell, and the career path. Creatures have a bestiary profile, Wounds, Initiative, a critical roll and their attacks, each with a test, a damage bonus and its qualities.
