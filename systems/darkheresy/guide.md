# Playing Dark Heresy 2nd edition

## The game
The grim far future of Warhammer 40,000. The players are **acolytes** of an Inquisitor, hunting heretics, mutants, cults and daemons across a decaying Imperium. Investigation matters as much as combat, and every victory costs something: wounds, fate, corruption or sanity.

## Tests
Roll **d100** and get the **target** or less. The target is a characteristic (or a skill: characteristic + rank) plus the difficulty:

| Difficulty | Modifier |
|---|---|
| Trivial | +60 |
| Easy | +30 |
| Routine | +20 |
| Ordinary | +10 |
| Challenging | +0 |
| Difficult | -10 |
| Hard | -20 |
| Very Hard | -30 |
| Hellish | -60 |

**Degrees of success**: 1 plus the target's tens digit minus the roll's tens digit. Degrees of failure count the other way. 01 always succeeds and 100 always fails. Skill ranks: untrained -20, Known +0, Trained +10, Experienced +20, Veteran +30. Each level of **fatigue** gives -10 to every test.

A characteristic's **bonus** is its tens digit; Strength bonus adds to melee damage and Toughness bonus soaks damage.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: 1d10 + Agility bonus, highest first. Each turn has a full action or two half actions.
- **Attacks**: Weapon Skill for melee, Ballistic Skill for shooting. The **hit location** is the attack roll's digits reversed (34 hits location 43): 1-10 head, 11-20 right arm, 21-30 left arm, 31-70 body, 71-85 right leg, 86-100 left leg. Semi-auto bursts score an extra hit per two degrees, full auto one per degree.
- **Damage**: roll the weapon's damage (add Strength bonus in melee), subtract the location's armour (less the weapon's penetration) and the Toughness bonus. What is left comes off **Wounds**. A natural 10 on a damage die is **Righteous Fury**: confirm with another attack test, then deal critical damage.
- Damage past 0 wounds is **critical damage**, read on the critical tables by location and damage type.
- Targets may **Dodge** (Agility) or **Parry** (Weapon Skill) once per turn.

## Fate, corruption and insanity
- **Fate points** return each session. Spend one to reroll a test, add a degree of success, heal 1d5 wounds or act first; burn one permanently to survive certain death.
- **Corruption** comes from the Warp and the daemonic; it brings malignancies and mutations.
- **Insanity** comes from horror; it brings trauma and disorders.

## Psykers
A psyker rolls a **Focus Power** test (usually Willpower) using a psy rating. Pushing the power or rolling **doubles** while unfettered causes **Psychic Phenomena**, which can lead to Perils of the Warp.

## Healing
Rest removes fatigue. Wounds heal slowly, about 1 a day without medicae; Medicae tests speed this.
