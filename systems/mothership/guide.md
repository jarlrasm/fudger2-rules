# Playing Mothership

## Rolling
Roll 1d100 and try to roll **under** the stat or save. A trained skill adds its bonus (+10, +15 or +20) to the target. Doubles are criticals: a critical success under the target, a critical failure over it. A failure usually gives 1 Stress.

- **Stats:** Strength, Speed, Intellect, Combat.
- **Saves:** Sanity, Fear, Body.
- **Advantage** rolls twice and keeps the better, **disadvantage** the worse.

## Stress and panic
Stress rises when things go wrong. When a character's nerve breaks, they make a Panic check: roll 1d20. Rolling over their Stress is fine. Rolling at or under it means a panic effect, worse the higher the roll.

## Combat
1. Attacks roll Combat (plus a weapon skill). A hit deals the weapon's damage to Health.
2. When Health reaches 0, the character takes a Wound, Health resets, and a wound effect follows. At maximum Wounds the character dies.
3. Armour reduces damage.

## Movement
The `Speed` stat is rolled to act quickly or win a chase. It is not a distance. In a round a character can move a short distance (about 10 metres, across a room or down a corridor) and still act, or run about twice that and do nothing else. Use Speed checks to see who gets to a door, an airlock or a weapon first.

## Style
This is horror. Keep the players scared, the ship fragile and the monster mostly unseen. Fighting should feel like a last resort.
