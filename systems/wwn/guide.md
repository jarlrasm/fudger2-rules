# Playing Worlds Without Number

## Skill checks
Call for a check only when failure is interesting. The player rolls **2d6 + skill level + attribute modifier** against a difficulty: 6 easy, 8 typical, 10 hard, 12 very hard, 14 nearly impossible. Situational modifiers stay within −2 to +2. An untrained skill is −1. Each skill row carries the attribute it usually uses; switch it when another fits better. The sheet asks for the difficulty; left empty it shows only the total. A full **Expert** may reroll one failed non-combat check per scene (Masterful Expertise): the roll offers the button and ticks it off in the Scene box. Opposed checks: higher total wins, PCs win ties. An NPC rolls 2d6 + its Skill bonus.

## Combat
- **Initiative:** each side rolls **1d8 + best DEX modifier**; PCs win ties. Individual initiative (d8 + own DEX) is an option.
- **Attack:** **d20 + attack bonus + combat skill (Stab, Shoot or Punch) + attribute modifier**, equal to or over the target's **AC** hits. No skill at all is −2. The roll asks for the target's AC. Long range is −2; one-handed and thrown attacks in melee are −4, bows can't be shot in melee.
- **Damage** is the weapon die plus the attribute modifier; a full **Warrior** adds half their level, rounded up (Killing Blow), to damage and Shock.
- **Shock:** many melee weapons list Shock such as 2/AC 15. On a miss against a target with AC at or under that number, it still takes the Shock (plus the attribute modifier). "−" means any AC. A shield ignores the first Shock each round; Total Defense ignores it all. A hit never does less than the Shock would have.
- **Veteran's Luck:** once per scene a full Warrior turns their own miss into a hit (the attack roll offers it) or an enemy's hit on them into a miss.
- The **Attacks** list builds a row for every weapon on the sheet, plus Unarmed (1d2 + Punch skill) and a thrown row for throwable weapons. Reach is 5 ft, 10 ft for Long weapons; ranges are the long range in feet.
- Trauma Dice are not used: the SRD keeps them as an option whose numbers live in another book.

## Damage, healing and strain
- At **0 HP** from a lethal attack a PC is **Mortally Wounded** and dies at the end of the sixth round unless stabilised: Dex/Heal or Int/Heal against 8 + rounds since falling (+2 without a healer's kit). They wake after ten minutes with 1 HP and **Frail**: dropping to 0 again while Frail kills. Frail heals with a week of bed rest and care. Unnamed NPCs usually just die at 0.
- **Night of Rest** (warm, fed, comfortable): HP back equal to level (none while Frail), −1 System Strain, all committed Effort and spell castings back.
- **First aid** with a healer's kit heals 1d6 + Heal skill and adds 1 **System Strain**. Magical healing adds strain too. Strain can't pass CON; anything that would push it over simply doesn't work.

## Saving throws
Roll **d20** equal to or over the save. A natural 1 always fails, a natural 20 always succeeds. **Physical**, **Evasion** and **Mental** are 16 − level − the better of two attribute modifiers; **Luck** is 16 − level. NPCs have one save, 15 − half their HD.

## Classes, Effort and spells
- **Warrior**, **Expert** and **Mage** (with a tradition: High Mage, Elementalist, Necromancer, Invoker) are full classes. An **Adventurer** takes two partial classes, such as Partial Warrior/Healer or Partial Expert/Vowed. The sheet works out attack bonus, hit dice, Effort and spell slots from that choice.
- **Effort** fuels arts. Commit it for the scene (back at End of Scene), for the day (back after a Night of Rest), or for as long as the power lasts. Each partial class has its own pool.
- Spellcasters prepare spells after a night's rest and cast up to Spells per Day; mark them in Cast Today. They can't cast in armor (without Armored Magic) or in a round they took damage.

## Encumbrance
Readied items up to half STR, Stowed up to STR. Pulling out a Stowed item is a Main Action. The sheet adds up the encumbrance of listed items; the player splits it between Readied and Stowed.

## NPCs and monsters
Stat lines give HD, AC, attack bonus (x2 for two attacks), damage, Shock, Move, Morale, Instinct, Skill and Save. Check **Morale** (2d6 over ML: they break) when the first of them falls, when the fight turns, or at terrifying magic. Roll **Instinct** (d10 at or under it: something rash) when they are confused, goaded or panicked. Make a reaction roll (2d6, + CHA of whoever greets them) before swords come out.

## Factions
Each faction has Cunning, Force and Wealth (1–8), Magic, Treasure, HP and assets. Each faction turn: roll d8 for order, **Collect Income** (half Wealth plus a quarter of Force and Cunning, rounded up), pay upkeep, use asset abilities, take one action with all its assets (Attack, Move, Repair, Expand Influence, Create, Hide or Sell Asset), and check its goal. An attack rolls **d10 + attacker's attribute** against **d10 + defender's**; the defender wins ties. A success deals the asset's attack damage, a failure takes the defender's counterattack. Damage to a Base of Influence also comes off the faction's HP. The Assets library lists the SRD's assets by attribute and rating.

## Style
A sandbox: build a region of ruins, lairs, settlements and factions with their own goals, let the players choose where to go, and run a faction turn between adventures.
