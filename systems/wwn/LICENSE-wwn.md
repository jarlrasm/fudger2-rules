# Worlds Without Number library

`wwn.json` is made by `tools/wwn_import.py` from the *Worlds Without Number System Reference Document v1.0* by Kevin Crawford, Sine Nomine Publishing (dated 13 March 2024).

## Source

- The SRD is published by Sine Nomine as a free PDF on DriveThruRPG (product 473939). Its introduction waives copyright and related rights under CC0 1.0 Universal (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode) and names VTTs and online utilities among the uses it covers.
- The import reads `SRD.md`, a Markdown transcription of that SRD, from https://github.com/klrkdekira/wwn-system-json at commit edc62dfe9ddc651d7f5ef0f7a7dfeaf9ef6a60e8 (SHA-256 of the file: c336627066600040eced23076756f6171fd1258ae26cd995900cb637c7702f0b). That repository dedicates the SRD content to CC0 as well; its MIT-licensed code is not used.

## What was taken and changed

- Weapons, armor, adventuring gear, foci, High Magic, Elementalist and Necromancer spells, the arts of every tradition, faction assets, and the example NPC and monster stat lines.
- Text is kept as in the SRD, with typographic quotes and dashes made plain. Weapon trait codes are spelt out in each weapon's description in our own words. Creature hit points are the average of their hit dice.
- Nothing from the free or deluxe *Worlds Without Number* books outside the SRD is included, as the SRD's introduction asks.
