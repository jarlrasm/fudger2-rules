# Playing Godbound

The player characters are Godbound: mortals who have bound Words of Creation (Fire, Sword, Death, Sky...) and become demigods in a broken world whose heavens have fallen. Play big. Most things a Godbound does inside a Word need no roll; roll when the outcome is in doubt or someone resists.

## Attribute checks
Roll **d20** and reach **21 minus the attribute score** (the sheet's Check). A fact from the character's past that fits the task gives a bonus, which the roll asks for first. Mortals roll the same way.

## Saving throws
**Hardiness** (STR/CON), **Evasion** (DEX/INT) and **Spirit** (WIS/CHA): roll d20 at or over the target, which is 15 minus the better modifier minus one per level after the first. Armor can raise a target (Penalty). A Godbound who fails a save can commit one Effort for the day to succeed anyway; the roll offers that button and records the Effort.

## Effort
A Godbound has **level + 1 Effort** (plus Effort of the Word). Gifts and miracles commit it for the scene or for the day; it returns when that ends. Use **End of scene** and **New day** on the sheet. A miracle (doing any plausible thing within a Word) commits Effort for the day.

## Combat
- **Initiative:** 1d8 + DEX modifier, highest first.
- **Attack:** d20 + level (at most +10) + attribute modifier + the target's AC; **20 or more hits**. AC is descending: 9 unarmored, 3 heavy armor. The sheet asks for the target's AC. A natural 20 hits and a natural 1 misses.
- **Damage chart:** read each damage die on its own: **1 or less = 0, 2-5 = 1, 6-9 = 2, 10+ = 4**. A flat bonus goes on the highest die only. Damage rolls show the chart result and the straight total.
- **Straight damage:** some powers, bosses and area effects deal the dice as rolled. Creatures with the Straight damage box ticked roll that way.
- **Fray die:** every round, for free, a Godbound deals its Fray die (1d8) on the chart to one **lesser foe** in sight: a creature with no more hit dice than the Godbound's level, or a mob. Worthy foes and other Godbound ignore it.
- **Divine Wrath:** commit Effort to strike one foe in sight with level d8s on the chart. **Corona of Fury:** half that (rounded up) on every foe within 30 feet of a point.
- Damage removes **hit dice** from NPCs; an HD 2 creature dies after 2 damage. Overkill can cleave into another foe in reach.
- At 0 HP a Godbound is out and regains 1 HP after an hour, dying only if struck again first. **Divine Fury** (once per level) lets them keep fighting.
- A night's rest restores all hit points and Effort (**New day**).

## Mobs and armies
A mob is one creature on the sheet with many hit dice (small 10 + twice the member's HD, large double, vast triple), one save, and several attacks. Lesser-foe checks use one member's HD. Area powers hurt mobs with straight damage.

## Influence and Dominion
**Influence** (level + 1) keeps a change in place while the Godbound tends it. **Dominion** is earned from deeds and worship (about 2 a session) and spent on lasting changes: scope village 1, city 2, region 4, nation 8, realm 16, times 1, 2 or 4 as the change is plausible, implausible or impossible. Track them in Projects.

## Factions and pantheons
Make **Factions** for nations, churches and cults: Power 1-5 sets the Action Die (d6 to d20), Cohesion up to Power, Trouble from Problems, Features that fit their actions (otherwise roll twice, take the lower). A **Pantheon** groups the player characters, their realm and pooled works.

## Creatures
NPCs have HD, AC, a single Save, an attack bonus, damage, Morale (2d6 over it breaks them) and often Effort to shrug off a Godbound's powers. A good boss has 10 + twice the party's total levels in HD.
