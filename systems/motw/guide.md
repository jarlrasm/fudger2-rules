# Playing Monster of the Week

## Moves
Hunters describe what they do. When that matches a **move**, they roll **2d6 + the rating** it names (Charm, Cool, Sharp, Tough or Weird):

- **10+** success: they do it.
- **7–9** partial: they do it, but with a cost, a complication or a hard choice.
- **6−** miss: they mark experience, and the Keeper makes a move.
- **12+** with an advanced (improved) basic move gives an extra benefit.

Forward on the sheet adds to the next roll; clear it after use. The basic moves are on the sheet: Act Under Pressure and Help Out (Cool), Investigate a Mystery and Read a Bad Situation (Sharp), Kick Some Ass and Protect Someone (Tough), Manipulate Someone (Charm) and Use Magic (Weird).

## The mystery
Each session the Keeper prepares a monster, its minions, bystanders and locations, and a **countdown** of what happens if the hunters do nothing. Every monster has a **weakness**; the hunters must investigate to find it, and usually need it to kill the monster.

## Fighting
There is no initiative: move the spotlight. **Kick Some Ass** (Tough) is an exchange of harm: on a hit both sides deal harm, on a 10+ the hunter picks an extra effect.

- Harm is the attack's harm minus armour. A hunter's track runs to 8: at 4 or more they are **unstable** and get worse unless treated; at 8 they die.
- Monsters have a harm capacity; when their harm reaches it, they die (if the weakness was used, where needed).

## Luck
Each hunter has 7 Luck. Spend one to turn a roll into a 12 or to make an injury count as 0 harm. When Luck runs out the hunter is **doomed**.

## Style
Be a fan of the hunters, make the monster frightening, and keep the mystery moving: every clue should point somewhere.
