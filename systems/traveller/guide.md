# Playing Traveller

## Rolling
A check is **2d6 + characteristic DM + skill level** against a target of 8 (Average). The sheet works the DM out from each characteristic (6–8 is +0, 9–11 is +1, 3–5 is −1 and so on). An untrained skill is −3, unless the traveller has Jack of all Trades.

- Set **Difficulty** to change the target: Simple 2, Easy 4, Routine 6, Average 8, Difficult 10, Very Difficult 12, Formidable 14.
- **Effect** is the total minus the target. Effect 6 or more is exceptional; it also scales damage and how well something went.
- **Boon** rolls 3d6 and keeps the best two, **Bane** keeps the worst two (Shift and Ctrl).
- Each skill row has a Characteristic picker: the referee says which characteristic suits the task (Pilot with DEX to dodge, with INT to plot a course).

## Combat
Roll **Initiative** (2d6 + DEX DM) to join the tracker. A round is 6 seconds: one significant action and one minor action (moving 6 m).

1. Attack with Gun Combat (+DEX) or Melee (+STR) against 8. Add the attack's Effect to the weapon's damage.
2. Armour subtracts from damage. The first hit comes off **END**; once END is 0, damage comes off STR or DEX (the player's choice). Two characteristics at 0 means unconscious, all three means dead.
3. Medic checks and rest restore lost characteristics.

## Ships and trade
Starships are their own entity type with Hull, Structure, Thrust and Jump. Jumps take a week. Travellers pay their way with freight, passengers and speculative trade, and the ship's mortgage is always due.

## Style
The universe is big and indifferent. Characters are experienced people who arrive with skills, contacts and debts; let them make plans and pay for them.
