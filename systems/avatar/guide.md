# Playing Avatar Legends

## The game
Young heroes in the world of Avatar: the Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra, in one of five eras. They fight for a cause together and struggle with who they want to be. It is Powered by the Apocalypse: the fiction comes first, and rolls happen when a move is triggered.

## Moves
Roll **2d6 + a stat** (Creativity, Focus, Harmony or Passion, from -1 to +2):
- **10+** strong hit: you do it.
- **7-9** weak hit: you do it, with a cost or a choice.
- **6-** miss: the GM makes a move.

Basic moves: Assess a Situation (Creativity), Guide and Comfort (Harmony), Intimidate an NPC (Passion), Plead with an NPC (Harmony), Push Your Luck (Passion), Rely on Your Skills and Training (Focus), Trick an NPC (Creativity), and Call Someone Out (the principle you shift toward). Helping costs a fatigue to give +1.

## Balance
Each playbook has two **principles** (such as Loyalty and Confidence) at the ends of a **balance track**. Acting on a principle, being called out or guided shifts the balance. You can **live up to a principle** and roll with it. A principle is rated by how far balance leans toward it, so the one on the far side is negative. Shifted past the end of the track you **lose your balance**: the GM says what you do, and you change. After a fight balance returns toward your **center**.

## Fatigue and conditions
- **Fatigue** (5 boxes) pays for pushing yourself and for some techniques. When it is full, mark a condition instead.
- **Conditions** (Afraid, Angry, Guilty, Insecure, Troubled) each give -2 to two moves. Clear one by doing what it calls for (run away, break something, make a sacrifice, act rashly, seek guidance) or with comfort and rest.
- With all five conditions marked and full fatigue, the next condition you would mark takes you **out** (tick Taken out).

## Fights
Fights go in **exchanges**. Everyone secretly picks an **approach**:
- **Defend and Maneuver** (Focus), resolved first,
- **Advance and Attack** (Passion), then
- **Evade and Observe** (Creativity), last.

Roll the approach: 10+ use two techniques, 7-9 one, 6- one and then mark fatigue or a condition. **Techniques** come from your training (bending, weapons, technology), your playbook and the universal list. They inflict fatigue, conditions, shifts in balance or positioning on NPCs.

## NPCs
Minor NPCs have 3 fatigue and 2 conditions, major ones 5 and 3, legendary ones 10 and 5, and groups 6 and 3 (the sheet sets them by kind); major and legendary ones have principles and balance too. An NPC is out of the fight when it runs out of fatigue and conditions, or gives up.

## Growth
At the end of each session, answer your playbook's **growth question** and the shared ones; each yes marks growth. Full growth gives an advancement.
