# Playing Shadowrun (6th World)

## Rolling
Roll a pool of **d6s**, usually skill rank + linked attribute (an untrained skill uses the attribute − 1; each skill row has an attribute picker). Every **5 or 6 is a hit**. Each test asks for its threshold (0 for none); set Modifier on the sheet for situational dice.

- **Glitch:** more than half the dice show 1. A glitch with no hits is a **critical glitch**.
- **Opposed tests:** both roll; the attacker needs more hits than the defender, and net hits add to damage.
- Wound modifiers (−1 dice per 3 boxes filled on each monitor) are taken off every pool automatically.

## Edge
Edge points (up to 7) are gained during play, for example from a better Attack Rating than the target's Defense Rating.

- **Reroll misses:** the roll window offers *Reroll misses (Edge)* after a roll, and takes the Edge point off the sheet.
- **Push the Limit:** set the Edge slider (Shift) before rolling to add Edge to the pool and make 6s explode.
- Other Edge actions (+1 to a die, buying a hit) are adjusted by hand.

## Combat
Roll **Initiative** (Reaction + Intuition + initiative dice) to join the tracker. Each turn has one major and one minor action (more minor actions with more initiative dice).

1. Attack: weapon skill + Agility, against the defender's Reaction + Intuition. Compare Attack Rating to Defense Rating for Edge.
2. Damage = weapon damage + net hits. The defender resists with Body (+ armor where it applies). Each unresisted point fills a box on the Physical (P) or Stun (S) monitor.
3. A full Stun monitor knocks the character out; more stun overflows to Physical. A full Physical monitor means dying, and overflow beyond Body kills.

## Matrix and magic
Deckers hack with Cracking and Electronics; technomancers use Resonance; mages cast with Sorcery and resist **Drain**. Track Essence: it caps Magic and falls with every piece of cyberware.

## Style
Professional criminals doing dangerous jobs for people who would sell them out. Plan, run, get paid, and expect betrayal.
