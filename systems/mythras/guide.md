# Playing Mythras

## The game
Mythras is a d100 game of gritty adventure, usually fantasy (its own setting, ancient history or swords and sorcery). Characters are skilled people rather than heroes from birth; combat is fast, tactical and dangerous, and wounds land on hit locations.

## Skill rolls
Roll **d100 at or under** the skill.
- The GM sets a **difficulty grade**: Very Easy (double the skill), Easy (+half), Standard, Hard (two thirds), Formidable (half), Herculean (a tenth), Hopeless (fails). The roll offers them; leave it at Not set for Standard.
- A roll of a **tenth of the target or less** (rounded up) is a **critical**. **01-05 always succeed** and **96-00 always fail**. **99-00** is a **fumble** (only 00 once the skill is over 100).
- **Opposed rolls**: both roll; if both succeed, the higher roll wins.
- **Augment**: a related skill or Passion adds a fifth of its value.
- **Luck Points** (1-3, back each session): reroll, downgrade a serious wound, or act first. The roll window offers the reroll and takes the point off.

## Combat
- **Initiative**: d10 + initiative bonus (half of INT + DEX, minus armor penalty). Each character has **Action Points** (usually 2) per round, spent on attacks, parries, evades and other actions.
- **Attack** with a **combat style** (the sheet uses the style whose Weapons name the weapon, or the first style) and roll d20 for the **hit location**. The defender **parries** (weapon size decides how much is blocked) or **evades**.
- **Special effects**: the side with the better success level gets one or more (Bleed, Impale, Disarm, Trip, Choose Location, Stun Location...).
- **Damage**: weapon dice plus the **damage modifier** (from STR + SIZ, up to +2d12 at 91-100 and more beyond), minus the location's armor, off its hit points. At 0 HP the location suffers a **Serious Wound**; at minus its HP a **Major Wound** (limb maimed, or death for head, chest and abdomen).

## Fatigue and healing
Fatigue grows with exertion: Winded, Tired, Wearied and worse make skills harder, and from Wearied on cost Initiative (-2 per level) and Action Points (-1 per level). Each level takes longer to rest off; a night's rest brings Winded and Tired to Fresh and Wearied to Tired, and restores Magic Points. Wounds heal by the **Healing Rate** per day (minor), week (serious) or month (major).

## Magic
Five traditions: **Folk Magic** (small cantrips for Magic Points), **Animism** (spirits), **Mysticism** (inner powers), **Sorcery** (formulas shaped by skill) and **Theism** (miracles from gods). Each casts from Magic Points (POW) or devotion.
