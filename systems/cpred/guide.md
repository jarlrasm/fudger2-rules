# Playing Cyberpunk RED

## Rolling
A check is **STAT + Skill level + 1d10** against a Difficulty Value (DV). Each roll asks for the DV before rolling (leave it blank to just see the total, such as when only the GM knows it). The total must **beat** the DV; ties go to the defender.

- Typical DVs: 9 everyday, 13 professional, 15 heroic, 17 incredible, 21 legendary, 24 impossible.
- **Critical success:** a natural 10 adds the "Crit" die. **Critical failure:** a natural 1 subtracts it. The Crit die is ignored otherwise.
- **Luck** points can be spent one for one, after deciding to spend but before rolling, to add to a check.
- Opposed checks: both roll, and the higher total wins (the defender wins ties).

## Combat
Roll **Initiative** (REF + 1d10) to join the tracker; highest goes first. Each turn has a move action (MOVE × 2 m) and one action.

1. Ranged attacks: Weapon skill + REF + 1d10 against a DV from the weapon's range band, or against the target's Evasion if their REF is 8 or more. Melee attacks are opposed by the target's Evasion.
2. Damage dice from the weapon; subtract the SP of the armor where it hit (Head or Body), and the armor's SP drops by 1 each time it is penetrated. Headshots double the damage that gets through.
3. Below half HP a character is **Seriously Wounded** (−2 to actions). At 0 HP they are **Mortally Wounded** (−4 to actions) and make a **Death Save** (roll under BODY) every turn; each earlier save adds 1 to the roll. The sheet takes the wound penalty off skills and attacks, and adds **Death Saves made** to the Death Save.
4. Rolling two or more 6s on damage dice is a Critical Injury.

## Netrunning and cyberware
Netrunners use Interface (their role ability) to move through a NET architecture and run Programs. Cyberware costs **Humanity**; EMP drops by 1 for every 10 Humanity lost.

## Style
Style over substance, attitude is everything, and always take it to the edge. The Corps are bigger than you, so the fun is in getting away with it.
