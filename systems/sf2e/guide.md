# Playing Starfinder 2nd edition

Starfinder 2nd edition uses the same core rules as Pathfinder 2nd edition, so the PF2 rules for checks, degrees of success, three actions, conditions and dying apply unchanged.

## Rolling
**d20 + attribute modifier + proficiency bonus** (Trained 2, Expert 4, Master 6, Legendary 8, plus level) against a DC. Each check asks for the DC (Strikes ask for the target's AC) and shows the degree of success. Leave it blank when only the GM knows it, and the roll shows just the total. Beat the DC by 10 for a critical success; miss by 10 for a critical failure. A natural 20 or 1 moves the result one degree.

## What is different
- **Ranged combat** is common. Ranged weapons use **magazines**: track rounds in the Ammunition list and spend an action to reload. Many guns have area and automatic fire traits that ask targets for a basic Reflex save.
- **Computers** and **Piloting** are skills. Hacking, sensors and flying vehicles use them.
- **Augmentations** replace a body system (eyes, arms, brain…), one augmentation per system.
- **Starships** are their own entity type (Spaceships). In ship combat each crew member takes a role (pilot, gunner, engineer, captain, science officer) and acts with their own skills.
- Money is **credits**; UPBs are the universal raw material for crafting.

## Encounters
Roll **Initiative** (Perception) to join the tracker. Each turn has three actions. The multiple attack penalty is −5/−10 (agile −4/−8); set the Attack slider before rolling a second or third Strike.

## Style
Science-fantasy adventure: magic and technology side by side, strange worlds, starships, and a galaxy full of cultures to meet.
