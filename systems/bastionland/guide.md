# Playing Electric Bastionland

## The game
The players are Debtors in Bastion, an endless city of electric wonders, bureaucracy and danger, with the Deep Country beyond it and the Underground below. Each failed at a career and together they owe a crushing debt, usually 10,000 pounds, to the creditor named by the youngest player's Failed Career. They go after treasure, Oddities and work to pay it off; paying it off ends the game. There are no classes, skills or experience. The gamemaster is the Conductor: describe the world, make danger clear before it strikes, and let smart play beat dice.

## Making a Debtor
Roll 3d6 for STR, DEX and CHA in order (two may be swapped), a d6 for HP and a d6 pounds. The lowest and highest abilities (Career index on the sheet) find the Failed Career. It gives a starting kit, and its two tables give more by the Debtor's pounds and HP: fewer pounds and less HP mean stronger gifts. The career's questions colour the character.

## Saves
Most actions just happen. Call for a **save** only when there is a risk: **d20 equal to or under STR, DEX or CHA** (the Save column). A 1 always succeeds, a 20 always fails.
- STR: muscle, endurance, poison and sickness. DEX: speed, stealth, aim, acting first. CHA: persuasion, command, nerve, keeping your mind.

## Turns
When a fight starts, Call for initiative: each Debtor rolls a DEX save. Those who pass act before the enemy, then the enemy acts, then those who failed (the tracker sorts it that way). On a turn a character moves and does one thing.

## Attacks and damage
- **Attacks always hit.** Roll the weapon die and subtract the target's **Armour** (0 to 3). The roll asks how the attack is made and the target's Armour.
- **Impaired** (through cover, grappled, at a bad angle): d4. **Enhanced** (a helpless target, a daring move): d12. **Two weapons**: roll both, keep the best. Several attackers on one target: roll all, keep only the highest.
- **Blast** weapons hit everything in the area; roll for each target.
- Damage comes off **HP** first. HP is how well you avoid harm, not health.
- A hit that leaves the target at **exactly 0 HP** gives a **Scar**: the damage dealt is the row on the book's Scars table. The damage roll names the row. Write the Scar on the sheet; some change Max HP (the Max HP roll rerolls a d6 and keeps the higher).
- Damage past 0 HP comes off **STR**, and the target saves STR (with the new score) or takes **Critical Damage**: it can only crawl until an ally tends it and it rests, and dies within the hour if left untended. Monsters with Critical Damage are dead or out.
- STR 0 is death, DEX 0 paralysis, CHA 0 delirium.

## Rest
- **Short rest** (a few minutes and a drink): all HP back, and tended Critical Damage ends. **Full rest** (a week somewhere safe, or a paid specialist): ability scores back. A **deprived** character (no food, sleep, warmth or safety) gets nothing from rest. The sheet's buttons do both.

## Money and debt
Money is pounds (£), with shillings and pence for small things. The **Shared debt** on each sheet is the group's; keep it the same on every Debtor. The creditor will want payments and favours.

## Oddities
Strange devices and relics. Using one is an action; each does one odd thing, some have uses or a price. Keep them strange and valuable rather than useful.

## Enemies
Inhabitants are Humans, Mockeries (wooden imitations of people and beasts), Machines, Aliens and Monstrosities. They have HP, Armour, STR/DEX/CHA and an attack die. **Morale**: a CHA save when a group is clearly losing; fail and it flees or surrenders. **Detachments** are twenty or more fighting as one: their attacks on individuals are enhanced, individuals' attacks on them impaired unless Blast.

## Other rolls
A **luck roll** is a d6: high favours the Debtors. Falls and blasts deal d4 to d12.
